## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $123.39.** kill-thesis: **SOUND (on this pass)** — conditions=6
(4 probable, 2 plausible), refuted=0, unknown=0.

Coach is a healthy, cash-rich brand machine — FY26 revenue +17% pro forma
cc, 23.3% GAAP operating margin, 76.6% gross margin, ~$1.7B/yr returned to
holders — and nothing since the Aug 13 print has damaged that. But at
$123.39 the market still implies only **~9.9%** on a conservative owner-FCF
base ($1.7B guided FY27 adjusted FCF less $104.5M SBC, 5% growth,
inflation-only terminal) against a **10.4% (β floored to 1.2) to 11.6%
(regression β 1.46) cost of equity**. A mono-brand fashion house whose
second brand is shrinking high-single-digits inside the same corporate
machinery has to *clear* its hurdle on the conservative case, not brush it.
The conservative case clears the friendly hurdle at **~$116** (market cap
$23.15B, verified +0bp), unchanged from the prior run; the strict hurdle
near $100. Two weeks of drift closed a third of the gap and disclosed
nothing new.

**Closest attack:** the hurdle/growth stack, now nearer to landing than on
Aug 17 — three of five scenario cells are positive at the β-1.2 hurdle
(algorithm 7% +17bp, raw TTM base +51bp, 8% growth +50bp), so the pass
hangs on three defensible-but-contestable choices made together: the SBC
haircut, the operations-weighted country ERP, and refusing to let the
regression beta fall below the 0.8–1.2 band floor. Detail in the
Kill-thesis record.
