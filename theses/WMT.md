## 2. Business

**Created:** everyday-low-price omnichannel retail — the customer gets the
lowest basket price across groceries and general merchandise plus, now,
speed (sub-30-minute delivery in 38 US markets, fast delivery +48% in Q2)
and convenience across 4,600 US stores acting as last-mile nodes for 80% of
e-commerce orders. Consumer surplus is the strategy: 11,000+ active
rollbacks, price gaps to conventional grocers "strong and widening" (call).

**Captured:** five distinct mechanisms, not one. (a) Retail gross margin on
first-party goods (25.2% TTM); (b) marketplace take rates — US marketplace
+52%, ~50% flowing through Walmart Fulfillment Services fees; (c)
advertising — +38% globally, Walmart Connect +43% ex-VIZIO, near-pure
margin; (d) membership — fee revenue +17%, members spend ~4× non-members;
(e) data ventures (Scintilla). The mix is shifting profit toward (b)–(e),
which is why operating income can grow 7–8.5% on 4–5% sales.

**Protected:** a scale-and-density cost mechanism, not a label: purchasing
scale on $736B of revenue plus a store network within ~10 miles of most of
the US population that makes 30-minute grocery delivery cheap for Walmart
and capex-prohibitive to replicate; grocery share anchors trip frequency
that general-merchandise rivals cannot match. The disclosed counter-threat
(10-K Item 1A, first strategic risk) is the omnichannel/AI-agent shift
diverting search and store traffic — the moat is real but the company
itself names the mechanism that could thin it.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative over five years, positive in the
trailing twelve months.** Revenue FY22→FY26 $572.8B→$713.2B (+24.5%) vs
operating income $25.9B→$29.8B (+15.0%); operating margin 4.53%→4.18%. The
TTM turns it: revenue $735.8B, operating income $32.3B, margin 4.39% and
rising on e-commerce/advertising mix (all fiscal years end January;
stockanalysis financials route, TTM to 2026-07-31).
