## 2. Business

**Created:** an adult nicotine user gets a branded, consistent product in
the form they prefer — a Marlboro cigarette (11% of the international
category, a record), a heated-tobacco stick that delivers the ritual
without combustion (IQOS, "more than half of total nicotine offtake" in
Japan, 11.8% HTU share in Europe), a nicotine pouch (ZYN, 60 markets), or
a closed-pod vape (VEEV, #1 in Europe). The surplus for the switcher is
health-risk reduction the regulator now lets PMI say out loud: 20 ZYN SKUs
carry the only FDA modified-risk authorisation in the pouch category. For
the state, the surplus is excise — $27.8B collected on PMI's behalf in six
months against $21.3B of net revenue — which is also why governments are
ambivalent about the transition.

**Captured:** three distinct mechanisms. (1) Combustible pricing: cigarette
net revenue $12.1B in H1 with pricing +9.2% and volume −1.9%, gross margin
67.7%, in markets where smoke-free is banned or tiny (Indonesia, Turkey,
Egypt, India) and the legal-age cohort is still growing. (2) Smoke-free
unit economics: an IQOS consumable earns "two times more $ per stick"
than a cigarette at a higher gross margin; international smoke-free gross
margin reached 70% in H1 (+190bp) on $7.7B of revenue, and the device
(ILUMA, DELIA, BONDS) is the razor that makes the stick the blade.
(3) US oral: ZYN at a premium price point ("clear premium leader," ~57%
retail value share), with a Kentucky plant supplying "substantially all"
US capacity and a new Colorado line at full-scale production from July;
Q2 US segment revenue $856M, OCI $0.1B after the ramp costs. Royalties
from deconsolidated RBH in Canada are immaterial.

**Protected:** the moat is a regulatory-and-brand mechanism, not a label.
Each smoke-free category needs a market-by-market authorisation (PMTA/
MRTP in the US, framework laws elsewhere — Argentina only regulated
heat-not-burn in May 2026) that PMI has spent "over $16 billion" since
2008 obtaining, and IQOS's ~76% global heat-not-burn share means most of
the category's installed devices are PMI's. In cigarettes the protection
is brand plus distribution density plus the fact that advertising is
banned almost everywhere, which freezes share where it stands (PMI 25.3%
flat). What it does not protect against: a competitor with an
already-authorised product in the US pouch category (the "competitive
gaps" ZYN conceded for six quarters), a state that equalises HTU and
cigarette excise (Japan, April 2026) or bans flavours (EU, Poland/Hungary
in 2026), and illicit trade, which the 10-K names as the pouch category's
next problem.

**Control:** one class, one vote per share; 1,558,530,268 shares at the
2026-03-13 record date. Largest holders Vanguard 8.57%, Capital World
8.17%, Capital International 6.61%, BlackRock 6.22% (proxy, Schedule 13G
basis). No controlling holder; no dual class; the 2022 Performance
Incentive Plan defines change in control at 20%. Nothing in the structure
forecloses a takeover or activist path; the $284B size and the tobacco
regulatory perimeter do. Group CFO changed on 2026-08-01 (Andolina for
Babeau, Form 3 filed 2026-08-03) — a planned succession announced on the
Q2 call.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative 2021–2023, sharply positive since;
