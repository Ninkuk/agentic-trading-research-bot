## 2. Business

**Created:** for patients with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis, EVO301
offers a non-IL-4/IL-13 mechanism with infrequent dosing: in Phase 2a, two
IV doses (day 1, day 28) cut EASI 55% vs 22% on placebo at week 12, EASI-50
63% vs 23%, EASI-75 29% vs 9%, with no conjunctivitis — the class side
effect of dupilumab/lebrikizumab — and PK the company says supports
quarterly dosing (2026-10-01 8-K exh. 99.1; 2026-02-10 release). For
migraine, EVO756 is an oral MRGPRX2 antagonist being tested as prophylaxis
(Phase 2b initiated July 2026). Nothing is sold; the surplus is hypothetical
until a pivotal trial.

**Captured:** none today. TTM revenue $10.0M was Maruho license income
recognised in 2025; H1 2026 revenue is zero (Q2 2026 release). Future capture
would be (a) a branded biologic price if EVO301 reaches market, (b)
milestones/royalties from out-licensing (the Maruho precedent shows the
company licenses territories), or (c) an acquisition premium. All three are
contingent on Phase 2b/3 data that do not yet exist.

**Protected:** composition-of-matter patents on EVO301/EVO756 (assumed; not
read this run) and the clinical lead in IL-18 for AD — no other IL-18
program in AD surfaced in this run's search, though that is an absence of
hits, not a landscape review. The real protection question is the reverse:
AD is protected *against* EVO301 by entrenched dupilumab, lebrikizumab,
tralokinumab, oral JAKs, and incoming OX40 antibodies. No moat beyond IP.

**Control:** one share class; no controlling holder. Venture/crossover
holders are concentrated — EQT ~16.8%, RA Capital ~11.1%, Pivotal
bioVenture ~10.7%, Andera ~6.0% (aggregator, low-confidence; proxy not
read); insiders 2.5%, institutions 64.6% (`stocks.db`). No structure
forecloses a takeover or an activist; concentrated VC holders with
underwater PIPE stock at $27.88 make a cash-return push possible but
undated.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue went from license income
($3.0M in H1 2025; $10.0M TTM) to zero in H1 2026 while quarterly operating
expense rose from $23.0M (Q2 2025: R&D $19.6M + G&A $3.4M) to $34.9M (Q2
2026: R&D $26.4M + G&A $8.5M); TTM operating income −$101.9M, net income
−$94.7M (stockanalysis via `stocks.db`). No annual operating-income series
