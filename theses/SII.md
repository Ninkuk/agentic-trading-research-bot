## 2. Business

**Created:** one-ticket, physically-backed exposure to metals that are
otherwise hard to hold (gold/silver, and uniquely uranium, copper, rare
earths). The physical trusts offer metal redemption and audited bar lists —
trust the ETF wrapper cannot fake. Critical-materials funds gave investors
the only listed physical uranium vehicle of scale ($6.8B).

**Captured:** management fees on AUM — disclosed per-product net rates
0.31–0.57% on exchange-listed ($56.7B at ~0.40% blended), 0.80% on managed
equities ($6.3B), 0.85% plus carried interest on private strategies
($2.0B). Q1-2026: $81.5M management fees (2× YoY), $52M carry/performance
fees (vs $nil), $3M net commissions (uranium ATM activity). Blended net fee
rate 0.45% (Q1 press release, Schedule 1).

**Protected:** first-mover physical trusts with redemption features and
brand ("achieve AUM and liquidity milestones more quickly with each
launch" — CEO, Q1 release); the uranium trust's physical inventory is
itself a moat (accumulating deliverable pounds a copycat cannot source
quickly). On gold specifically the moat is thin — IAU charges 0.25% vs PHYS
0.35% and GLD is the liquidity default; Sprott wins on wrapper mechanics
and clientele, not price.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive through the cycle trough and
steeply positive in the metals bull.** (FY, stockanalysis financials from
SEDAR-sourced statements): revenue $164.6M → $145.2M → $151.4M → $178.7M →
$285.1M (FY21→FY25); operating income $56.1M → $38.5M → $66.4M → $82.8M →
$110.8M. TTM (Mar-2026): revenue $384.7M, operating income $142.1M.
