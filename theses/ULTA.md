## 2. Business

**Created:** one-stop beauty retail — prestige + mass + salon services
under one roof (a category-spanning assortment no competitor replicates:
department stores are prestige-only, drug/mass are mass-only, Sephora is
prestige-focused). 1,591 stores (1,505 US + 86 Space NK UK/Ireland), ~10,000
sq ft each with in-store salon. Customer gets breadth, trade-up/trade-down
freedom within one loyalty account, and services that pull visit frequency.
(10-K, filed 2026-03-26.)

**Captured:** retail gross margin (43.2% TTM incl. other revenue; ~40%
in-quarter) on owned inventory; salon services; a growing retail-media and
marketplace layer (200+ brands, 5,000 SKUs launched FY25); credit-card
royalty. Loyalty program: 47M active members (Q1 FY26 call), the CRM asset
that powers personalization and brand-partner co-marketing.

**Protected:** scale + loyalty data + brand-partner shelf relationships.
The mechanism is real but shallow: the 10-K's own words are "highly
competitive with few barriers to entry," and the top ten brand partners
(L'Oréal, Estée Lauder et al.) supplied ~51% of net sales in FY25 — the
brands own the product and can widen distribution (and have: Sephora-at-
Kohl's, Amazon Premium Beauty). ULTA's protection is being the largest,
most-convenient shelf with the best member data, not exclusivity.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative** (the load-bearing fact).
FY2022→TTM revenue $8.63B→$12.71B (+47%); operating income
$1.297B→$1.639B→$1.678B→$1.565B→$1.533B→$1.579B — flat since FY2023. Op
margin 16.1% (FY2023) → 12.4% (TTM). EPS growth ($17.98→$26.66) is buybacks
(shares −4.5%/yr), not operating leverage. (stockanalysis.com
income-statement history; live probe 2026-08-18.)
