## 2. Business

**Created:** the cheapest reliable national e-commerce parcel delivery in
China. ZTO owns the scale-sensitive middle mile: 92 sorting hubs (87
self-operated), >3,600 line-haul routes, >10,000 self-owned trucks and 782
automated sorter sets (Q2'26 6-K). ~6,000 direct network partners and
>31,000 outlets own last-mile economics with their own capital.

**Captured:** three mechanisms.
- Per-parcel transit + sorting fees charged to network partners (core
  express, ~94% of revenue).
- Direct key-account revenue, mostly e-commerce return parcels (+63.6% YoY
  in Q2). This is gross billing with pickup/dispatch costs passed through,
  so it inflates the headline ASP.
- Accessories (thermal waybill paper), plus small freight and financing
  lines.

**Protected:** a cost gap, not a pricing gap. Middle-mile fixed costs spread
over 19.9% of the nation's parcels give the lowest unit cost. The gap's
direction is observable (unit cost falling faster than peers' prices); its
width is not disclosed by anyone. The state currently referees pricing
(anti-involution). That favors the low-cost leader, but a policy is not an
equilibrium, and YTO's August cut shows the referee does not stop one
player's ASP from falling.

**Control:** founder-chairman Meisong Lai controls the vote through
weighted-voting-rights Class B shares (Q2'26 HKEX interim announcement,
carried from 08-19; exact voting % not re-pulled). This forecloses any
unsolicited takeover or activist path. Alibaba's group is now a 6.1%
economic holder (46,941,287 shares, 13D/A 2026-09-22) and was never in
control.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Q2'26 revenue +23.0% YoY
(RMB14,549.9M), operating income +30.4% (RMB3,227.9M), operating margin
20.9% → 22.2% (Q2'26 6-K). TTM (stockanalysis): revenue $7,980.2M, opinc
$1,680.2M (21.1%). Annual (RMB M, revenue/opinc): 2021 30,406/5,503 → 2022
35,377/7,737 → 2023 38,419/10,008 → 2024 44,281/11,777 → 2025 49,099/10,475.
Leverage was positive 2021–24, negative in 2025, and has been positive for
two quarters since. No new quarter since 08-19.
