## 2. Business

**Created:** a second-line option for advanced biliary tract cancer, where
paclitaxel alone gave a 5.3% response rate and 2.6-month median PFS;
tovecimig+paclitaxel gave 18.0% ORR and 4.7-month PFS (HR 0.44, p<0.0001)
(8-K 2026-09-22 exh. 99.1). Patients get tumor shrinkage and delayed
progression; the trial could not show they live longer (ITT OS HR 1.05).
Behind it: CTX-10726 (PD-1×VEGF-A, Phase 1), CTX-8371 (PD-1×PD-L1,
expansion cohorts, data Q4 2026), CTX-471 (Phase 2 initiating) (8-K
2026-08-06).

**Captured:** nothing today — revenue was $0.85M in FY2024 and nil since
(stockanalysis financials). Capture would come via (a) a US label for
tovecimig, now requiring a new survival trial, (b) a licensing/partnering
deal on any asset (none announced), or (c) eventual product sales. None is
a mechanism in operation.

**Protected:** composition-of-matter patents and clinical data are the only
barrier, and for a molecule without an approvable dataset that barrier
protects nothing commercial. In PD-1×VEGF the class is crowded by far
larger players (Summit/Akeso ivonescimab and big-pharma licensing deals —
low-confidence colour, not fetched this run). No moat in the business sense;
the domain science that would make CTX-10726 differentiated is UNKNOWN to
this author.

**Control:** one class of common stock (10-Q cover, 180,087,915 shares at
2026-07-28) plus 6.71M pre-funded warrants at $0.0001; insiders hold 5.86%,
institutions 78.4% (`stocks.db`). No controlling holder identified; the
June 2026 bylaw amendment (8-K Item 5.03, 2026-06-23) was not read, so any
anti-takeover provision there is unassessed. Nothing structural forecloses a
takeover or an activist on the face of what was read.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** No revenue in any year; the
operating loss widens every year — FY2021 −$31.3M, FY2022 −$41.7M, FY2023
−$50.4M, FY2024 −$56.6M, FY2025 −$72.8M, TTM −$81.1M (stockanalysis
financials). Costs grow with zero revenue to lever against.
