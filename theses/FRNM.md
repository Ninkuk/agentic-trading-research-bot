## 2. Business

**Created:** a blood draw that screens average-risk adults 45+ for
colorectal cancer without a colonoscopy prep or a stool kit. In PREEMPT CRC
(48,000+ enrolled, 200+ sites) SimpleScreen CRC detected cancer with 81.1%
sensitivity, advanced precancerous lesions with 13.7% (30.7% for high-grade
dysplasia) at 90.4% specificity for advanced neoplasia (8-K 2026-07-27).
The surplus for the patient is convenience and adherence — 40–50M eligible
Americans are unscreened; the cost is that a blood test misses most
precancerous lesions, so it is a screen for the non-adherent, not a
colonoscopy substitute. The underlying platform is a non-bisulfite,
base-resolution epigenetic assay plus multiomic ML classifiers; lung
(Breakthrough Device Designation 2026-08-12, clinical evaluation ongoing)
and other indications are pipeline.

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms, none of which is US product sales.
(1) Abbott (formerly Exact Sciences) license: $75M upfront (Nov 2025),
$100M FDA-approval milestone (earned Jul 2026), up to $600M further
development/regulatory milestones, up to $20M/yr development-cost
reimbursement for three years, lab service fees for tests Freenome runs for
Abbott, and tiered royalties capped at 10% of US net sales conditioned on a
20% gross margin; Abbott owns the exclusive US commercialization of the
CRC-only test. (2) Roche Sequencing option: $75M option fee received
(sitting in $71.1M of current deferred revenue), up to $59M further
milestones plus $24M SBX milestones, and low-single-digit to mid-teens
royalties on ex-US kitted tests if Roche exercises. (3) EarlyCDT Lung kit
sales and royalties (UK/international; ~$0.8M/quarter of "service and
other" revenue). (4) Retained rights: SimpleScreen CRC when ordered as part
of a Freenome multi-cancer bundle (CRC + lung LDT, "second half of 2026"),
where Freenome would keep the full test economics — no volumes or pricing
exist yet.

**Protected:** the FDA PMA and the PREEMPT dataset are the moat that
exists — a second entrant needs a multi-year prospective trial (Freenome is
one of two approved; the prospectus names Caris, Exact, GRAIL, Guardant,
LabCorp, Natera, Quest as competitors). What it does not protect: the
platform's edge over Shield is unproven at equal headline sensitivity, the
"data flywheel" claim rests on volumes that are Abbott's to generate, and
the reagent supply is sole-sourced (Illumina to 2033; NEB with minimum
purchase commitments). The commercial moat in the US belongs to Abbott's
Cologuard sales force, which Freenome rents at a capped royalty.

**Control:** one class of common, one vote per share, no controlling holder.
Five-percent holders at closing: Roche 17.4% (also a commercial partner),
RA Capital 14.3% (13D/A 2026-07-22), Perceptive 12.4% (the SPAC sponsor's
affiliate and PIPE investor), Andreessen Horowitz 5.2%; directors and
officers as a group 4.8%. A staggered three-class board, blank-check
preferred and DGCL §203 make an unsolicited takeover slow but not
foreclosed; Roche's 17.4% plus its ex-US option makes Roche the natural
acquirer and a practical veto on any other.
