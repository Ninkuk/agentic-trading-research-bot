## 2. Business

**Created:** a subcutaneous antibody that blocks the neonatal Fc receptor
(FcRn). FcRn normally recycles IgG, so blocking it lowers circulating IgG,
including the pathogenic autoantibodies behind IgG-mediated autoimmune
disease (stockanalysis company profile; 8-K 2026-05-20). IMVT-1402 is the
second-generation molecule. It was designed to keep batoclimab's depth of IgG
lowering without batoclimab's albumin drop and LDL rise (low-confidence
secondary summary of company disclosures). For a patient with uncontrolled
Graves' disease, the promise is a non-ablative alternative to antithyroid
drugs, radioiodine or thyroidectomy. Immunovant's batoclimab Phase 2 claimed
treatment-free six-month remissions (Immunovant press release, 2025;
open-label). In D2T RA, the patients have failed two or more advanced
mechanisms (86.7% of the Period 1 cohort), so a new mechanism has real value
if it holds up under randomization.

**Captured:** nothing today. There has been no product revenue in any fiscal
year (stockanalysis financials, FY22–FY26). Capture would come through three
channels:
(a) a US GD label, where it would be first-in-class if first to file;
(b) entry into the existing FcRn market in MG/CIDP. That market is a
specialty-pharmacy, buy-and-bill/self-injection franchise, priced like
VYVGART;
(c) an RA label, a large market but a crowded one.
There is no partner and no royalty income. Roivant could also buy out the
minority (see Control), which would be capture for today's minority holders
through a takeout premium. That is *possible*-tier option value only (§6).

**Protected:** composition-of-matter IP on IMVT-1402, plus clinical lead
time in GD. There is no moat in MG/CIDP. The class leader (argenx) has an
estimated ~$6B annualized run-rate (Q2 2026 $1.5B), seronegative-MG label
expansion and an established prefilled-syringe franchise. J&J's nipocalimab
is also in market. Roivant's own CEO concedes VYVGART "may very well remain
the class leader" (Q1 call). In GD the protection is timing: be first and set
the label. argenx (efgartigimod), Biohaven (BHV-1300) and Sanofi
(rilzabrutinib) are all pursuing GD (low-confidence secondary summaries).
Whether depth of IgG suppression is a real clinical advantage is **UNKNOWN**
to this author. It is domain science I cannot evaluate. The batoclimab MG
data were debated on exactly this point (Gline, Q1 call), and the CLE study
failed despite a post-hoc depth-response association.

**Control:** Roivant Sciences beneficially owns 113,327,007 shares, 54.9%
(DEF 14A filed 2026-07-21, as of 2026-06-30). The holder of the Series A
preferred elects four directors, including CEO Eric Venker. Immunovant is a
Nasdaq "controlled company" and uses the board-independence exemptions. This
forecloses any unsolicited takeover or activist path. The only change-of-
control route runs through Roivant: a squeeze-out, or a sale Roivant chooses.
Insider (non-Roivant) ownership is 0.70% (stocks.db).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue is $0 in every year. The
