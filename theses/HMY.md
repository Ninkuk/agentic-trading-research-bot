## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $22.44.** kill-thesis: **UNPROVEN** — conditions=5 (1 probable,
4 plausible), refuted=0, unknown=4.

Harmony is executing: an eleventh straight year on guidance, CSA integrated at
the top of its range, a credible 40kt copper path, a refinanced balance sheet
with a fraction of a turn of net debt. The problem is the price of the
execution. At $14.0B the market pays ~20× trailing FCF and ~14× the company's
own adjusted FCF, earned at a record $3,811/oz received; the FY27 guide takes
~5% of the ounces away and adds ~13% to USD unit cost before Eva's heaviest
spending year. On flat cash the price implies 5–8%/yr against an ~11.4%
hurdle; it clears the hurdle only when Eva's post-2028 ramp is credited in
full at today's gold. That is a leveraged bet on one commodity price this
desk has no edge on, priced as though the bet has already paid.

**Closest attack:** the FY27 cash bridge — 1.35Moz × ($4,600 − $2,480) is
only ~$2.9B pre-tax and pre-hedge; after ~30% tax/royalty and ~$1.0B of
committed growth capex the year's levered FCF is ~$1.1B (7.8% yield), and
that number uses *zero* hedge drag on a 30%-of-production rolling book whose
strikes are still unfetched.

Load-bearing conditions for the bull case — **5**:

1. Gold sustained ≥ ~$4,500/oz through the FY27–FY29 build. *Plausible* —
   spot is there today; nothing in any disclosure evidences persistence, and
   the FY26 average received was $3,811.
2. SA operations deliver the FY27 guide (1.3–1.4Moz, AISC ≤ $2,570/oz).
   *Probable* — eleven consecutive years on guidance (trading statement 6-K,

5. USD unit costs hold inside the FY27 guide (rand and wage/electricity
