## 2. Business

**Created:** a customer buys into an integrated device-plus-software
system whose parts are designed together — custom silicon (A20 Pro on a
2nm process, the in-house C2 modem announced today), the operating
systems, the stores, the payment rail, the watch, the earbuds — and gets
performance, battery life, privacy posture and cross-device continuity
that no assembled-from-parts competitor matches at the same friction cost.
The surplus is legible in the numbers management quotes: 99% US customer
satisfaction on iPhone and 95–98% across Mac, iPad and Watch (451 Research
via the Q3 call, primary-transcribed), June-quarter records for upgraders
on iPhone, Mac and Watch, and over half of iPad and Watch buyers new to the
product (Q3 call). Demand this cycle outran Apple's own forecast: iPhone
+22% and Mac +29% in the June quarter *with* supply constraints (Q3 call;
10-Q products revenue $78.678B, +18%).

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms, not one. (1) **Hardware margin at
scale** — products gross margin 40.1% in the June quarter (10-Q; ~37.5%
ex-refund), on $272.6B of nine-month products revenue; pricing power is
being exercised right now (Mac/iPad increases over the summer, iPhone 18
Pro +$100, Duo at $1,999). (2) **Platform rents** — the App Store
commission, advertising (App Store search ads), and payments (Apple Pay,
Apple Card) — the part of services regulators are prying at (§3). (3)
**Subscriptions** — iCloud, Music, TV, Arcade, Fitness+, News+, AppleCare,
1.5B paid subscriptions (Q3 call), with iCloud+ tiers named by Cook as the
intended monetization of Siri AI compute. (4) **Search licensing** — the
Google default-search payment, named in the FY25 10-K as a revenue source
"currently subject to government investigations and legal proceedings"
whose remedies "could materially adversely affect the Company's ability to
earn revenue from such licensing arrangements". Services were $109.158B in
FY25 (10-K) and $120.5B TTM (FY25 less nine-month FY25 plus nine-month
FY26, both 10-Q), 25.8% of revenue, at 75.6% gross margin (10-Q).

**Protected:** the mechanism is switching cost compounded across devices
and time — purchased apps and media, iMessage groups, iCloud photo
libraries, a paired Watch that works with nothing else, Apple Pay
enrolments, family sharing — multiplied by a silicon lead the 10-K states
plainly ("custom components available from only one source") that
competitors cannot buy off the shelf. The observable evidence is the
upgrader records and the installed base compounding (1.3B → 1.4B → 1.5B
active *iPhones*, 2021–2025, stockanalysis metrics; >2.5B devices, Q3
call). The honest weakness: the rents in mechanism (2) rest on control of
distribution, and three jurisdictions are litigating exactly that (§3).
The moat on the device is intact; the moat on the toll booth is being
adjudicated.

**Control:** one class of common stock, one vote per share,
14,594,180,000 shares outstanding as of July 17, 2026 (10-Q cover); no
controlling holder — insiders 0.06%, institutions 65.7% (probe). The null
answer: nothing forecloses a takeover or an activist except the $4.6T
size. Governance note: Tim Cook moved to executive chairman on
2026-09-01 as John Ternus became CEO (8-K/A 2026-09-01, Item 5.02) — a
former CEO chairing the board of his hand-picked successor.
