## 2. Business

**Created:** a protein-replacement therapy for Friedreich's ataxia (FA), a
progressive, fatal neuromuscular disease caused by frataxin deficiency.
Nomlabofusp is a fusion protein that carries frataxin into cells via a
cell-penetrating peptide, given by subcutaneous injection (Q2 8-K;
curefa.org). Patients would get the first therapy that replaces the missing
protein itself. The only approved drug, Skyclarys (omaveloxolone, Biogen),
works downstream through Nrf2 activation. Whether frataxin replacement
modifies the disease more than Skyclarys does is the central scientific
question, and I cannot evaluate it. UNKNOWN.

**Captured:** nothing today; there is no revenue. Future capture would be:
- US list-price sales of a chronic rare-disease biologic. The launch is
  targeted for mid-2027 if approved (Q2 8-K). The 2026-09-29 hire of a
  commercial-background President/COO (ex-Esperion, Melinta CCO; 8-K Item
  5.02) is launch preparation.
- Ex-US licensing. No partner is disclosed.
- A possible priority review voucher on approval, if the program holds rare
  pediatric disease designation (NOT OBTAINED this run, not load-bearing).
- A takeover by an FA or rare-neuro franchise.

**Protected:** molecule patents (term not checked this run) and biologic
exclusivity on approval. The real threats are rival mechanisms, not copies:
Skyclarys is already on the market, Lexeo's LX2006 is an AAV gene therapy for
FA cardiomyopathy in SUNRISE-FA 2, and PTC's vatiquinone received a CRL.
Protection here is regulatory exclusivity plus whatever clinical edge the data
show. I find no moat beyond that.

**Control:** one class of voting common. Insiders hold 1.16% and
institutions 49.1% (stocks.db `v_latest`). The 500,000 preferred shares came
from exchanging common in Q1 2026 (250,000 preferred for 2.5M common, so
10:1). That pattern usually means a large holder is managing an ownership cap.
The terms and the holder were NOT OBTAINED: the 10-Q text truncated before the
equity note, and the exhibit 3.1 certificate was not read. Nothing structural
forecloses a takeover. The new President/COO has a change-in-control severance
of 12 months' salary plus target bonus (8-K, 2026-09-29), which is standard.
