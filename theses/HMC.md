0.55× equity. The price does not need the turnaround; it needs autos to stop
losing money and motorcycles to stay what they are.

input that can turn the automobile segment into a large loss again.

## 2. Business

**Created:** Three engines. Motorcycles (FY26 revenue ¥4.02T): durable,
cheap-to-run two-wheelers with the densest dealer and service network in
India, Vietnam, Indonesia, Thailand and Brazil; the customer gets reliable
mobility and resale value at an entry-car-like price point. Automobiles
(¥13.9T): fuel-efficient compact and mid-size cars and light trucks (Civic,
CR-V, Accord, HR-V) with a hybrid ratio in the high-17% range in the US and
~10% US share; the customer gets low running cost, reliability and resale.
Financial services (¥3.5T): captive retail loans and leases on Honda
products, mainly in North America — ¥10.2T of receivables and a ¥6.6T
operating-lease fleet that both sell cars and earn a spread.

**Captured:** (1) Motorcycle unit margin of 18–20% from scale, in-house
engines and local procurement; (2) automobile unit margin that is thin and
volatile — FY26 adjusted 0.3%, Q1 FY27 5.0% — managed through 1–2%/yr price
revisions and incentive discipline; (3) finance spread and lease residuals
on ¥17.65T of finance assets (consolidated interest received ¥791B vs paid
¥495B in FY26), segment profit ¥276B; (4) royalty and equity-method income
from the China joint ventures, now small (Q1 FY27 equity income ¥22.6B
group-wide) after ¥90.9B of JV impairments.

**Protected:** Motorcycles — distribution and service density, a reliability
brand and the India cost curve are a real mechanism against incumbent
rivals; they are not a mechanism against electric two-wheelers, where Honda
is a follower. Automobiles — the two-motor hybrid system, a tariff-exempt
North American footprint and brand loyalty protect the US position; against
Chinese software-defined and electric competitors in China and ASEAN there
is no protection, and Honda says so itself (2026-03-12: "a decline in the
competitiveness of Honda products in Asia"). The honest answer is one moat
in motorcycles, none in autos outside North America.

**Control:** One share, one vote, no controlling holder. Holders of record
above 5% at 2026-03-31 are trust banks (Master Trust Bank of Japan 17.5%,
Custody Bank of Japan 7.1%) and the ADS depositary nominee 5.7%; BlackRock
6.1% by 13G. Three-committee board, 11 directors re-elected at 90–97%
(2026-06-26 AGM). Nothing forecloses a takeover or an activist path; Honda
was the acquirer, not the target, in the collapsed Nissan talks.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue rose 55% from FY22 to
FY26 while operating profit went from ¥871B to a loss; even excluding the
EV charges, FY26's adjusted ¥1,039B is below FY24's ¥1,382B on 7% more
revenue. Figures ¥B, fiscal years ending March (stockanalysis, JPY; TTM to
2026-06-30):
