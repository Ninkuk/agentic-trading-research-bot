## 2. Business

**Created:** Pemvidutide is a once-weekly peptide that agonises GLP-1 and
glucagon receptors in a 1:1 ratio. Glucagon acts directly on the liver: fat
oxidation, which lowers liver fat. GLP-1 drives weight loss. The pitch to a
hepatologist is that it treats liver fat and weight together with less muscle
loss and simpler titration than the incumbents. It targets three indications:
MASH (Ph3 PERFORMA), alcohol use disorder (Ph2 RECLAIM met its primary
endpoint 2026-07-28), and alcohol-associated liver disease (Ph2 RESTORE,
topline 2H27) (Q2 2026 call; Stifel 2026-09-30). The patient surplus is
hypothetical until approval. AUD has few effective drugs, so a drug that
reduces drinking and liver injury at once would be a real surplus there.

**Captured:** Nothing today: TTM revenue is $31K (stockanalysis). Capture
could come three ways. (a) Direct US specialty sales in MASH/AUD; the company
has a Chief Commercial Officer and talks of "pre-commercial activities"
(April 8-K use of proceeds). (b) An ex-US or global licence with upfront,
milestones, and royalties. (c) Outright sale of the company. Which one
happens is UNKNOWN; management names (b)-style funding for AUD.

**Protected:** Composition-of-matter patents on the molecule (term not
verified this run — §6). The claimed mechanism is the "balanced" ratio:
management says "there aren't any other balanced glucagon and GLPs in
development" (Stifel). That is a description, not a barrier. Lilly, Novo,
Boehringer, and others can tune ratios, and the incumbents already have MASH
labels or Ph3 data. The only real protection is regulatory exclusivity once
approved, plus first-mover status in AUD-with-liver-disease if RECLAIM's
signal reproduces. On a competitive moat in MASH, I have no good answer.

**Control:** One class of common stock; no controlling holder. Insiders hold
0.48% and institutions 62.8% (`stocks.db`). Several Schedule 13Gs were filed
after the April offering (EDGAR, 2026-04-30 to 05-15). Charter anti-takeover
provisions were not read this run. Nothing structural forecloses a takeover
or an activist.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): flat** (pre-revenue; not meaningful). Revenue
rounds to zero; the operating loss is range-bound. EPS "improvement" is share
dilution, not leverage.
