## 2. Business

**Created:** custom software/product engineering and modernization for
Forbes-G2000 clients — senior engineering density (historically
Eastern-European talent arbitrage, now globalized: 56,650 delivery
professionals across CEE, India, LatAm) applied to complex product work the
client cannot staff internally at comparable quality/cost. The current pitch:
AI-led legacy modernization (mainframe reverse-engineering via MF Lens, data
platforms, "AI-native" engineering) — the claim is that decades of client
technical debt are finally addressable *because of* AI, and someone must do
that work [Q2'26 call].

**Captured:** overwhelmingly time-and-materials billing, re-sold/renewed
continuously — the CEO's own words: "most of our business is time and material
contracts… you need to resell or renew the contracts quite regularly" [Q2'26
call]. Rate minus globally-arbitraged delivery cost: 29.5% TTM gross margin,
10.0% TTM GAAP operating margin (16.4% Q2 non-GAAP IFO). Revenue per employee
$89.4k [stockanalysis statistics]. No backlog annuity: revenue visibility is
one contract renewal cycle deep.

**Protected:** thin, and the write-up should not pretend otherwise. Switching
costs on embedded teams and client intimacy (top-20 clients still grew 3.1%
YoY in Q2), a reputation/talent brand, and now AI partner certifications at
scale (5,700+ Anthropic-, 2,000+ Gemini-certified engineers; OpenAI advanced
partner) [Q2'26 call]. None of this stops a competitor tomorrow; contracts
re-bid regularly and the 10-K concedes clients "may seek other service
providers or expect price concessions" as AI cuts delivery cost [10-K Item
1A]. The moat is a mechanism only at the engagement level (incumbent team
knowledge), not at the firm level.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** FY2021→TTM: revenue $3.76B→$5.62B
(+49%) while operating income went $542M→$563M (+4%); GAAP op margin
14.4%→10.0% [stockanalysis income-statement route]. Revenue growth since 2021
bought no profit growth. Partial offset: diluted shares 59.1M→52.3M (−11.5%),
so GAAP EPS compounded anyway ($8.15→$7.39 trough-to-recovery; Q2'26 EPS +26%
YoY). The margin bleed is 2022-cost-inflation + geo-rebalancing +
acquisitions; direction has stabilized in 2026 (Q2 GAAP IFO +20.4% YoY on
+4.5% revenue; FY IFO guide raised to high end 10.5–11%).

## 3. Threads pulled

- **The NA stall — the load-bearing thread.** Q2: Americas (57% of revenue)
  grew **0.5%** YoY while EMEA grew **10.9%** (9.4% cc); FY guide cut to
  3.2–4.2% (organic cc 2–3%) [Q2'26 release]. Management: the weakness is
  "largely idiosyncratic to EPAM," two causes: (a) a genuine demand shift —
  SaaS/high-tech clients reprioritizing budgets away from task-based services
  (manual testing, UX, JS front-end) toward AI, funding "tokens, hardware, and
  infrastructure" faster than replacement AI-services work ramps; (b) their
  own NA go-to-market "has not been operating at the level it needs to,"
  selling engineering excellence to CTOs when buyers now demand business cases
  [Q2'26 call]. The EMEA precedent (same delivery org, rebuilt go-to-market,
  double-digit growth) is the tangible evidence the fix can work; it is also

  **Unresolvable this quarter — the reopen condition.**
