## 0. The reopen question, answered first

**Was the amendment executed?** Yes — but not via a standalone item-1.01 8-K.
The **Amended and Restated Master Services Agreement was signed July 29, 2026**
and disclosed in the 10-Q filed 2026-07-30 (Note 11 Subsequent Event,
incorporated into Part II Item 5; the executed agreement itself is **Exhibit
10.2** to that 10-Q). EDGAR confirms no 1.01 8-K exists; the only new filings
since 07-27 are the 2.02/9.01 earnings 8-K and the 10-Q (both 2026-07-30).

**On LOI-like terms?** Yes, with one substitution. CFO Bachus, on the call:
"terms of the amended MSA are generally consistent with the letter of intent."
Executed terms vs the June 3 LOI:
- Effective July 1, 2026; initial term 15 years to **June 30, 2041**, plus up
  to three 5-year auto-renewals (18-month non-renewal notice). = LOI (~2041).
- **Termination-for-convenience eliminated** (and its 100%-of-TTM fee with it). = LOI.
- Fee restructured to **60% of tuition and academic-related fees only**;
  ancillary revenue (housing, food, arena, etc.) solely GCU's; GCE's academic
  cost reimbursement to GCU eliminated. = LOI.
- **Substitution:** the LOI said the 50%-of-TTM non-renewal fee would be
  "substantially lowered"; the executed MSA **replaces it entirely** with an
  18-month post-termination period in which GCE keeps providing services and
  collecting service fees. A fee became a paid wind-down — economically softer
  than a cash fee, but it converts a cliff into an 18-month tail.

**Amendment economics (confirmed at the print):** revenue −$4M Q3'26, −$6M
Q4'26, ~**−$20M/yr** (~1.8% of revenue); offsetting cost relief $3M Q3 / $5M
Q4 (reimbursement eliminated), so op income declines "by an immaterial amount
that should not exceed $1 million per quarter" (10-Q Note 11 + CFO on call).
Matches the LOI's disclosed impact.

**GCU online new starts:** Q2 new online enrollments grew **low single
digits** against a prior-year quarter in which starts were up mid-teens
(management: "expected... the second quarter is not a traditional
back-to-school time"). Total GCU online enrollment 113,011, **+7.8%** YoY;
total GCU 121,921 (+7.5%); hybrid sites +18.5% ex-teach-out. H2 2026 guided:
new online enrollments up **mid-to-high single digits**.

**Op-income impact / print quality:** Q2 revenue $264.0M (+6.7%), op income

$1.81 vs $1.53. FY2026 guide updated: revenue $1,165.3–1,172.3M, op margin
