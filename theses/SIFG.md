## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at €3.80.** kill-thesis: **UNPROVEN** — conditions=3 (2 probable,
1 unknown-by-construction), refuted=0, unknown=1.

Sif is Europe's monopile-foundation capacity leader, and the operational
turnaround is real: the debt-funded Maasvlakte 2 mega-factory now runs at
its planned 4–5 monopiles/week, H1 2026 revenue nearly doubled to €498.4M
and adjusted EBITDA more than tripled to €43.4M. The equity, however, is
not priced on that recovery — it is an at-the-money call on a financing
event. Cash was €7.1M on 30 June against a €50M undrawn RCF; a €175M
Nordic bond was mandated 23 June and pulled 1 July on terms; the CFO said
on the H1 call that the balance sheet must be strengthened *regardless* of
whether the pivotal 190kt order lands; and the press release puts the
company in "discussions with its largest shareholder and its lenders." The
terms of that event will be set by a 56% PE owner and the banks, are
disclosed nowhere, and govern most of the equity outcome across a 2027–28
order-book air pocket management itself calls "a very difficult period."
Cheap is established; own-able is not — an outside minority cannot price
the one event that decides the case.

**Closest attack:** the survival scenarios imply ~30%+/yr against a ~7–8%
hurdle, and the option lens prices the equity at 0.97–1.24× market — "you
are paid handsomely for a coin flip, so expected value argues buy." It
fails because risk-neutral is not physical probability, the lens excludes
dilution (a distressed raise transfers value from minorities before the
coin lands), and a band that straddles 1.0× says the market already pays
roughly fair value for the flip — there is no margin of safety in either
frame, only variance.

Load-bearing conditions of the pass:

1. *Probable* — a balance-sheet strengthening happens before mid-2027 on
   terms not knowable today. CFO, H1 call: "either way, we need to
   strengthen our balance sheet and improve our liquidity position"; the
   bond attempt and its withdrawal are press-released facts.
2. *Probable* — 2027 is a trough with real downside width. Company facts:
   €40M of adjusted EBITDA deliberately shifted 2026→2027, order book only

3. *Unknown (by construction)* — the discount is insufficient compensation
   for the information asymmetry: Egeria (~56%) and the lenders know the
   financing terms and the 190kt status; minorities do not. The evidence
   that would price this exists in no disclosure — which is the pass.

## 2. Business

**Created:** offshore wind farms need foundations; monopiles are the
dominant fixed-bottom type and are getting larger (XXL) as turbines grow.
Sif fabricates them at Roermond and at the new Maasvlakte 2 (Rotterdam)
plant — deep-water quay access, marshalling services, and now a
demonstrated 4–5 monopiles/week run rate. Customers (Ørsted-class
developers, Equinor) get delivery certainty for the single
longest-lead-time structural item in a wind farm.

**Captured:** three distinct mechanisms, not one. (a) Contribution per ton
on contracted monopile tonnage — the core; management calls current levels
"very healthy" and H1 contribution was €134.7M on 141kt. (b) OSS
(offshore substation structures) fabrication — €42.3M revenue in FY2025,
growing. (c) Marshalling/logistics rental at the Rotterdam site — small
(€2.1M FY2025). Contracts carry milestone prepayments (contract
liabilities), which financed part of the factory — and whose unwind is now
draining cash.

**Protected:** thin, and honestly stated: capacity plus location plus
qualification. There are only a handful of European XXL monopile makers
(EEW, Steelwind, Haizea, a struggling UK entrant), entry costs hundreds of
millions and years (Sif's own plant took €338M capex over 2023–24), and
the Rotterdam deep-water position is genuinely scarce. But the CEO said it
plainly on the H1 call: "there's not enough projects for the total supply
chain... we fight for it every day" — in a 2027–28 demand trough the moat
does not protect price, and Dajin (China) "is very aggressively bidding
for projects." Protection is real at full utilization and nearly worthless
in a capacity glut; the EU steel-protection measures management lobbies
for are pending, not landed.

**Control:** Egeria Capital Management (Dutch PE) holds ~56%; Equinor
~13.9% plus a €50M convertible preferred (stake figures: financial-press
tier; "one large shareholder" and the shareholder discussions are primary,
from the H1 call and release). Free float is ~9.0M of 29.9M shares (~30%).
One share class. The structure forecloses an unsolicited takeover and any
activist path, and makes the imminent financing a negotiation between
insiders — including the unmodellable possibility of a low-ball
take-private. CEO transition on the day of the H1 print: Fred van Beers
out, Koen Bogers in (1 Aug 2026).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative through FY2025, inflecting
positive in H1 2026.** FY2021→FY2025 revenue €422.5M→€597.0M (+41%) while
operating income went €16.0M→**−€41.5M** (FY2024: +€3.9M) — the ramp was
paid through the P&L. H1 2026 swung hard the other way: revenue +93% YoY
to €498.4M, adjusted EBITDA €12.9M→€43.4M, net loss narrowed €25.9M→€4.1M.

  still no contract; 60–80 people across customer/supplier/design working
  "as if" toward a May 2027 start (September fallback); early-works
  agreements under discussion. It is 190 of the 197kt exclusive slice of a
  422kt order book (225kt contracted). Management is "pretty confident";
  the same call concedes the whole market's FIDs are slipping. Finding:
  binary, imminent-ish, unpriceable from outside.
- **The 2027–28 air pocket.** Gigawatts *connected* 2026–27 are being
  produced now; the FID gap hits fabricators in 2027–28 before the

## 5. Falsifiers

For the pass (flip toward buy):

- **Shift —** the financing lands on disclosed, minority-fair terms (a
  pro-rata rights issue, or debt at a viable coupon with no equity
  sweetener): the un-analyzable event becomes analyzable — revalue.
- **Shift —** the 190kt contract is signed with a May-2027 start: 2027
  EBITDA visibility jumps toward the €90M frame and the trough narrows —
  revalue immediately.
- **Shift —** contracted (not exclusive) order intake covering 2027–28
  beyond the 190kt, or landed EU steel-protection/localization measures —
  revalue.

For an owner (sell):

- **Break —** a rescue financing that heavily dilutes or primes minorities
  (deep-discount issue to Egeria, convertible with ratchets, or a
  take-private at a distressed price) — the asymmetry cashed in; over.
- **Break —** the 190kt exclusivity ends or the project cancels — the 2027
  book collapses to ~225kt contracted and shrinking; over.
- **Shift —** Q3 trading update (2026-11-06) shows cash below ~€40M
  total liquidity or a covenant re-amendment — the financing gets done on
  worse terms, sooner.
