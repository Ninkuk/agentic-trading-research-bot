## 2. Business

**Created:** a one-stop wholesale catalogue for indoor and hydroponic
growers. About 1,000 wholesale accounts (hydroponic specialty retailers,
garden centers, commercial resellers, e-commerce) buy nutrients, grow
media, lighting, climate equipment and supplies from DCs in the US and Spain
(Oct-7 investor presentation, slides 3 and 11). Roughly three-quarters of
FY2025 net sales were consumables replenished every grow cycle (slide 11).
The end customer is overwhelmingly cannabis cultivation, which the company
names as the source of "industry oversupply and falling crop prices" (Q2
release).

**Captured:** three mechanisms. (a) Manufacturer margin on 30+ proprietary
brands (Roots Organics and others), consolidated into Eugene, OR
manufacturing; Q2 had the best proprietary sales mix ever (Q2 release).
(b) Distributor margin on partner brands, being cut back; "discontinued
distributed brands" is a named driver of the revenue fall. (c) A
third-party-logistics fee business ("Project Agility") that rents out
surplus DC capacity; logistics income is 2.8× its 2023 level (slide 11,
base undisclosed). Company-wide adjusted gross margin was 20.0% in Q2 and
GAAP gross margin 11.3%.

**Protected:** no moat. Retailer shelf relationships and brand familiarity
among growers are real but contestable: nutrients and media are formulated
commodities, lighting is commoditized, and the 2022–2025 history shows a
$232M impairment in FY2025 alone, mostly acquired brand value written off
(slide 13). The protection question is moot while creditors decide who owns
the business.

**Control:** stocks.db shows insiders at 19.2% and institutions at 5.9%.
The share-class structure was not verified from the 10-K cover or the 2026
DEF 14A (NOT OBTAINED; immaterial). Under forbearance, the term-loan lenders,
with FEAC installed as agent, control the outcome, not the shareholders. A
2026-10-05 settlement agreed to issue 300,000 non-voting convertible preferred
shares (convertible 1:1 on stockholder approval, ~6.2% of the 4.82M common)
to settle claims from an unnamed party's "engagement" (8-K 2026-10-09).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue fell 78% while the
operating loss grew from breakeven to about a third of sales.
