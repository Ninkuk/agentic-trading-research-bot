## 2. Business

**Created:** JADE101 is a half-life-extended monoclonal antibody against
APRIL, a cytokine that drives production of the galactose-deficient IgA1 at
the root of IgA nephropathy. The patient gets disease modification (less
pathogenic IgA, less proteinuria, slower eGFR loss), which class rivals
already offer. The customer surplus Jade proposes is a 350 mg maintenance
dose every 12 weeks. Half-life is 24.2 days, "8.7-fold longer than
povetacicept; 2.6-fold longer than sibeprenlimab" (8-K 2026-06-01,
cross-trial). Behind it: JADE201 (anti-BAFF-R, rheumatoid arthritis, Phase 1
dosing) and JADE301 (anti-IFN-β, dermatomyositis, Phase 1 start Q4 2026,
interim H2 2027; 8-K 2026-09-30).

**Captured:** None today. Revenue is zero. The future mechanism is a specialty
biologic sold to nephrologists, probably at a list price near the class
(UNKNOWN), with a royalty owed to the licensor. The JADE101 license terms
were not obtained this run. The July 2026 license for an undisclosed target
carries "low to mid-single-digit" royalties and up to $22.0M of milestones
(8-K 2026-07-01), which suggests the JADE101 terms are similar. Partnering
JADE101 to a large nephrology franchise is the other capture route; nothing
has been announced.

**Protected:** Composition-of-matter patents on the antibody (in-licensed from
Paragon) protect the molecule, not the mechanism: four other APRIL or
BAFF/APRIL agents exist. The one mechanism-level defence is PK. If Q12W
dosing holds in patients, a rival needs its own half-life-extended antibody,
and Novartis and Otsuka could build one. Read honestly, the protection is a
dosing-interval lead of unknown durability. It is not a moat.

**Control:** One class of voting common. Fairmount Funds (which also founded
the licensor, Paragon) beneficially owns 14,069,494 shares, 19.99% (13D/A
2026-06-09). It holds further pre-funded warrants and non-voting Series A
preferred, capped by 9.99% and 19.99% ownership blockers. No legal controller
exists, but Fairmount is both the largest holder and the counterparty to the
pipeline's licenses: a related-party relationship on every in-licensed asset.
Insiders hold 0.28% (stocks.db). No takeover is foreclosed. A large-pharma
bid needs Fairmount's assent in practice, not in law.
