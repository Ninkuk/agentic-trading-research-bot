## 2. Business

**Created:** never-run-out convenience for a non-discretionary, emotionally
attached spend. Autoship (84.4% of net sales, +10.5% y/y vs +7.7% company, Q1 FY2026
10-Q) auto-delivers food/litter/meds; deep pet-specialty assortment incl. Rx pharmacy;
24/7 service. 21.5M active customers (+3.6% y/y), NSPAC $597 and compounding
($565 → $597 over 8 quarters, metrics route).

**Captured:** ~30% retail gross margin, plus three higher-margin layers —
Sponsored Ads (management underwrites 3%-of-sales entitlement, ~two-thirds flowing to
the bottom line), health/pharmacy mix shift, and vet clinics (47 post-Modern-Animal,
~60 exiting FY2026, ~$290M steady-state revenue; 40% of clinic customers are new to
Chewy with ~$900 year-1 NSPAC — management figures, undisclosed elsewhere).
Adj EBITDA margin 7.5% in Q1 (+130bp y/y); FY guide 6.6–6.8% held through the revenue
cut; stated long-term target 10%.

**Protected:** honestly mild. Rx pharmacy licensure and vet relationships are the
hardest piece; Autoship inertia and pet-specialty assortment help; nothing hard
protects national-brand food from Amazon/Walmart except scale economics and a decade of
demonstrated share retention. 10-K Item 1A's dominant structural risk is exactly this
competition. Silence on a harder mechanism is itself the answer: this is an
execution-and-inertia moat, not a structural one.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** (Income-statement route, FY =
fiscal year ending early Feb): revenue $8.97B → $10.12B → $11.15B → $11.86B
→ $12.60B → $12.84B TTM while operating income went −$74M → $56M → −$24M →
$113M → $254M → $306M TTM. FCF $8.6M → $584.5M over the same span. SBC
~$290M/yr ≈ half of FCF.
