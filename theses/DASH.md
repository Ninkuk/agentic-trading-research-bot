## 2. Business

**Created:** consumers get ~anything local delivered in under an hour — restaurants
first, now grocery/convenience/retail — with selection, speed, and (via DashPass)
affordability; merchants get incremental demand plus increasingly a services stack
(Digital Ordering, SevenRooms reservations, Drive white-label logistics); Dashers get
low-barrier flexible earnings, often same-day (10-K Part I). Management claims 20–25
eating occasions/week per person as the addressable frame (Q1'26 call) — the surplus
argument is that a delivered meal or basket buys back an hour.

**Captured:** not "a commission" — at least six distinct mechanisms: merchant
commission tiers on marketplace GOV, consumer service/delivery fees, DashPass /
Wolt+ / Deliveroo Plus subscriptions (record signups Q1'26), advertising (record
high and growing fastest; CPG "cracked," lower ad load than peers — Q1'26 call),
DoorDash for Business (catering/Meal Manager/corporate DashPass), and Drive
(per-delivery white-label fees). Net revenue margin — revenue as % of marketplace
GOV — was 12.8% in Q1'26 (release), down from 13.3% Q4'25 on Deliveroo mix and the
gas-rewards program.

**Protected:** the honest answer is *scale density per city, compounded across three
sides*: more consumers → more Dasher hours → faster/cheaper delivery → more
merchants → more selection. DashPass adds a switching cost on the consumer side
(accelerating member growth, reduced churn — Q1'26 release). Management's preferred
frame is the proprietary "catalog of the physical world" — billions of annotated
local SKUs that "cannot be scraped" (Tony Xu, Q1'26 call), which is also their
answer to agentic-AI disintermediation (precedent cited: Google Food Ordering ran
~8 years and shut down on weak retention). Label that management framing, not a
verified fact. What the moat does NOT protect: consumers multi-home (Uber Eats one
tap away), merchants multi-home, and the labor input is priced by regulators as
much as by markets (Prop 22 costs already passed through as higher CA fees — 10-K).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive** ("strongly positive" — a 22-point
margin climb in three years, GAAP profitable from 2024 (net income) / 2025
(operating)). Income statement route, stockanalysis; `/statistics/` shows
