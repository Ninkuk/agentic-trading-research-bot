  shareholders approve the full conversion (deadline March 15, 2027), the
  conversion is capped at **19.99% of voting power** and the balance is

- Runway restated in Item 8.01: **"until October 15, 2026"**, after which,
  absent new funding or a strategic transaction, "the Company will be unable

the stub is *fairly priced at best* at the contractual maturity (1.15× model
