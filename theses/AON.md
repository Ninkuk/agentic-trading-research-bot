## 2. Business

**Created:** risk broking (commercial P&C, reinsurance) and human-capital
services (health, wealth). Clients get placement leverage, analytics
(Risk/Health Analyzers, Claims Copilot), and access to capital — including
non-traditional capital the client could not source alone (data-center
program now $5B capacity, single facilities up to $13–15B; Q2 call).
Retention mid-90s.

**Captured:** commissions on placed premium plus fees on advisory/analytics;
fiduciary investment income on client float ($58M in Q2, falling with rates).
Not one business: commercial risk, reinsurance treaty/fac, ILS, health
benefits brokerage, pension/wealth advisory, and NFP's middle-market platform
each price differently.

**Protected:** brokerage is a scale-and-data oligopoly (Aon/Marsh/AJG/WTW).
The mechanism: placement data across thousands of programs feeds pricing
analytics a sub-scale broker cannot replicate; mid-90s retention plus
multi-line penetration raises switching cost; ABS centralizes ops cost. Nine
consecutive quarters of 9–11pt new-business contribution to organic growth
(Q2 2026 call) is the observable evidence the machine works. The 10-K itself
names the counter-mechanism: technology-enabled risk-transfer that
"eliminates the traditional broker-client relationship" (Item 1A).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue 2021→TTM $12.19B→$17.58B
(+44%); op income $2.11B→$5.01B (2021 depressed by the WTW breakup fee; on
the clean 2022 base both legs +41%). Margins holding ~28.5% through a $13B
acquisition.

## 4. Valuation

Reverse DCF, levered FCF paired with market cap ($72.80B, `marketcap` hover,
2026-08-18). Base FCF = TTM `fcf` $3.248B (ncfo $3.531B + capex −$0.283B)
**minus SBC $370M** (comp, not owner cash) = **$2.878B**. No net-debt input
(levered flow). No minority interest. Cash tax ~21.7% ≈ marginal — no NOL
flattery. Not charged for M&A: the 5% growth path is the organic (MSD
revenue + margin) case, so tuck-in spend is not double-counted against it.

Hurdle: rf 4.74% + beta 0.8 × ERP 4.28% = **8.16%** (Damodaran home page,
ERP as of Aug 1 2026). Reported beta is 0.68 — below the 0.8–1.2 stable
band, so floored per the anchors rule; at 0.68 the hurdle is 7.65% and the
conservative run still misses (−62bp) while the generous one squeaks by
(+23bp). The ownership call does not hinge on the flooring: a 7.0–7.9%
implied return sits at or below the ~7.8% US median cost of capital either
way, and a low implied return on optimistic assumptions is a bad bet by
construction.
