## 2. Business

**Created:** GLP-1 receptor agonists that produce 15–21% weight loss and
glycaemic control on a weekly injection or a daily pill, plus insulins and
rare-disease biologics. The customer surplus is medical and visible:
41.6M people with diabetes and 4.9M with obesity treated (Q2 announcement);
Wegovy pill hit 5M US prescriptions in under seven months — "the
strongest-ever GLP-1 volume launch in the US" (company). The global branded
obesity market doubled by volume in 2025 (20-F) and management puts GLP-1
category growth "close to 40%" (Q2 call, primary-transcribed).

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms, and the split between them is the
whole story. (1) **US insured channel** — gross-to-net is the business: 2025
gross sales DKK 729B became DKK 309B net, with US rebates, discounts and
returns of DKK 395B equal to *70% of US gross sales* (69% in 2024, 74% in
2023; Annual Report note 2.1). Managed Care/Medicare rebates alone were DKK
247B. (2) **US self-pay** — NovoCare Pharmacy, telehealth partners (Ro,
WeightWatchers, LifeMD, Hims & Hers pending) and TrumpRx at $149–$349 per
month; ~35% of US Wegovy volume now, up from 10–15% a year ago (call), and
~120K of ~310K weekly injectable scripts (announcement). Lower absolute
price, but no PBM rebate — net price per script is closer to list than the
insured channel's. (3) **International Operations** — 48.7% of H1 2026
adjusted sales, growing 10% CER in Q2 with obesity +37% (EUCAN +52%),
tender/reference-priced, and where semaglutide's compound patent has begun
expiring (Canada, Brazil, China, India in 2026). (4) **Rare disease** —
DKK 19B TTM, roughly breakeven at the operating line (segment metrics).
Ozempic is still the largest product (DKK 127B in 2025); Wegovy injectable
DKK 79B; Wegovy pill DKK 5.5B in H1 2026.

**Protected:** three mechanisms, unevenly strong. *Molecule IP*: the US
semaglutide compound patent runs to 2032 (20-F), and every ANDA filer in
the US has settled on confidential terms; but the same patent has expired
or expires in 2026 across markets holding "33% of the world's adults with
obesity" (IQVIA blog, low-confidence), and Sandoz launched generic
Ozempic in Brazil and expects the Canadian market to form in Q3 2026.
*Manufacturing scale*: DKK 232B of net PP&E, three ex-Catalent fill-finish
sites bought for $11.7B in 2024, DKK 91B of contract-manufacturer purchase
obligations (Annual Report note 5.2) — the capacity that let the pill
launch at 265K scripts/week and that management says is at "very low
utilization", i.e. it can flood the channel. *Brand and formulary
position*: Wegovy pill "on par with injectable Wegovy" on commercial
formularies; the three largest PBMs added Wegovy HD; CVS Caremark will
re-add rivals from 1 Oct 2026. What the moat demonstrably does **not**
protect is price: US Wegovy injectable −22% CER on higher volume is the
measured fact, and the 20-F itself says "pricing pressure is expected to
continue in the future".

**Control:** dual-class. Novo Holdings A/S (100% owned by the Novo Nordisk
Foundation) holds all 1,074.9M A shares (76.0% of votes) plus 177.6M B
shares (1.3%), for ~77.3% of votes on ~28.3% of capital; the A shares
"cannot be sold or be subject to any disposition so long as the Foundation
exists" (20-F Item 7). An unsolicited takeover and any activist path are
foreclosed; the Foundation replaced the chair and CEO itself in 2025
(EGM 14 Nov 2025; Doustdar succeeded Jørgensen 7 Aug 2025). ADRs are 15.3%
of B shares. Insiders hold 0.02%.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive over five years, flat since
2024.** Revenue 2021→TTM DKK 140.8B → 329.4B (2.34×); operating income
59.2B → 161.4B reported (2.73×), or ~144B adjusted for the DKK 26.8B 340B
reversal and DKK 9.9B of impairments (2.44×). But 2024→2025 operating
income grew 1% on revenue +6%, and H1 2026 adjusted operating profit grew
2% CER on adjusted sales +2% — the leverage has run out where it matters,
because the incremental revenue is arriving at a lower net price. Not
loss-making; not heavily levered (net debt/EBITDA 0.8×, interest cover
>1,500×).
