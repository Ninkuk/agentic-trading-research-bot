the company; there is a price problem, and it is the same one as in July with

## 2. Business

Unchanged from `research/MYRG-2026-07-29.md` §2 in mechanism; deltas below.

**Created:** builds and maintains electrical infrastructure — transmission,
distribution and substations (T&D, 44% of Q3 revenue, down from 53% a year ago
as C&I grew faster) and complex commercial/industrial electrical work: data
centres, water/wastewater, transit, hospitals, warehousing (C&I, 56%).
Customers are utilities and large owners who need scarce skilled union labour,
safety records and schedule certainty; Valley Electric (Pacific Northwest)
and Comet Electric (Southern California) added since July 1.

**Captured:** three contract shapes. Q3 mix (10-Q): fixed price 64% of
revenue (C&I 90%, T&D 31%), unit price 19%, T&E 17%. MSAs are ~65% of T&D
revenue (70% in Q1). Margin capture is execution alpha — Q3 gross margin 13.6%
vs 11.8%, "better-than-anticipated productivity and favorable job closeouts,"
with +0.4pt from net changes in estimated gross profit. Capital intensity is
rising: capex guided to 3% of revenue in 2026 and "low to mid 3%" in 2027 (CFO),
versus 2.6% LTM.

**Protected:** still thin, and management's own words have not changed —
labour tightness is not translating into pricing power, margin comes from
contract-term selection and prefab. Shared with Quanta, EMCOR, Primoris, IES.
One new mechanism worth naming: customers are now pre-committing labour and
material "out into 2029, 2030" via Part A/B contracts (CEO) — capacity
reservation, which is a mild switching cost while labour stays short and
evaporates when it does not.

**Control:** one class of common stock, 15,570,008 shares; preferred
authorised, none issued (10-Q cover and balance sheet). Insiders 1.65%; 5%
holders are BlackRock (2.51M, ~16%), Vanguard (1.26M) and Wellington (1.02M)
per the 2026 proxy. No controller; nothing forecloses a takeover or an
activist. Null answer, written.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive and steepening over the last 18
months; flat-to-negative over the full five years.**
