## 2. Business

**Created:** industrial identification and workplace safety — durable labels,
wire/cable ID, portable and benchtop printers, safety signage, lockout/tagout,
healthcare wristbands. Products are mission-critical and compliance-driven but
a trivial share of the customer's spend: a mislabeled wire in a data center or
an OSHA violation costs orders of magnitude more than the label. Customers pay
for reliability in harsh environments (materials science) and for labor
saving over improvised alternatives ("picking up a Sharpie" — CEO, Q3 call).
PSS adds enterprise mobility: rugged mobile computers, barcode scanners,
workflow software for warehouse/logistics/retail frontline workers.

**Captured:** three distinct mechanisms. (1) Printer placements seed a
consumables annuity — proprietary label stock and ribbons follow every unit
(placements +7.8% in Q3 precisely because "the consumable revenue will
follow" — CFO). (2) Direct pricing power on spec'd-in engineered products —
management says openly it could push gross margin above 52-53% and chooses
volume instead. (3) Post-PSS, service contracts and software (SwiftDecoder,
voice, operational intelligence) — ~4M service contracts came with PSS.

**Protected:** no single wide moat; a bundle of narrow ones. Materials science
in adhesives/substrates for extreme environments (hard to replicate, low
incentive for a generalist), an installed base whose consumables and workflows
are spec'd into compliance procedures (switching cost is requalification, not
price), global distribution across fragmented industrial niches too small to
attract a focused attacker, and ~4,000 patents at PSS. The honest caveat: in
PSS's market the moat question runs the other way — Zebra is the scale player
and PSS the share loser; Brady is buying INTO a competitor's moat shadow.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** FY21→TTM revenue +41.7%
($1,144.7M→$1,621.9M) while operating income +70.9% ($170.3M→$291.1M);
operating margin 14.9%→17.9% (income-statement route, fiscal years ending
July 31: FY21 170.3/1,144.7; FY22 193.0/1,302.1; FY23 225.2/1,331.9; FY24
243.4/1,341.4; FY25 259.5/1,513.6; TTM 291.1/1,621.9).
