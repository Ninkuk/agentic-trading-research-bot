## 2. Business

**Created:** CML patients whose disease resists, or who cannot tolerate,
their current ATP-competitive TKI (imatinib, dasatinib, nilotinib,
bosutinib) need another agent that controls the disease and is tolerable over
years of daily dosing. Relcobatinib binds the ABL myristoyl pocket (the
allosteric "STAMP" site asciminib also uses), not the ATP site. That should
spare off-target kinases and, the company argues, improve tolerability. The
company reports a "favorable safety and tolerability profile" across 161
patients, median 35 weeks on drug, with no adverse-event table in the release
(8-K, 2026-08-05). I cannot evaluate the selectivity claim or what the
missing AE table hides. UNKNOWN.

**Captured:** nothing today; there is no revenue. Future capture would be:
- US list-price sales of a branded oral oncology drug taken chronically.
  CML is a long-duration, high-persistence, high-price market, and that is
  where the value sits.
- Ex-US licensing or a buyout. No partner is disclosed.
- No second clinical asset was found in this run's sources. The company's
  materials are all about relcobatinib (low-confidence third-party summary
  calls it "almost all of its value"). An earlier HER2 program, ELVN-002,
  was not traced this run (NOT OBTAINED, 10-K Item 1).

**Protected:** composition-of-matter patents on relcobatinib (term not
checked, NOT OBTAINED, 10-K Item 1). Patents do not stop the real threat,
which is rival molecules at the same site: asciminib, already approved
