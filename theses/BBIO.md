## 2. Business

**Created:** BridgeBio sells oral, small-molecule drugs for genetically
defined diseases. The value product today is Attruby (acoramidis), a
transthyretin stabilizer for ATTR cardiomyopathy — a progressive, fatal
heart-failure disease of the elderly. The patient gets slowed disease
progression and fewer cardiovascular hospitalizations; the prescriber's
reason to choose it over Pfizer's incumbent Vyndamax (tafamidis) is a
claimed near-complete stabilization and, per BridgeBio, faster event
separation and a kidney-protective signal (Q2 2026 call). Three more oral
drugs for rare diseases (LGMD2I, ADH1/hypoparathyroidism, achondroplasia)
are filed; for achondroplasia the pitch is a daily pill against injections
(35–40% of eligible families cite injection aversion, per management).

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms. (i) US net product sales of Attruby
through specialty pharmacy — $222.4M in Q2 2026 at a 94% gross margin
(COGS $10.5M), launched below tafamidis list. (ii) Ex-US royalties on
Beyonttra (Bayer in the EU, Alexion in Japan) — $15.4M in Q2 2026. (iii)
The reverse flow: a deferred royalty obligation ($908.7M carrying) means a
slice of acoramidis sales is already sold to royalty investors, so
BridgeBio captures less of each dollar than the income statement's revenue
line suggests. (iv) Option value on GondolaBio, an off-balance-sheet
spinout of 17 early programs.

**Protected:** patents on acoramidis, orphan exclusivity on the rare-disease
filings, and — the load-bearing part — clinical differentiation versus
tafamidis. The mechanism of protection against a *cheaper* rival (generic
tafamidis from June 2031 in the US, earlier in Europe where Pfizer's
tafamidis patent was revoked) is only that prescribers believe acoramidis is
better. Management's own argument is that "clinical differentiation will
carry the day" and that the specialty channel economics disfavour generics.
Raymond James downgraded the stock on 2026-05-26 on exactly the opposite
view. No head-to-head trial settles it. This is an **unassessed moat**, not
an absent one: the domain science is beyond what I can evaluate.

**Control:** single class of common stock; insiders hold 3.94% and
institutions 87.4% (stocks.db). No controlling holder visible. The
2026-07-01 Series A preferred (Sixth Street $800M lead, HealthCare Royalty
$133.9M) is a new senior claim: 7% cumulative dividend payable in kind or
cash, perpetual, no holder redemption, convertible at $137.79 (rising to
$153.10 after year 5). Any consent or board rights attached to it were not
read (§6).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** The loss shrinks as revenue
scales, but it is still a loss in every year on record.
