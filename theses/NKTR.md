## 2. Business

**Created:** an injectable that tries to re-balance the immune system rather
than block one cytokine. Rezpeg is a PEG-conjugated IL-2 biased toward the
high-affinity receptor on regulatory T cells. Management's framing is that it
"acts upstream to restore immune balance rather than blocking single
downstream inflammatory pathways" (Q2 call). The patient surplus, if real, is
durability: a course that keeps skin or hair clear after dosing stops.
- **AD (Phase 1b):** 71% kept EASI-75 and 80% kept vIGA response 9 months
  off drug after 12 weeks of dosing (2026-10-01 call). Small n.
- **AA:** 63% (5/8 observed per the release; management cites 21 evaluable)
  of week-52 SALT≤20 responders held response 6 months off drug, vs ~20% for
  baricitinib on management's comparison (2026-10-01 call; cross-trial).
- **Safety:** no infection or conjunctivitis signal (Q2 call). Injection-site
  reactions occurred in 69.7% of treated patients (8-K 2025-06-24).

**Captured:** nothing from rezpeg today. Revenue is legacy (TTM $54.6M,
falling; composition NOT OBTAINED, unrelated to rezpeg). Capture would come
through:
(a) a US/EU AD label, BLA planned 2029 (Q2 call);
(b) an AA label, data H2 2029;
(c) Type 1 diabetes, a TrialNet-funded Phase 2 with first cohort data in 2027;
(d) the TNFR2 research programs NKTR-0165/0166.
Nektar holds full rights after Lilly returned them. Whether any economics flow
back to Lilly is NOT OBTAINED. Management disclosed no partnering intent (Q2
call). Launch would be self-funded or partnered, after more capital.
Separately, a jury awarded $90M plus interest against Lilly for breach of the
implied covenant on the old rezpeg collaboration (2026-09-24, N.D. Cal.;
Nektar sought ~$1B). Lilly says "the correct amount is zero" and will seek
reversal (BioSpace via search; low-confidence).

**Protected:** composition-of-matter IP on a PEG-IL-2 conjugate (patent
schedule NOT OBTAINED) plus a mechanism lead. The Treg-IL-2 class has a poor
record: rezpeg itself missed in lupus under Lilly, and other IL-2 muteins were
dropped (low-confidence recollection, not fetched). That thins the field
rather than crowding it. AD is crowded by outcome, not mechanism: dupilumab,
lebrikizumab, tralokinumab, OX40s, JAKs, and a wave of oral STAT6 programs.
Protection rests on durability data that exist only in small subsets. Whether
the biology confers a lasting edge is UNKNOWN to this author.
