## 2. Business

**Created:** Two Hong Kong subsidiaries — Joint Cross Border Logistics
Company Limited (OTC pharma cross-border e-commerce supply-chain services:
customs clearance, drug enlisting, warehousing, logistics) and V-Alliance
Technology Supplies Limited (OTC pharma cross-border procurement and
distribution). The customer is a mainland-China e-commerce logistics
partner, merchant, or pharmaceutical distributor who needs OTC drugs moved
from Hong Kong into mainland channels and cleared through customs. What they
receive is regulatory paperwork and physical movement. SIC 5122 (drugs,
druggists' sundries — wholesale).

**Captured:** A ~10–12% gross spread on goods bought and resold, plus fees
on the logistics leg. The split is not disclosed at segment level in
anything reachable. The spread is thin and compressing: gross margin 11.89%
(FY2025) → **10.32%** (FY2026). Revenue per employee is $1.22M across 11
people — the signature of a pass-through trading book, not a service
business.

**Protected:** Nothing identified, and the silence is the answer. Hong Kong
cross-border pharma requires licensing, which is a real but low barrier; it
did not stop the gross margin compressing or revenue falling a third in one
year. A customs-brokerage and distribution relationship is re-tenderable.
The December 2025 rename to "Cellyan Biotechnology" asserts a moat the
income statement does not carry: opex is **$2,395,075 of SG&A and $0 of
R&D**. A biotechnology company with zero research spend is a distributor
with a new name; stockanalysis still classifies it Medical Distribution.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue $12.56M (FY2023) →
~$20.31M (FY2025) → $13.43M (FY2026), against operating income of +$190,250
(FY2025) → −$1,004,909 (FY2026). Revenue grew 61.8% FY23→FY25 and never
produced an operating margin above **0.94%**; it then fell 33.9% and the
margin went to −7.48%. This business has never had operating leverage
because it has never had operating margin. (FY2024 not directly sourced —
the trajectory rests on three points.)

and only H2 turned loss-making. It fails on the level rather than the trend:
**0.94% operating margin in the best year** is not a business with pricing
power having a bad half.
