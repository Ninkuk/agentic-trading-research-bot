## 2. Business

**Created:** Amgen makes biologic medicines for conditions where the
alternative is worse disease, and sells the molecule plus the evidence that it
works. A cardiologist prescribes Repatha not because it lowers LDL-C — several
things do — but because Amgen ran the outcomes trial showing it prevents heart
attacks, and that trial is what payers reimburse against. The same pattern
repeats across the book: IMDELLTRA has a randomised overall-survival benefit
in second-line small cell lung cancer, EVENITY has a bone-builder label,
TEZSPIRE has severe uncontrolled asthma without a phenotype restriction. In
the biosimilar business the created value is the opposite — the identical
molecule at a lower price, with Amgen's manufacturing reliability standing in
for the innovator's brand.

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms, not one.
(i) *Patent-protected branded biologics* — Repatha, EVENITY, TEZSPIRE,
IMDELLTRA, UPLIZNA, BLINCYTO: list price less rebates, negotiated per payer,
for the life of exclusivity. This is where the 71.9% gross margin comes from.
(ii) *Rare-disease pricing* — the Horizon franchise (TEPEZZA $1,066M H1,
KRYSTEXXA $655M, UPLIZNA $597M): very high per-patient prices to small
populations, sold through a specialty channel Amgen bought rather than built.
(iii) *Legacy annuities in decline* — ENBREL, Otezla, Prolia, XGEVA, Aranesp:
still $4.0B of H1 revenue, but three of the five are shrinking double digits
and ENBREL and Otezla are inside the IRA price-setting queue. These are being
harvested, not grown.
(iv) *Biosimilars* — $855M in Q2, +29%, PAVBLU +121%; more than $15B
cumulative since 2018. Amgen is simultaneously the incumbent losing Prolia to
biosimilars and the challenger taking Eylea, Humira and Stelara revenue from
others. That internal hedge is the least-discussed part of the model and it
is the reason the cliff has been absorbable.

**Protected:** three mechanisms of decreasing strength. Biologics
manufacturing is the strongest — a monoclonal antibody is defined by its
process, and a competitor must build the plant, pass the regulator and
demonstrate comparability, which is why biosimilar entry takes years after
patent expiry rather than the ninety days a small molecule takes. Second, the
outcomes-trial evidence base: Repatha's cardiovascular outcomes data is a
sunk cost a new entrant must repeat, and Amgen's own management argues on the
Q2 call that an oral PCSK9 with food restrictions and no outcomes trial is a
market expander rather than a substitute — a claim in their own interest,
recorded as theirs. Third, and weakest, patents: Prolia and XGEVA just
demonstrated exactly how that protection ends, and nothing in the model
stopped it. The honest summary is that Amgen's moat is a *manufacturing and
evidence* moat with a *known expiry schedule*, and the business is a treadmill
that must keep replacing what runs off. It has, for a decade. That is a
record, not a guarantee.

**Control:** one class of common stock, $0.0001 par, one share one vote, on
Nasdaq. No controlling holder: insiders own 0.169% and institutions 83.87%
(`stocks.db` `v_latest`); float is 539.16M of 540.63M shares outstanding
(99.7%). The structure forecloses nothing — an unsolicited bid or an activist
campaign is structurally available. Delaware incorporation supplies the usual
statutory defences and nothing more.

## Kill-thesis record

**FLAWED** — conditions=5 (3 probable, 2 plausible), refuted=1, unknown=0,
pending=2, not_obtained=0. Object of the attack is the **bull case**: that
owning AMGN at $377.33 beats SPY over 63 trading days. The PASS in §1 is what
the refuted bull condition leaves behind, not a thesis with claims of its own.

**Per-condition adjudication**

1. *Growth drivers keep outrunning the biosimilar cliff* — **SURVIVED
   (probable)**, weakened. The attack: the cliff annualizes while the offset
   does not. At-risk H1 revenue — Prolia $1,486M, XGEVA $763M, ENBREL $900M
   (IRA-set from 2026), Otezla $922M (from 2027), Aranesp $663M — is $4.73B,
   about a quarter of the company, and every line of it is falling or
   price-set. Management's own arithmetic concedes the deceleration: H1 2026
   total revenue was $18,672M against $17,328M (+7.8%), while the raised
   FY26 guide of $38.2–39.4B implies H2 of $19.5–20.7B against H2 2025's
   $19.42B — **+0.5% to +6.7%, midpoint +3.6%**. The condition still stands
   because even the low end of the company's own guide has the drivers
   outrunning the cliff, and because Amgen has beaten consensus in eight
   consecutive quarters (Q4'24–Q2'26, +4.5% to +14.0%) and raised rather than
   cut. But it survives with the margin more than halved, and a further halving
   turns it over. Closest attack on this condition.
2. *Non-GAAP operating margin holds near the guided 45–46% of product
   sales* — **SURVIVED (probable)**. Attacked on the direction of travel:
   the guide is *below* the 48% Q2 print, and management flagged sequential
   opex increases including a $100M Q3 business-development upfront and
   high-single-digit non-GAAP R&D growth carrying nine phase 3 MariTide
   trials — an uncapped commitment against an asset with no public phase 3
   efficacy data. Nothing today stands against the guide itself, and Q2
   printed above it. Stands.
3. *The IRS Tax Court decision does not impose a payment materially greater
   than accrued* — **PENDING 2027-02-12**. Decided by the decision, expected
   "no earlier than late 2026 or early 2027" per the Q2 10-Q, with the
   FY2026 10-K as the backstop that must disclose it. Nothing today stands
   against it — Amgen calls the IRS positions "without merit" and has not
   accrued to them — so it is not REFUTED. Nor is it credited: the same note
   says the outcome "may result in payments substantially greater than
   amounts accrued," the exposure is $3.6B + $5.1B + $2.0B of penalties less
   up to $3.1B of repatriation credits *plus a decade of interest*, and the

   Uncertainty is written as PENDING, never as SURVIVED.
4. *Terminal cash flows survive US drug-pricing policy at ~breakeven
   inflation* — **PENDING 2027-02-02**. FY2027 guidance at the Q4 2026 print
   is the first document that must embed Otezla's IRA-set price and any MFN
   or GENEROUS concession. Today's evidence neither confirms nor refutes:
   the 10-Q's own word is "uncertain." Recorded as pending, not credited.
