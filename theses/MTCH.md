## 2. Business

**Created:** a single is shown other singles who are likely to talk back,
ranked by a recommendation system, with identity verification (Face Check,
−60% exposure to bots and bad actors per management) and lower-pressure
formats (Double Date, Events, Modes). The customer surplus is liquidity: in
any given city the app with the most active people of the sex you want is the
only one worth opening, and Tinder is the most-downloaded dating app in 166
countries. Hinge sells the same liquidity to people who want a relationship
rather than a night out ("designed to be deleted"); E&E brands (Match,
Meetic, OkCupid, Plenty of Fish, Pairs in Japan, Azar one-to-one video chat,
BLK, Chispa, The League, OurTime) serve older, affinity and regional
audiences.

**Captured:** three distinct mechanisms, all per-payer. (1) Tiered
subscriptions — Tinder Plus/Gold/Platinum, Hinge+ and HingeX, with a fourth
Hinge tier at a lower price point testing in late 2026. (2) À la carte
"liquidity purchases" — Boosts, Super Likes, Roses — which let one user pay to
be seen by more of the other side. (3) Indirect/advertising revenue, now only
$13M a quarter (−28%), ~1.5% of the total. Q2 2026: 13.25M payers × $21.13
RPP = $840M direct revenue; Tinder 8.5M payers at $17.90, Hinge 2.0M at
$33.11, E&E 2.7M at $22.24. The toll the platforms take sits inside this:
Apple and Google charge up to 30%/15% on in-app subscriptions, and
management's "alternative payment savings" (0% US commission on link-outs
since the Epic injunction, Google's User Choice Billing partnership through
Q1 2027) cut cost of revenue 16% in Q2 and are worth ~$130M in 2026.

**Protected:** the mechanism is local two-sided liquidity plus brand recall,
not technology — the 10-K itself says the category has "low switching costs
and a consistent stream of new services". That moat is real (Bumble's
payers −16% while Tinder's are −5%: the weaker network loses first) but it
is narrow: Tinder lost product-market fit with under-30s while still being
#1, which is what a liquidity moat looks like when the product stagnates for
a decade. The honest statement is a durable but eroding position whose
protection has to be re-earned through product velocity, which is exactly
what management now sells.

**Control:** one class of common, one vote per share, no controlling holder
(10-K). Vanguard 13.7%, BlackRock 11.9%, State Street 6.9% (proxy, April
2026); Starboard Value filed a 13D in July 2024 at 7.6% and amended on
2026-05-06 to 4.6% (10.8M shares, partly via forward contracts) — the
activist is trimming, not pressing. Insiders hold 0.63%. Nothing structural
forecloses a takeover or a proxy fight; shareholders used the vote on
2026-06-16 to reject say-on-pay (85.3M for, 114.0M against; CEO total pay
$14.4M for 2025) and cast 34.7M votes against director McDaniel, who then
filed a Form 144 for 3,531 shares on 2026-09-02.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): flat on revenue 2021–2024, positive and
cost-led since 2025.** Revenue has barely moved since 2023; the margin is
being rebuilt by headcount, legal, and app-fee savings, not by growth.

gross 2.7×, all fixed-rate — not the Phase 0 kill, but book equity is

## 3. Threads pulled

- **Tinder's turnaround is real in usage and not yet in payers.** DAU −4% YoY
  in Q2 (best in 10 quarters), −2.5% July, ~−1% August, better in early
  September; Sparks (six-way conversations) −4% Q2 → +2% August; Sparks
  coverage +2% → +6%; matches +14%. MAU −7% in Q2, payers −5%, RPP +4%, and
  Tinder direct revenue −1% (−2% FXN) with $8M of deliberate "user give-back"
  tests. Management's bridge: DAU leads MAU (existing users engage more) and
  reconsideration from lapsed/never users is what Events (75 cities by
  year-end) must deliver; targets are flat MAU and growing payers by Q4 2027
  and Tinder 2027 revenue above 2026. Give-back budget cut from $60M to
  $30–40M because the rebrand had no revenue cost. The Phase 2 reconciliation
  since the call: the two September conferences raised the usage numbers but
  gave no new payer figure; the 10-Q and 8-K stream since 2026-08-05 carries
  no business update, so the Q3 print is the next datum.
- **The in-app-fee thread is the single biggest swing to FCF.** 2026 savings
  ~$130M (CFO, Q2 call; +$20M vs plan) rest on Apple's 0% US link-out
  commission under the Epic injunction. In August 2026 Apple asked the
  remand court for a tiered commission of up to 15% (5–10% for programs);
  Epic argues the Ninth Circuit's "necessary costs" standard implies 0%; the
  Supreme Court granted review of the contempt standard on 2026-06-30 but
  declined to pause remand. Google's new global fee structure gives ~$5M of
  2027 benefit; Apple's EU fees rose 3–4 points in August with a 15% link-out
  option. If the court adopts 15%, roughly half the US savings reverse — a
  ~$65M haircut to base FCF, run as scenario E in §4.

  guideline change banned random/anonymous chat; Azar ($155.8M 2025 direct
  revenue, 76% through the App Store) was removed 2026-02-22, redesigned and
  reinstated 2026-04-06 at a lower base; $25.2M trade-name impairment in Q1;
  a $15M-per-quarter revenue hole through at least Q3. E&E direct revenue

## 5. Falsifiers

For the pass (flip toward buy):

- **Shift —** price at or below ~$39 (scenario E clears the hurdle) with the
  bull conditions intact, or the base path re-cleared by a lower rf.
- **Shift —** the Epic remand court sets the US link-out commission at 0–5%,
  removing scenario E; revalue on scenario B/C alone.
- **Break (of the pass) —** Tinder payers turn positive year-over-year before
  Q4 2027 while RPP keeps rising; the base case becomes the conservative
  case.

For an owner (sell):

- **Break —** Tinder DAU does not print positive YoY at the Q3 or Q4 2026
  prints, or MAU decline widens again — the product-velocity story is over.
- **Shift —** the court sets ≥15% on US link-outs; move to scenario E.
- **Shift —** capital return drops below ~80% of FCF for M&A (a Sniffies
  buyout plus more "selective M&A"); the FCF-per-share compounding leg
  weakens.
- **Shift —** E&E direct revenue still falling mid-teens in Q2 2027 after
  Azar laps the removal.
