## 2. Business

**Created:** until 2026-10-02, allogeneic ("off-the-shelf") CAR-T cell
therapies edited with Caribou's chRDNA CRISPR platform: vispa-cel (anti-CD19,
r/r B-cell NHL) and CB-011 (anti-BCMA, r/r myeloma). The customer promise was
autologous-like efficacy without the weeks-long manufacture from the
patient's own cells. Management's last pre-announcement framing (H.C.
Wainwright conference, 2026-06-30): vispa-cel median PFS 17.1 months vs
14.8–14.9 for published autologous comparators; CB-011 BCMA-naive 92% ORR,
83% CR/sCR. Both programs are now discontinued. No product was ever sold.

**Captured:** almost nothing, and through one mechanism: licensing. Caribou
sublicenses foundational CRISPR-Cas9 IP (licensed from the University of
California and University of Vienna) to Intellia (human therapeutics field),
Corteva/Pioneer (agriculture, industrial) and others (10-K FY2025).
Licensing and other revenue was $3.9M in H1 2026, $11.2M in FY2025, $34.5M
in FY2023 (a lumpy milestone year). The Pfizer information-rights payments
expired 2026-06-29 (10-Q Q2 2026). Nothing else is captured.

**Protected:** UNKNOWN for the science and now moot. The chRDNA patents and
the clinical data packages are the only assets anything could protect, and
their value is whatever Wedbush can sell them for. As a going concern there
is no moat to assess, because there is no business left.

**Control:** one class of common stock, one vote per share, no controlling
holder (10-K FY2025 cover). Insiders own 4.0%, institutions 49.2%
(stockanalysis). Nothing structural stops an outside bid for the shell; a
dispersed register is what lets a cash-shell bidder or a reverse-merger
partner approach the board.
