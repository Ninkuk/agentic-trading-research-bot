## 2. Business

**Created:** Three businesses under one balance sheet since the Windstream
merger closed 2025-08-01.
- **Kinetic:** residential and SMB broadband in mostly rural/exurban
  footprints. Cable is present in fewer than 50% of its markets (Citi TMT
  2026-09-08). Customers get symmetric fiber where the alternative is DSL,
  fixed wireless or Starlink. That is a real surplus where cable is absent.
- **Fiber Infrastructure:** the legacy Uniti lessor. Dark fiber, waves and
  anchor builds for carriers, hyperscalers and neoclouds. Q2 bookings mix:
  neoclouds 20%, superscalers 18%, hyperscalers 10%.
- **Uniti Solutions:** legacy copper/enterprise voice and data, declining.

**Captured:**
- Kinetic charges monthly subscription ARPU, with consumer fiber revenue
  guided at $635–655M for 2026.
- FI earns long-dated lease revenue: hyperscaler dark fiber on 10–20-year
  IRUs, lit/wave deals on 3–5-year terms. It also collects upfront NRCs
  that fund about 70% of anchor builds, plus lease-up on the same route
  (blended anchor-plus-lease-up cash yields 37%).
- Solutions sells legacy enterprise contracts with no growth.

2026 guide: Kinetic $2.145B revenue / $905M contribution, FI $1.0B / $575M,
Solutions $700M / $320M (Q2 2026 call).

**Protected:** In non-cable Kinetic markets, the moat is a first-fiber
overbuild. A second fiber entrant faces the same per-passing economics
against a 29%-penetrated incumbent, and that is a genuine but local
deterrent. It does not stop fixed wireless or LEO. Management says it is
"losing a little bit more to both [LEO and FWA] than … to cable or to fiber
overbuilders" (Citi TMT). In FI, the moat is route density: "80% of our
hyperscaler builds have actually been … connected to our existing
infrastructure." That is a real cost advantage on adjacent builds. Neither
moat protects the equity from the capital structure, and this run cannot
assess either one's depth (no route-level economics disclosed).

**Control:** The insider holding is 1.94% and institutions hold 69.9%
(`stocks.db`). This run found no dual-class structure. Former Windstream
holders, notably Elliott, received a large block in the merger. Elliott's
current stake and any board/nomination rights are NOT OBTAINED (2026
proxy). Series A preferred ranks ahead of common ($15.7M quarterly
dividend). No common dividend is paid.
