## 2. Business

**Created.** A property manager running 1,600–40,000 units replaces 6–14
disconnected systems (accounting, leasing, screening, maintenance, payments,
insurance) with one platform that is also the trust-accounting system of
record. Customer stories in the Q2'26 call: RST (6 systems), Advanced
Management (9), Northpoint (14) consolidated onto AppFolio. 22,751 customers,
9.6M units (Q2'26).

**Captured — three distinct mechanisms, not one.**
1. *Subscription* (~21% of revenue, $60M/q, +14%): per-unit tiered SaaS
   (Core/Plus/Max); premium tiers now ~1 in 3 units, up from 1 in 4.
2. *Value Added Services* (~78%, $219M/q, +22%): per-transaction take —
   electronic payments (percentage of transaction, booked **gross** of
   interchange; interchange sits in cost of revenue), tenant screening
   (per-use), FolioGuard risk mitigation/insurance, plus newer per-use AI
   "Performers" and move-in services (LiveEasy, Second Nature partnership).
3. *Other* (~2%): one-time/legacy, incl. a one-time Q4'25 Stack partnership
   payment (2025 Other revenue +108% — non-recurring, don't extrapolate).
   Blended: ~$108 of revenue per unit per year and rising ~11%/yr.

**Protected.** Switching costs as a mechanism: the platform holds the trust
accounts, resident/owner data, bank and insurance integrations, and
compliance workflows; migrating is a business-risk event for the customer,
not a software swap. Scale in a fragmented SMB segment (enterprise
multifamily belongs to Yardi/RealPage/Entrata; AppFolio leads below it).
What it is *not* protected by: patents (10-K relies on trade secrets),
cross-customer network effects (weak), or entry cost — the 10-K's own words:
"the barrier to entry for competition in one or more areas we serve may be
low."

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive** ("strongly positive since
2023"). Revenue 359M → 472M → 620M → 794M → 951M → 1,041M TTM (2021→TTM);
operating margin −3.3% → −15.4% → +0.2% → +17.1% → +16.1% → +17.5%. FCF:
27M → 19M → 51M → 186M → 239M → 270M TTM.

## 5. Falsifiers

For an owner (sell) — what would kill a buy:

- Units under management growth < 5% YoY, or customer count flat — the net
  numbers are the only observable retention proxy AppFolio provides.
- VAS revenue per unit declining year-over-year (ex the Q2/Q3 seasonal shape
  disclosed in the 10-K).
- GAAP operating margin contracting while revenue growth is below 15% —
  operating leverage stalling at the wrong time.
- Interchange/credit-card-competition legislation (e.g. CCCA-style) reaching
  a floor vote — direct gross-revenue risk on the payments majority.

For the pass (flip toward buy):

- An NRR/cohort disclosure, or two more quarters of
  units ≥ 8% + VAS/unit ≥ 10% + GAAP margin expansion, or price back near
  the June trough (~$145, where the base case implies ~12%) flips this
  toward buy.
