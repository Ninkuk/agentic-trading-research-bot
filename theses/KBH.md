## 2. Business

**Created:** a new single-family home the buyer configures — lot, floor
plan, and studio options — at a transparent base price with limited or no
incentives, delivered in ~99 days from start (Q3; 23 days faster than a
year ago). About half of buyers are first-time; the pitch is
personalization plus energy efficiency at a price competitive with area
resale (10-K Item 1; call). The buyer's alternative is the resale market,
which management names as its "largest competitor" (call).

**Captured:** three mechanisms. (i) The home sale itself — housing revenue
$1.29B in Q3 at a $473,000 ASP and a 16.5% GAAP / 16.8% adjusted gross
margin; built-to-order sales carry 300–500bp higher margin than spec
(call, Q&A). (ii) Land — value is created between lot acquisition or option
and delivery; 61,581 lots owned or under contract, 60/40 owned/optioned,
West Coast carrying $3.05B of the $5.67B inventory at FY2025 (release; 10-K
MD&A). (iii) Financial services — a 50% mortgage JV (KBHS Home Loans, with
Guaranteed Rate's GR Alliance) at 85% capture, plus title and insurance:
$7.4M pretax in Q3, $19.6M nine-month (release; 10-K Item 1A).

**Protected:** no moat. Homebuilding is a local, fragmented, capital-heavy
business competing on price against other builders and against resale
inventory. What KB has is process — the built-to-order model with 26% unsold
inventory (41% a year ago) and 9% finished unsold, a top customer-
satisfaction ranking, and long-tenured regional land teams — and a land
pipeline. None of that stops a competitor tomorrow; it changes how much KB
loses in a downturn, not whether it does. That silence is the answer.

**Control:** one class of common, one vote per share, no controlling
holder. BlackRock 14.2%, Vanguard 10.4%, FMR 10.3%, State Street 5.6%;
directors and officers 4.7%, Executive Chairman Mezger 3.5% (DEF 14A,
2026-03-13). Majority voting, annual elections; the NOL rights plan expired
April 2024 (10-K). Nothing forecloses a takeover or an activist. Leadership
transition: Robert McGibney became CEO 2026-03-01, Mezger Executive
Chairman (DEF 14A). Annual incentive is paid on adjusted pretax income with
an asset-efficiency hurdle (DEF 14A) — it rewards profit, not volume, which
is consistent with the pace-for-price behaviour on the call.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Fiscal years end November 30
(stockanalysis annual statements; TTM through Aug 31, 2026):
