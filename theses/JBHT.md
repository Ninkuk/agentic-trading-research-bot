## 2. Business

**Created:** shippers get reliable long-haul and regional capacity without
owning it. Intermodal (JBI, ~50% of revenue) sells door-to-door container
moves — JBHT owns the containers, chassis and drayage trucks, the railroads
(mainly BNSF and NS) pull the line haul — at a discount to a truck (normally
~15%, currently >30% per the Q3 call). Dedicated (DCS, ~26%) runs a private
fleet for a customer under multi-year contracts with ECI/CPI escalators. ICS
(brokerage), JBT (truckload) and Final Mile fill the rest. The surplus to the
customer is the truck-vs-rail cost gap plus reliability.

**Captured:** (a) JBI: the spread between what the shipper pays per load and
JBHT's rail purchased-transportation cost plus its own drayage and container
costs — re-priced annually in a bid cycle (10% of volume in Q4, ~30% in each
of Q1–Q3); fuel surcharge passed through weekly with a lag. (b) DCS: a
contracted rate on capital (trucks) and drivers with index escalators, and a
targeted return on invested capital per deal. (c) ICS: brokerage gross margin
(14.3% in Q3). (d) JBT: trailer-pool truckload rate per load.

**Protected:** scale in the domestic 53' container fleet and long-term rail
contracts on both coasts' Class I carriers are hard to replicate (a new
entrant needs rail access agreements and a container fleet); DCS sticks
because switching a private fleet is operationally risky (96% retention). The
protection is real but rents are cyclical: the rail partner sets line-haul
cost, and truck competes on price whenever truckload is loose — 2022–2025
proves the margin is not defended through a freight recession (op income
$1.33B → $0.83B).

**Control:** one share class, one vote, annual board elections. Johnelle Hunt
(co-founder) holds 19.4% (18.3M shares, DEF 14A, Feb 17 2026); her son Bryan
Hunt sits on the board. Not a controller, but a block large enough to make an
unsolicited takeover or activist path impractical without the family.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Over the cycle revenue fell 10%
from the 2022 peak to TTM while operating income fell 28%; in the latest
quarter revenue +20% bought operating income +2%.
