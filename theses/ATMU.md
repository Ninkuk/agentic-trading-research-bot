## 2. Business

**Created:** keeps diesel/natural-gas engines alive. Fuel, lube, air, and
crankcase filtration for commercial trucks, buses, agriculture, mining,
construction — mission-critical consumables at a trivial fraction of engine
cost. NanoNet N3 media and 65 years embedded with Cummins engine programs
mean the filter is designed into the engine platform, and the installed base
then consumes replacement filters for 15–20 years. Since Jan 2026, also
industrial air filtration (Koch Filter: commercial HVAC, data centers,
healthcare).

**Captured:** razor-and-blade. First-fit sales to OEMs (Cummins 18.8% of 2025
net sales, PACCAR 16.x%, Traton >10%) are the low-margin razor that seeds the
installed base; ~85% of Power Solutions revenue is the aftermarket blade,
sold through OEM service channels (~40% of net sales flow through Cummins
channels, 10-K), independent distributors, and its own distribution network.
Pricing +2% realized in H1'26 on flat volumes shows the blade holds price. JV
income (~$8M/qtr) from China and India adds equity-method earnings.

**Protected:** the mechanism is switching cost plus spec-in. A filter is
qualified per engine platform; OEM supply agreements (Cummins first-fit +
aftermarket agreements signed at separation) entrench share for current and
named upcoming platforms; the Fleetguard brand and on-shelf availability
(management: all-time-high delivery metrics through Atmus-controlled
distribution) defend the aftermarket against will-fit competitors. The honest
gap: agreements commit no volumes, and the moat is narrower in industrial
air, where Koch is a smaller player in a fragmented market (10-K: fragmented,
regional players).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue +32% / operating income
+62% since 2021 (income statement); op margin 12.6% → 15.5%, 2021 → TTM.
