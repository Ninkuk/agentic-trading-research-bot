## 2. Business

Unchanged from `research/CVSA-2026-10-13.md` §2 in substance; restated
briefly with this print's deltas.

**Created:** a working adult gets a licensed-profession credential (nursing,
mental-health practice, medicine, veterinary medicine) they can finish while
employed, from schools with licensure and placement records. The CEO's own
framing this quarter: "the binding constraint here is capacity, not
interest" — 93,000 qualified nursing applicants and 30,000 medical
applicants turned away nationally for lack of faculty, clinical sites and
seats (Q1 call; company-cited figures, not independently verified).

**Captured:** four mechanisms — (1) Walden per-credit-hour tuition (Q1
revenue +14.1% on +12.8% heads); (2) Chamberlain per-session tuition over a
largely fixed leased campus base (Q1 revenue +7.4% on +1.3% heads: "better
mix, more credit hours per student, and pricing" — the gap between the two
numbers is the quarter's most important line); (3) medical/veterinary
semester tuition (+10.0% on +3.5% heads, 370bp of EBITDA-margin expansion);
(4) employer contracts — Advocate Health enrolling in Wisconsin and
expanding to three Chicago campuses, SSM Health targets raised. Delta: three
new Chamberlain campuses (Cincinnati, Salt Lake City, Nashville in January)
carry ~$3M a quarter of pre-enrollment cost, guided to rise.

**Protected:** a regulatory and operational barrier — ED-recognized
accreditation, programmatic nursing accreditation, state authorization,
clinical-placement networks, licensure history — that blocks a new
for-profit entrant but not a non-profit expanding its own seats, and that
the regulator holding it can devalue: all five institutions remain on
provisional Title IV certification with heightened cash monitoring and
$202.6M of surety-backed letters of credit, now expiring 2027-09-30, plus
$85.0M of state surety bonds (10-Q Note 13). New this quarter: the CEO put
LLM search on the record as "a real dynamic," claiming ~90% positive
descriptions for Chamberlain and "slightly below that" for Walden, "no
implications for total enrollment" — a management measurement of an
unaudited channel, recorded, not credited.

**Control:** one class of common stock, one share one vote, no controlling
holder; 33,255,241 shares outstanding (2026-10-23). Beneficial owners (DEF
14A, 2026-10-02): BlackRock 15.3%, FMR 10.5%, Vanguard 7.2%, Ariel 5.6%,
Dimensional 5.2%; all directors and officers 2.70%. The 2026-11-11 ballot
carries a 2026 Equity Incentive Plan reserving 2,100,000 shares (6.3% of
shares out; three-year burn rate 1.33%, FY2026 1.85%) and no charter,
bylaw or takeover-defense item. Nothing forecloses an unsolicited takeover
