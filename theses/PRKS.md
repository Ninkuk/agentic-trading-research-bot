## 2. Business

**Created:** a day out: marine-life parks, roller coasters, water parks and
seasonal events (Howl-O-Scream, concerts), mostly in Orlando, Tampa, San
Diego, San Antonio and Williamsburg. The customer comes for regional
convenience and a price well below Disney/Universal. Annual and season passes
turn locals into repeat visitors. Surplus over price is plausible for pass
holders and thin for one-off tourists (low-confidence judgment, no survey
evidence).

**Captured:** four mechanisms. (a) **Admission**: single tickets and paid
passes. Admission per capita fell 1.8% in Q2 2026 on a higher pass mix. The
paid-pass base is down 1% YoY, with deferred revenue at $211.9M current (Q2
call). (b) **In-park spend**: food, merchandise, upcharge experiences. Per
capita rose 5.1% to a record (Q2 call). It carries the P&L while attendance
falls. (c) **Sponsorship and IP partnerships**: guided above $15M in 2026, with
a stated path toward $30M (Q2 call). (d) **Owned real estate**: not captured
today. Management says "multiple, highly credible third parties" are
negotiating to buy "some or most" of it (Q2 call). A sale would turn
owned land into rent, a financing trade rather than new value.

**Protected:** land, permits and animal collections in fixed metro locations
are slow and costly to replicate: a new park takes years and billions. That
protects existence, not pricing. The competitors that matter are already
built next door: Universal's Epic Universe (opened 2025) and Disney, which
promoted $59 tickets on 2026-09-21 (ad-hoc-news citing NY Post,
low-confidence). There is no mechanism that stops a larger neighbour from
out-investing or out-discounting it. Operating income falling on flat
revenue is what that looks like in the numbers.

**Control:** one share class. Hill Path Capital LP beneficially owns
27,205,306 shares, **60.0%** (Schedule 13D/A No. 24, 2026-08-11), and holds
three board seats (Q2 call). The amendment was filed because buybacks shrank
the denominator: Hill Path bought and sold nothing in the prior 60 days, and
the buybacks walked its stake to a majority without a control premium. This
rules out an unsolicited takeover and any activist path. Only Hill Path can
decide a take-private or a real-estate sale.
