## 2. Business

**Created:** CTRL Media (Hong Kong, founded 2014) buys and runs advertising
for mobile-game publishers — mostly PRC-developed games launching in Hong
Kong — across online channels (social, search, KOL/celebrity videos), offline
media (MTR podiums, public venues), Animation-Comic-Game exhibition booths,
and "strategic planning" (localisation projects). The customer gets a local
agency that knows HK game launches. Twenty-eight employees, sixteen in
project execution (20-F Item 6D).

**Captured:** project-based, non-recurring fees (20-F risk factor: "our
business revenue is substantially project-based and non-recurring"). FY26
mix (HK$): online advertising 10.9M (+1.2%), offline/web-banner 14.3M
(−20.5%), strategic planning 0.4M, other 3.1M. Gross margin 21.5%. Four
customers were 43% of FY26 revenue; two customers 42% of receivables. A
related party (Act Media, controlled by shareholder Shum Tsz Cheung) is
simultaneously a supplier (HK$1.14M in FY26) and a customer (HK$1.53M).

**Protected:** no moat. The 20-F itself says the industry "requir[es] few
capital investments"; the assets are relationships and 28 people. Nothing
stops a competitor tomorrow — and the revenue trajectory says several have.

**Control:** since 2026-08-12 two classes — 9,623,356 Class A (1 vote) and
476,667 Class B (5 votes, convertible 1:1, auto-convert on transfer)
redesignated to Wei Jinchan, giving 19.9% of votes; the company can create
more Class B only with unanimous Class B consent. As a BVI FPI it follows
home-country practice: no shareholder approval for the reverse split, for
>20% issuances below market, or for equity plans; no annual meeting
required. The 20-F's "73.5% of voting power held by directors, officers and
5% holders" is computed on a stale 15.3M-share count (Aug 2025) that
predates the April 2026 placement of 15M pre-split shares (49.5% of the
company); the F-3's non-affiliate float of 7,976,688 Class A implies
affiliates hold ~21%. The April 2026 buyers are unnamed (best-efforts
placement via Eddid Securities USA). What the structure forecloses: any
minority path — no activist lever, no vote on dilution, and a 5-vote block
whose holder is unidentified.
