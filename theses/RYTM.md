## 2. Business

**Created:** setmelanotide restores signalling in the MC4R hunger pathway for
patients whose obesity comes from a broken link in it: genetic (POMC, PCSK1,
LEPR deficiency; Bardet-Biedl syndrome) or acquired (hypothalamic damage,
usually from craniopharyngioma surgery or radiation). In the Phase 3 TRANSCEND
trial, HO patients lost a mean 15.8% of BMI at 52 weeks against a +2.6%
placebo gain, an 18.4-point placebo-adjusted difference (FDA approval coverage,
2026-03-19). Before this approval HO had no approved therapy, and diet and
GLP-1s work poorly when the hypothalamus itself is damaged. The patient gets
something nothing else gives, which is the surplus that supports a
rare-disease price.

**Captured:** product sales at a rare-disease price, through three channels:
(a) US specialty-pharmacy sales, $51.0M in Q2 2026 (72%); (b) ex-US sales via
named-patient and reimbursed markets, $20.3M (28%), with an HO launch in
Europe from 2027 and Japan approved 2026-08-24 (8-K 2026-08-04; Health Canada
2026-09-22 per web colour); (c) label expansion into new subpopulations
(congenital HO substudy; Prader-Willi via RM-718). The capture is partly
pledged: a deferred royalty obligation of $106.1M ($12.7M current) is a
royalty-financing claim on future sales (8-K 2026-08-04). Net price per
patient is NOT OBTAINED.

**Protected:** three mechanisms, each time-limited. (1) Regulatory: orphan
exclusivity per indication blocks the same drug for the same use. For HO this
would run ~7 years from 2026-03 (inferred), but the 5-year NCE exclusivity
from the 2020 approval has already lapsed. (2) Patents: the composition-of-
matter family expires 2026–27; a formulation patent runs to 2034
(drugpatentwatch/greyb, low-confidence). (3) Know-how: prescriber
relationships in a small endocrinology community, and genetic-testing
programmes that find patients. Clinically, GLP-1s are complements, not
substitutes: TRANSCEND showed effect on top of GLP-1 therapy (Rhythm TOS 2025
poster). The protection is real for ~7 years and then depends on the oral
successor, bivamelagon (Phase 3 starts by YE2026), which is option value, not
a moat.

**Control:** one class of common stock; insiders hold 0.79% and institutions
95.5% (`stocks.db`). No controlling holder. A Series A convertible preferred
(115,000 shares, $116.5M liquidation preference; 8-K 2026-08-04) ranks ahead
of common. Its conversion terms and any consent rights were not read. Nothing
read forecloses a takeover; charter anti-takeover provisions were not swept.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): flat.** Revenue grew 2.5× from 2023 to TTM,
but the operating loss is the same size. Since 2024 the gain is one-off, and
since 2025 the incremental operating margin is about zero (SEC XBRL
companyconcept):

|---|---|---|
| FY2021 | $3.2M | −$170.1M |
| FY2022 | $23.6M | −$179.2M |
| FY2023 | $77.4M | −$184.4M |
| FY2024 | $130.1M | −$265.5M |
| FY2025 | $189.8M | −$192.0M |
| TTM Q2 2026 | $239.9M | −$200.7M |

Q2 2026 alone: revenue +47% y/y, operating loss −$48.6M vs −$45.3M. SG&A rose
47% ($67.4M vs $45.9M) while R&D was flat ($43.4M vs $42.3M). Gross margin is
89% (`stocks.db`), so the leverage is available. It has not shown up yet
because launch SG&A and SBC ($87.2M TTM, 36% of revenue) grow as fast as
gross profit.
