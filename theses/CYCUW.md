## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at any price — including the $0.0304 last trade.** kill-thesis:
**SOUND** — conditions=3 (load-bearing), refuted=0, unknown=1. Condition
tiers not recorded in original run.

CYCUW is not a stock — it is the Western Acquisition Ventures de-SPAC public
warrant, struck at **$345.00** and expiring **2030-02-13**, on a common stock
that closed at **$0.7315**. The underlying must rise **471.6×** (+482%/yr
compounded for 3.49 years) just to reach *zero* intrinsic value, which means
Cycurion must be worth **~$5.25B** — against a $6.28M market cap today and
$14.4M of declining TTM revenue.

Pass. Not because the operating story is bad (it is, but that is a separate
question) — because **no version of the operating story reaches the strike.**
Take the entire bull case at face value and then double it: $50M revenue at a
35% gross margin with SG&A held flat produces ~$2.5M of operating income, a
$75M market cap at 30×, and **$3.71/share**. That is a 5× win for the equity
and still 1.1% of the warrant's strike. CYCUW's payoff does not depend on
Cycurion executing; it depends on Cycurion becoming a large-cap.

**Closest attack:** the full-ratchet anti-dilution hypothesis — if the public
warrants reset toward $1.35, CYCUW at $0.0304 would be worth ~$0.38, a 12×
mispricing (adjudication in the Kill-thesis record).

Load-bearing conditions for the pass (adjudication in the Kill-thesis
record):

1. The strike is ~$345.
2. No repricing, anti-dilution reset, or exchange offer rescues it.
3. The underlying cannot approach $345 by 2030-02-13.

The expiry date is *not* load-bearing and is excluded from the count (see the
Kill-thesis record).

## 2. Business

**Created.** Cycurion is a cybersecurity and IT-services roll-up selling
managed security, compliance, and network services to state/federal agencies
and enterprises, assembled by acquisition (Secuvant LLC; Kustom
Entertainment's video-solutions business). The 17.22% TTM gross margin is the
tell: that is reseller/staffing economics, not software. The customer is
buying labour and third-party product with a wrapper, and the surplus over
what they pay is thin.

**Captured.** Time-and-materials and managed-service contracts, mostly
through public-sector vehicles. TTM: revenue $14,401,378, cost of revenue
$11,921,065, gross profit $2,480,313.

**Protected — no mechanism found.** Nothing in the disclosures identifies
what stops a competitor tomorrow. Government IT-security work is recompeted
on contract vehicles, clearances, and past performance; the company has
quantified none of these. **SG&A of $11,992,498 against $2,480,313 of gross
profit** — overhead is 4.8× the gross profit the business generates — is what
an absent moat looks like on the income statement. This silence is the
answer, and it is written down here rather than papered over.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative** — decisively:

| period | revenue | operating income | op margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2021 | $16.88M | n/a (net income **+$0.19M**) | ~breakeven |
| TTM to ~Jun 2025 | $16.29M | **−$1.71M** | −10.5% |
| TTM to 2026-06-30 | **$14.40M** | **−$13.05M** | **−90.6%** |

Revenue fell 11.6% YoY and 15% since 2021 while the operating loss grew

This is a company that has been *bought* bigger and *operated* smaller.

**Standing checks.** *Base rate* — the relevant one is not the turnaround
rate but the survival rate: a company with two quarters of cash, negative
tangible equity, a $13.6M working-capital deficit, and an active delisting
determination faces a materially elevated probability of dilution-to-nothing;
against that, "becomes a $5B company in 3.5 years" has no meaningful base
rate at all. (Damodaran's EVA dataset puts only ~29% of firms above their
cost of capital; BLS puts under a third of new firms alive at seven years —
both far above what this requires.) *Short case* — two quarters of cash, a
hearing in three days, the standard cure publicly declined, an acquisition
target that could not produce audited financials, and serial dilution at
falling prices; strong on the fundamentals, though 20% short interest and
borrow cost make the equity short a poor trade. That distinction does not
touch the warrant. *Management incentives* — equity compensation rewards a
higher share price; the July letter attributes the decline to market abuse
without engaging the 5.3× share-count increase management itself authorised
over the same window. *Moat* — asked as a mechanism, not a label; none found
(§2). *Disconfirming search* — run explicitly for reasons the warrant might
be worth something (repricing, exchange offers, squeeze, anti-dilution), not
for reasons it is worthless.
