## 2. Business

**Created:** medicines with clinically demonstrated superiority in targeted
sub-populations — osimertinib in EGFR-mutant NSCLC, durvalumab across
perioperative oncology, the Daiichi Sankyo ADCs (Enhertu, Datroway), C5
complement franchise in rare disease. The customer surplus is survival
benefit; H1 2026 carried six positive Phase III readouts and 30 major-region
approvals since the Q4 print (H1 6-K).

**Captured:** patent-window pricing across US/EU/China/EM, extended by
combination strategies (FLAURA2 keeps Tagrisso the 1L backbone; AMPLIFY
moves Calquence into fixed-duration 1L CLL) and by indication stacking on
the same molecule (Imfinzi: NSCLC→SCLC→BTC→HCC→gastric→bladder). Geographic
breadth includes the largest western China pharma business (~12–13% of
revenue) — both a capture channel and a VBP/policy exposure.

**Protected:** patents (time-decaying by construction — see the cliff map in
§4), regulatory data exclusivity, biologics/ADC manufacturing complexity,
and an oncology commercial infrastructure a generic cannot replicate. The
durable "moat" is therefore the **replacement process** — R&D productivity
plus in-licensing — which is exactly the thing that cannot be verified
forward. CARDIO-TTRansform (§3) is a live reminder that the process has
attrition.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): strongly positive.** (stockanalysis, FY,
$B): revenue 37.4 → 44.4 → 45.8 → 54.1 → 58.7 (2021→2025), operating income
1.06 → 3.76 → 8.19 → 10.00 → 13.74; TTM 61.4 / 13.97 (margin 2.8% → ~23%;
2021 was depressed by Alexion-deal charges).

1. **Whether the 2031–33 LOE wave gets replaced** (terminal condition). Not
   in any disclosure — peak-sales by asset is not given. Its absence does
   not kill the pass; it kills the buy.
2. **US pricing policy endgame (MFN/tariffs/IRA expansion).** Absent from
   AZN's own risk disclosures; sector-unresolved. Bounded: US is ~44% of
   product sales (20-F), so a forced price reset is a first-order terminal
   risk.
3. **Growth to $80B/2030.** On-track evidence exists (guide reconfirmed,
   11/20 medicines delivered) but the number is forward — credit withheld
   per the uncertainty rule.
4. **Domain science I cannot evaluate:** per-asset clinical differentiation
   (e.g., camizestrant vs rival SERDs, Baxfendy uptake). Bounded by the
   portfolio's breadth — no single asset is >7% of revenue (Tagrisso ~12% is
   the exception).

   not verified in the 20-F remuneration section this run; low-confidence
