## 2. Business

**Creates value:** aggregation of authentic human opinion — 26B+ posts and
comments across 100k+ active communities (Q2'26 letter). The customer surplus
is trust: subjective questions ("what should I buy/watch/do") answered by
people with nothing to sell. Two customer sets get surplus: users (free
answers, community) and advertisers (high-intent context adjacent to purchase
deliberation).

**Captures value:** unpacked, four mechanisms — (1) auction-priced feed and
conversation-page ads (impressions × pricing both growing, per COO); (2)
performance formats taking direct-response budgets: Dynamic Product Ads and
app installs each doubled YoY, Reddit Max automation revenue +150% QoQ,
advertisers +70% YoY, scaled channel (SMB/mid-market) doubled; (3) content
licensing to AI labs (in "Other revenue": $43M in Q2, +24% YoY — ~5% of
revenue, decelerating relative to ads); (4) Premium/Gold (immaterial, per
10-K). US ARPU $11.85/quarter vs international $2.26 — the monetization
engine is overwhelmingly US logged-in/direct users.

**Protects value:** the corpus and the communities are unreplicable — a
competitor cannot bootstrap twenty years of niche-community accumulation, and
advertisers cannot buy "human recommendation context at scale" elsewhere. But
the moat protects the *content*, not the *traffic route*: the route for new
US users runs materially through Google, which Reddit does not own. That
split — owned content, unowned distribution — is the whole battleground.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive (strongly).** Revenue 2021→TTM:
$485M → $2,779M while operating income went -$127M → +$785M (stockanalysis
income-statement route; the 2024 -$561M opinc is the IPO SBC charge, visible
as Q1'24's -$589M). Five straight quarters of revenue growth + adj EBITDA
margin > 100 ("rule of 104" in Q2).

## 3. Threads pulled

**Why the stock fell 21% (the central thread).** Not the P&L — Q2 beat on
everything (revenue $805M vs ~$786M consensus-implied guide, EPS $1.25 vs
$0.97 est). The cause: US DAUq fell sequentially for the first time (53.5M →
53.2M), attributed to "choppy" search referrals that worsened late in the
quarter, with visibility "remain[ing] low" (CEO, Q2 call). Analyst reaction
split violently: Roth cut to $145, B. Riley raised to $270 — the market
cannot agree on whether this is noise or the start of the Google cliff.

**The cohort mix under the growth (the attack that came closest).** Global
DAU +18% YoY decomposes into logged-in +7% and logged-out +27% (Q2'26 letter,
final disclosure of this split). The high-value cohort is growing slowly; the
fast-growing cohort is exactly the one Google threatens. DAU/WAU frequency
ratio *declined* YoY: 26.5% → 25.3% global, 27.8% → 27.0% US (computed from
letter figures). Reach is expanding; habit is thinning. New-app-user
retention +50% YoY "on a relative basis, from a small base" (CEO) is the
countervailing product evidence — real but early.

**Disclosure cadence signal (adverse).** Q2'26 is the last quarter Reddit
will report logged-in vs logged-out users (CFO, call) — the disclosure being
withdrawn is precisely the metric under pressure. A June 2025 securities
class action (10-K, Note 11) alleges misleading statements about the impact
of Google Search and AI Overviews. Neither refutes the business case; both
mean management's qualitative framing ("not our first rodeo") gets zero
evidentiary weight here — only disclosed numbers were credited.

**Is ad revenue exposed to the declining traffic?** Logged-out users are
monetized "mainly through conversation pages and feed ads" (10-K). No
disclosure quantifies revenue by login state or referral source — marked
UNKNOWN. The indirect evidence: ads +64% and +61% total through two quarters
of referral chop, and Q3 guided to +48% — the revenue engine has been
resilient so far.

**Asset-light verification (passed).** 10-K contractual obligations: $372M
total non-cancellable purchase commitments (2026 $207M, 2027 $111M, 2028
$54M — cloud infrastructure minimums) + $26M operating leases. Against $1.02B
TTM FCF and $2.79B cash, trailing capex ($7.4M TTM, $1M in Q2) is honest —
hosting runs through cost of revenue ($70M in Q2), not hidden capex.

**Guidance culture.** 8/8 quarters of large EPS beats (est → actual: 0.24→
0.36, 0.02→0.13, 0.19→0.45, 0.52→0.80, 0.94→1.24, 0.58→1.01, 0.97→1.25;
Robinhood earnings results, actuals cross-checked against
sec_fundamentals.db eps_diluted = 1.25 for Q2'26 — agree). The +48% Q3 guide
is probably sandbagged like its predecessors; treat guide-vs-actual, not
guide level, as the falsifier trigger.

**Buyback (context).** $235M repurchased in Q2 at avg $157.57 — above
today's price; $760M authorization remains. FDSO up only 0.2% YoY net.
SBC full-year guide *lowered* to low-to-mid-teens % of revenue.

**Marketing ROI (adverse, minor).** CFO: paid user-acquisition returns were
"mixed... needs stronger attention"; international growth included a decline
in machine-translated content. The DAU growth machine has soft spots beyond
Google.

**Options read (mandatory):** path 2 (Robinhood stopgap; RDDT is not in the
CBOE 24-symbol catalog, no options.db history) — see §4's table.

**Dead ends:** (1) Data licensing renewal (mostly). Google and OpenAI
renewals hit "early Q1" 2027; CEO: "the range of outcomes is wide," no
strategy change. Content licensing is only ~5% of revenue, so a lapse is a
small direct hit; the real coupling is strategic (licensing ↔ Reddit
placement in AI Overviews and blue links). Unquantifiable from disclosures —
folded into UNKNOWN #2. (2) Insider activity. No Form 4s filed since the
July 30 print (latest 2026-07-17; routine 10b5-1-pattern sales and 144s
earlier in the year — EDGAR). Nothing signal-bearing either way.

## 5. Falsifiers

- **US DAU declines again in both Q3 and Q4 prints** — two consecutive
  declines makes "confined to logged-out" untenable regardless of what
  management says (the login split goes dark, so US DAU is the remaining
  observable).
- **Q3 actual revenue misses the $860-870M guide** — this management has
  never missed; a first miss would mean the referral decay reached the P&L.
- **Q4 revenue guide below ~+35%** — deceleration beyond the glide path the
  valuation needs.
- **Adjusted EBITDA margin declines YoY in any quarter** — would mean they
  are buying users to mask decay (watch marketing spend % of revenue).
- **Google demotes/removes Reddit placement or licensing lapses without
  replacement** — observable via the renewal announcements (early Q1'27) and
  third-party traffic trackers (low-confidence tier).

**Reopen trigger:** 2026-10-29: Q3 print (re-examine; or if price revisits
~$140).

## 6. UNKNOWNs

1. **Share of ad revenue attributable to logged-out / search-referred users.**
   Would come from: a revenue-by-surface or revenue-by-login-state
   disclosure that has never existed in any filing. Absence does not kill
   the thesis (the +64% through the chop bounds it indirectly) but it makes
   condition 2 unverifiable — and management just withdrew the adjacent
   user-split disclosure.
2. **Google's forward referral behavior** (AI Overviews rollout pace, Reddit
   placement persistence, licensing-renewal linkage). Would come from:
   Google, which discloses nothing. This is the unknown that can cliff. The
   thesis dies here if the decay is fast; nothing in Reddit's disclosures
   can rule that in or out.
3. **Whether "destination" status holds terminally in an AI-answer internet**
   (condition 6). The letter's own cohort data (logged-in +7%, DAU/WAU ratio
   falling) reads mildly against; WAU +24% and retention +50% read for. Not
   decidable from any current disclosure; the Q3/Q4 US DAU prints are the
   next evidence.
