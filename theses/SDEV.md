## 2. Business

**Created:** for a shareholder, a listed, equity-wrapped exposure to SKY (the
Sky Protocol / ex-MakerDAO governance token; 1 MKR = 24,000 SKY) plus the
staking yield, without holding the token directly. For the Sky ecosystem, a
public-market buyer of ~10% of supply that also stakes and votes (company
profile; S-3 2026-08-11). The customer surplus is thin: anyone can buy and
stake SKY directly at 1.0× NAV.

**Captured:** three mechanisms, all small. (a) Staking rewards: 31,746,251
SKY earned in Q2 on ~2.27B staked ≈ 1.40%/qtr ≈ 5.7%/yr in token terms;
booked as $2.2M Q2 / $4.7M H1 "staking revenue" (Q2 8-K 2026-07-31).
(b) Mark-to-market on the SKY pile — which is not capture, it is the token
price (Q2 unrealized loss $50.6M; TTM "revenue" −$23.29M per stockanalysis
because fair-value changes run through revenue). (c) Any issuance above NAV
— the digital-asset-treasury flywheel — which only works while mNAV > 1 and
the board chooses to issue; none disclosed since January.

**Protected:** no moat. Spot SKY and self-custodied staking are free
substitutes; the vehicle's edge is regulatory convenience for equity-only
holders. The one structural feature — the Sky Frontier Foundation's 24-month
consent right over "any material amendment ... of the Company's digital asset
strategy" (S-3) — protects the *strategy* (the company cannot diversify out
of SKY without SFF's consent), not the shareholder.

**Control:** controlled company. R01 Fund LP (investment manager's managing
member is CEO/Chair Michael Kazley) and Framework Ventures IV together hold
"approximately 87.6% of the voting power" (DEF 14A, 2026-09-28); each has a
board nomination right; SFF designated director David Garcia Rios (8-K
2026-09-08). Forecloses any unsolicited takeover or activist path; a
discount-to-NAV liquidation campaign is impossible without the sponsors.
5,000,000 blank-check preferred shares were restored to availability
2026-09-02 (8-K Item 5.03).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative (never positive; not meaningful
post-conversion).** The legacy eyecare business never earned an operating
profit, and the treasury vehicle's operating line is the token price:
