MakeMyTrip is a good business in a structurally growing market. It is
India's dominant online travel agency (OTA): "nearly one in three domestic
air passengers" book through it. Its non-air segments are compounding at
20–30% in constant currency. The stock is not cheap on owner cash. The
cheapness is an optical effect of three things. First, a zero-coupon

that it will "press on building market share … rather than focus on
building profitability". That is the opposite of the margin expansion the
price needs. The one real way to re-rate is the India listing of the
subsidiary, but it is undated and unpriced.

depend on the owner-cash adjustments. It depends on the WACC. At the US

## 2. Business

- **Created:** a single app for Indian travel spend — flights, hotels
  (branded and a long tail of standalone and homestay supply), holiday
  packages, intercity buses (redBus), cabs, trains, visas (Atlys stake), and
  corporate travel (myBiz, 77,000+ SME/corporate clients). 87M+ lifetime
  retail customers and 549M+ app downloads (6-K 2026-03-16). For the
  customer, the surplus is price comparison plus aggregated supply that no
  single supplier offers. Fragmented hotel and bus operators get demand
  they could not reach alone. Growth increasingly comes from Tier-2 cities:
  45% of Myra AI-assistant usage (Q1 call).
- **Captured:** three mechanisms with different economics.
  (i) **Air:** a commission and service fee on GBV. Q1 air revenue was
  $55.6M on $1,542.3M GBV (3.6% gross). Adjusted margin is $98.5M before
  customer inducements are netted — thin, commoditised, supplier-dependent.
  (ii) **Hotels & packages:** merchant/agency margin. Revenue $151.2M on
  $746.4M GBV (20% gross, adjusted margin $134.5M) — the profit pool, and
  mix shift toward it raises group margin. (iii) **Bus and others:**
  commission on operator inventory, plus ad and ancillary fees. Bus revenue
  $44.9M on $501.9M GBV. Customer inducements ($106.8M in Q1, +20% y/y) are
  netted from revenue. Marketing is a further 5.4% of GBV. Adjusted operating
  profit is 1.8% of GBV, flat (Q1 release and call).
- **Protected:** partly a mechanism, partly habit. In hotels and buses the
  moat is supply aggregation of a fragmented base: tens of thousands of
  standalone properties and bus operators onboarded, rated and paid, which
  a new entrant must rebuild one by one. In air there is no structural moat:
  inventory is identical, and IndiGo holds a 64.2% domestic share (DGCA
  August 2026, low-confidence press), so it can sell direct. Share there
  rests on brand, app habit and the inducement budget. The emerging threat
  is LLM agents that transact (Google AI Mode — low-confidence press).
  MMYT's answer is an OpenAI partnership and Myra, not a mechanism. Treat
  excess returns as fading in the terminal period.
- **Control:** after the 2025 recapitalisation there is **no controlling
  holder**. The company repurchased 34,372,221 Class B shares from Trip.com
  for $3,038.8M (~$88.41/share) on 2025-07-02. Trip.com retains
  10,773,694 ordinary plus 5,295,690 Class B shares: **17.08% of voting
  power** as of 2026-03-31 (20-F FY26, Item 7, via search excerpt). It also
  keeps several board nominees (20-F related-party note). Class B votes with
  ordinary as one class (note 26). Founder Deep Kalra holds 4.0% (13G/A
  2026-02-06). An unsolicited takeover is not structurally foreclosed. In
  practice it needs Trip.com's 17%, and the board seats make a hostile path
  slow.

## 5. Falsifiers

**For the pass (flip toward buy):**

- **Shift —** price at or below **~$22**, where scenario A (18% USD growth,
  current tax) clears the 10.96% WACC.
- **Shift —** a public RHP or listing that values MakeMyTrip (India)
  Limited at **≥ ~$8B** (≥1.5× today's EV), with OFS proceeds committed to
  retiring the 2030 notes and the parent retaining ≥80%. That makes the
  SOTP discount the thesis rather than an option.
- **Shift —** Q2 FY27 (2026-10-27) adjusted operating profit **≥ +25% y/y
  in USD**, with adjusted operating profit/GBV rising above 1.8%. That
  would contradict management's share-over-margin framing and restore
  condition 3.

**For an owner (sell):**

- **Break —** air GBV declining y/y in constant currency, or MMYT's
  domestic air share falling below ~1 in 4 passengers. Disintermediation is
  then under way, not a macro pause.
- **Break —** an OFS priced at or below the Nasdaq-implied EV, or an
  inversion whose terms dilute Nasdaq holders. The IPO destroys value
