$120M guidance raise is tariff timing, not the business. The activist who

stake into the rally the month after losing the vote. Good business, wrong

today is a poor sample point anyway.

## 2. Business

**Created:** Bras, panties, sleep, lingerie and swim under Victoria's Secret;
the same categories plus apparel for an 18–24 customer under PINK; prestige
fragrance and body care (Bombshell, "America's number one fragrance" per the
CEO — unverified externally) under Beauty. The customer gets fit expertise
(the "top 10 bra frames" edited over 18 months), a steady drumbeat of fashion
newness, and — the part management now leads with — a brand "world" she
wants to belong to. 858 company-operated stores (790 US/Canada, 65 China JV,
3 Adore Me) at FY-end, 565 partner-operated stores in ~70 countries, and
VictoriasSecret.com / PINK.com / AdoreMe.com / DailyLook.com.

**Captured:** Five distinct mechanisms. (1) Company-operated North America
stores: $3.54B of FY2025 sales (54%) — merchandise margin on regular-price
selling, currently rising as promotions are cut (AUR up mid-single digits).
(2) Direct/digital: $2.04B (31%), same merchandise economics without
occupancy but with fulfilment cost; app downloads +50% YoY in Q1. (3)
International: $967M (15%, +27% in FY2025, +45% reported in Q1 FY26) —
consolidated China JV store and digital sales (51%-owned; social selling is
the growth engine), royalties on franchise partners' sales, wholesale and
sourcing sales to partners, and EU direct sales fulfilled from a new European
DC. (4) Adore Me DTC and the DailyLook styling subscription (acquired Jan
2023 for $369M cash; $120M of long-lived assets impaired in FY2025 — a
capture mechanism that has not worked). (5) A private-label credit card
program, $5M of the FY2025 sales bridge — immaterial.

**Protected:** A brand in a taste-driven category. Real assets: scale (the
largest specialty intimates chain in North America), a physical fleet the
CFO calls "a competitive advantage" for fit, an owned media franchise (the
fashion show; Angels Among Us drew 100,000 applicants and 1.7B impressions),
and a marketing budget (~$430M advertising in FY2024) no single competitor
matches. What is missing: switching costs (none), a structural cost
advantage (none disclosed), or a network. Management claims share gains in
intimates on every one of the 7 calls in the corpus without ever printing a
share number. The honest reading is a brand moat that must be re-earned each
season — it eroded from FY2021 to FY2023 (Aerie, Skims, Savage X, Amazon,
Shein) and is being rebuilt, which is exactly the shape of a moat that can
erode again.

**Control:** One class of common, 79.5M shares, no controller. Largest
holders per the 2026 proxy and later 13D/A: BlackRock 10.78M (~13.6%,
13G/A), BBRC International (Brett Blundy) 7.81M (9.8%, down from 10.31M /
13.0% before the meeting). Independent chair (Donna James); nine directors
all elected annually under majority voting, so the activist path is fully
open and was just used — BBRC's withhold campaign against the chair lost
53.5M–12.3M (8-K 2026-06-15). The board has twice declined to seat Blundy,
citing "reputational, legal, conflict of interest and governance risks"
(DEF 14A). Nothing in the charter forecloses a takeover; a hostile bid by
BBRC is the standing structural possibility the press flagged.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative FY2021→FY2023, positive FY2023→TTM;
currently positive.** Fiscal years end late January (FY2021 = Jan-2022).
