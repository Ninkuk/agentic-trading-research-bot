**Disputed expectation:** the price implies ~9–11%/yr on flat-to-modest
FCF growth off a trough-margin base (a 7.2% levered FCF yield) — i.e. the
market prices the FY26 margin reset as roughly permanent and AI as a
seat-count threat; the Sep 30 FY27 guide (margin ≥35% and organic ASV ≥5%)
and the Nov 10 medium-term targets are what revise it, in either direction.

The thesis is unchanged in substance from 2026-07-30: FactSet is a good
business (31% GAAP / 34% adjusted operating margin, >95% ASV retention, 26
consecutive dividend increases, 3.2% of shares retired in a year), it is
defensible (concorded point-in-time data, workflows embedded in clients'
audit chains, the CGS identifier franchise), the price is fair-to-cheap
against an 8.3% cost of equity, and the new management is spending margin
down to chase an AI opportunity that five consecutive quarters of ASV
acceleration say is demand, not disintermediation. The market's two doubts
— that the margin trough is a slope, and that AI eventually shrinks the
seat base — are both undecidable from any current filing, and the next 13
days plus the Nov 10 Investor Day are where they get decided. That is why
the label stays UNPROVEN and the call stays BUY: bounded downside on the
stress case (~8%/yr on FY25 cash flow and 2% growth), asymmetric upside
if the guide lands where the Q3 call's "clear line of sight" points.

**Closest attack:** the incentive plan pays for growth twice as hard as
for margin — refined this run from the FY25 proxy (DEF 14A, 2025-10-27):
the annual cash incentive's company component is ASV Growth **2/3** and
Adjusted Operating Margin **1/3**, with the margin component capped at 150%
unless ASV hits target; the three-year PSUs pay on cumulative *dollar*
operating earnings and revenues, not margin. Q3's margin miss *was* the
ASV-outperformance accrual, and the CEO reserved "the flexibility in the
margin range" for Q4. July's "ASV-linked, not margin-linked" was
overstated — margin is in the plan — but the weights say a guide that
keeps buying ASV with margin is the plan working as designed. Detail in
the Kill-thesis record.
