## 2. Business

**Created:** TEN Events, Inc. (the operating subsidiary) runs corporate
webcasts — earnings calls, investor days, town halls, product launches,
training — in two modes. *Full-service broadcast* is white-glove production:
on-site crews, a live control room, dedicated project management, sold to
companies that will not risk a mission-critical broadcast on self-serve tools.
*Self-service broadcast* is the Xyvid Pro / TEN Pro platform: a browser studio
with CDN distribution, claimed 10K–100K concurrent attendees and 99.99%
uptime. What the customer buys is the removal of the risk that a
high-visibility corporate event fails in public. That is a real preference,
and it is why the gross margin is 74.8% (85.8% in Q2'26) rather than that of a
staffing business.

**Captured:** three distinct mechanisms, and the mix is moving the wrong way.
(i) *Platform usage revenue* — $469K in Q2'26, **−45.1% YoY** — consumption
billing on the self-serve platform; this is the piece the deck calls ARR and
the piece that is collapsing fastest. (ii) *Professional and managed services*
— $262K in Q2'26, +0.4% — per-event production fees, i.e. labour. (iii) There
is no third leg. The company's stated strategy is to add one (AI analytics,
captioning, intent data), but as of the Q2 print none of it is generating
disclosed revenue. The revenue mix is therefore drifting from the scalable
half toward the labour half, which is the opposite of the "SaaS/ARR
transition" the January 2026 deck promises.

**Protected:** nothing identified. There is no disclosed switching cost, no
contractual lock-in, no data asset and no network effect — a webcast platform
is substitutable at the next event. The competitive set (Zoom Webinars,
ON24, Cvent, Webinar.net, Goldcast) is better capitalised by two to three
orders of magnitude. Most tellingly, the deck's own answer to "why us" is a
**technology partnership with Webinar.net** — the company licenses a third
party's platform to build its own next-generation product. A capability you
rent from a vendor who will rent it to anyone is not a moat; it is a supply
agreement. Write it down: **no moat.**

**Control:** UNVERIFIED. SEC EDGAR returned HTTP 403 on all three routes
tried, so the 10-K beneficial-ownership table could not be read. What is
observable: a single class of common stock appears in every registration
statement and quote-vendor record, with no second class disclosed;
stockanalysis reports insiders 1.00% and institutions 0.65%; the January 2026
deck asserts a "Diversified Shareholder Base." Against that, the company filed
**DEF 14C information statements twice in 2025** — a Form 14C rather than 14A
means corporate action taken by written consent of a majority holder without a
stockholder meeting, which implies a controlling block existed at that time.
The company then announced 2026 annual-meeting key dates on 2026-08-25 (Rule
14a-8 proposal deadline 2026-09-04), so a proxy process is now running. The
null answer ("one share, one vote, no controller") is *not* established here;
it is unread. See §6.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Not the mild version — revenue
fell 41% while the operating loss multiplied ~130-fold.

## 4. Valuation

**Inputs and pairing.** Levered TTM free cash flow of **−$5.184M** (operating

**Hurdle.** rf 4.75% + beta 1.2 × ERP 4.14% = **9.72%** (Damodaran, implied
ERP and T-bond rate as of 2026-09-01). Beta is reported `n/a` — XHLD has

0.8–1.2 stable band and that clamp is stated**: for a name with an Altman
Z-Score of −5.93 that moved 12x in nineteen sessions, 1.2 is a floor on risk
dressed as a ceiling, and the true cost of equity is far above 9.72%. Every
number below is therefore generous to the stock.

**The required-FCF inversion (what replaces the DCF).** For $112.83M of

revenue**. At a 20% steady-state FCF margin it needs **$43.6M of revenue** —
15.4x the current base and 9.1x the FY2022 peak; at a heroic 30% margin,
$29.0M — 10.2x the current base. The company generates −$5.18M today, from a
base that has fallen four years running with 25 employees.

   deal timing — falling 45.1%. The attack explains the wrong variable.
2. *Operating loss structural and widening* — **SURVIVED.** Attacked on the

**Standing checks.**

- *Base rate:* only ~29% of firms earn above their cost of capital
  (Damodaran EVA), and under a third of new firms survive seven years (BLS) —
  so "the turn happens" is a minority outcome before any evidence. The
  sharper local base rate is this security's own: it has already run the
  pattern once, from a split-adjusted $128.10 on IPO day (2025-02-13) to
  $0.7644 on 2026-08-05, a 99.4% decline, through a 1-for-15 reverse split.

  at every point of contact.
- *Management incentives:* funding comes from issuing stock, not from

  reverse-split-adjusted options struck at $5.40 are in the money for the

- *Disconfirming search:* run in the bull direction — the 2026-08-06
