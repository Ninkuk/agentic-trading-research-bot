## 2. Business

**Created:** fastest time-to-deployment for AI compute at rack/data-center
scale — first-to-market building-block designs on each new silicon generation
(GB200/B200 shipping; Vera Rubin, MI450 prepped), in-house liquid cooling
(>3,000 DLC racks/month capacity claimed), now sold as turnkey "DCBBS" (racks
+ cooling + switches + management software + service). Customers buy
quarters-not-years deployment speed, not the cheapest box.

**Captured:** hardware markup, and thin — FY26 GM 10.8%, FY25 11.1% (GAAP).
Mix splits roughly: high-volume GPU systems (lowest margin),
enterprise/CPU/storage (better), DCBBS software/services (best; management
targets ~20% of gross profit, "not far away" — unaudited claim, no disclosure
splits it out yet). One CSP customer = 28% of FY26 revenue; nine customers
>$1B each (vs 4 in FY25).

**Protected:** speed and NVIDIA/AMD proximity — a mechanism, but not a
durable one. Nothing structural stops Dell/HPE/ODMs from matching a rack
design within a generation; management's own risk factors say larger
customers → lower margins and less predictability. The moat is a lead
measured in quarters. Written down as such.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): flat-to-negative through FY25, recovered in
FY26 — but the recovery is one quarter's mix.** (GAAP, $M, fiscal years ended
June 30; income-statement route, stockanalysis; FY26 = tonight's preliminary
release):

| FY | Revenue | Op income | Op margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 5,196 | 335 | 6.4% |
| 2023 | 7,123 | 761 | 10.7% |
| 2024 | 14,989 | 1,211 | 8.1% |
| 2025 | 21,972 | 1,253 | 5.7% |
| 2026 | 39,063 | 2,770 | 7.1% |

Q4 alone: 17.5% GM / 13.4% op margin vs 6.3–9.9% GM the prior three quarters.
Revenue 7.5× over four years, op income 8.3× — proportional, not operating
leverage.
