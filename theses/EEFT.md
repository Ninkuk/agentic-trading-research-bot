## 2. Business

Unchanged from `research/EEFT-2026-09-07.md` §2. In brief:

**Created:** physical reach a customer cannot build alone — ATMs and
currency conversion where a traveller's bank has no presence; prepaid
content reaching cash-paying consumers at corner shops; payout points in
the recipient's town for migrant senders.

**Captured:** per-transaction fees, six distinct mechanisms — ATM
withdrawal fees and DCC spread; merchant discount on acquiring; SaaS
processing fees on Ren/CoreCard; epay distribution commissions; remittance
send fees plus FX spread; Dandelion wholesale payout fees. Q2 2026: PI
$377.1M revenue (+11%) / $86.1M op income (+2%); epay $294.0M (+5%) /
$32.8M (+5%); Cross-Border $439.6M (−4%) / $43.3M (−34%). [8-K ex-99.1,
2026-07-30]

**Protected:** density and per-country licensing — slow, capital- and
regulator-heavy to replicate. It protects the *position*, not the *price*,
and does nothing against contactless adoption. A shallow moat.

**Control:** one class of common, one share one vote, no controlling
holder; insiders 7.71% (`stocks.db`). Nothing forecloses an unsolicited
approach or activist campaign.

**Delta:** August's Banxico print says the remittance mechanism's volume
driver is still falling: transactions −6.4% YoY while average ticket rose
3% to $412. Ria's send fee is per transaction, so the transaction count,
not the dollar total, is the revenue-relevant number.
