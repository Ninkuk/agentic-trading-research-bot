## 2. Business

**Created:** Patients with genetically defined muscle diseases get an
oligonucleotide that reaches skeletal and heart muscle through a transferrin
receptor-1 antibody fragment (Fab). In exon-51 DMD, management claims monthly
dosing against Exondys 51's weekly infusions, and 18–19% dystrophin at 12–24
months (conf. 2026-09-15; company data, not independently evaluated here).
About 1,600 exon-51-amenable US patients are known, and 400–500 are on Exondys
(same source). The surplus to patients is real if the drug works. Whether it
works is the question this run cannot answer: **UNKNOWN (domain science)**.

**Captured:** There is no revenue yet. The planned mechanism is list-priced
orphan-drug infusions paid by commercial insurers and Medicaid, weight-based
dosing (pricing undisclosed), sold through a specialty channel the company says
is built ("manufacturing, supply chain, commercial, market access, and patient
services teams", MS conf.). The three future businesses are DMD exon 51 (BLA
filed), DM1 (z-basivarsen, registrational cohort plus the HARMONIA Phase 3) and
FSHD (DYNE-302, IND cleared July 2026). Other DMD exons would reach "39% of the
boys" (CFO, MS conf.).

**Protected:** Protection has three mechanisms. Composition-of-matter patents
on the Fab-linker-oligo constructs (not read this run). Orphan exclusivity
(seven years US) once approved. A dosing-convenience edge over Sarepta's weekly
PMOs. The competitor Avidity/Novartis platform is the same idea (an antibody
delivering an oligo to muscle): del-zota (exon 44) received BLA priority review
on 2026-09-08. So the moat against a same-mechanism rival is the specific
molecule and exon, not the platform. Where exons do not overlap, each drug is a
near-monopoly on a small population. Where they overlap, nothing structural
stops a second entrant.

**Control:** NOT OBTAINED — this run did not read the 10-K cover or the proxy.
`stocks.db` shows insiders at 0.27% and institutions at 101.5% of shares, so no
insider controller is evident. Share class count and charter defenses (e.g. a
classified board) are unverified.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue is $0 in every year, and
operating losses widen each year: 2021 −$150.0M · 2022 −$171.0M · 2023
−$242.2M · 2024 −$343.9M · 2025 −$468.2M. TTM EBITDA is −$534.8M. Q2 2026 R&D
was $152.2M (vs $99.2M) and G&A $29.5M (vs $16.6M) — spending is accelerating
into launch.
