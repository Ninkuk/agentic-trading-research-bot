## 2. Business

**Created:** 3.64B people use a Meta app daily (DAP, September 2026, +3%
YoY); free connection and entertainment whose relevance is now the product
— Instagram time per user at an all-time high and up double digits YoY,
Facebook Reels at all-time highs, Threads time per user +20% (Q3 call,
primary-transcribed). New since July: Muse, a personal agent — 10M+ set-ups
in the US/Canada in six weeks, ~25% daily use among set-ups, shopping the
top use case with >$200 average purchase (company claims, no independent
null); Meta One subscriptions 15M+ including trials.

**Captured:** (i) an auction over 10%-more impressions at 14%-higher average
price — $63.06B of Q3 ad revenue, 97.6% of total; (ii) FoA "other revenue"
$1.10B/quarter, +59% (WhatsApp paid messaging, subscriptions); (iii)
partnership ads at a $15B annual run rate, +97%; (iv) nascent lines with no
disclosed revenue — Muse commerce fees and subscriptions, Meta Enterprise
(model API, Muse Code, business agents). Reality Labs is a −$4.1B/quarter
cost center guided to ~−$15B in 2027. (Q3 8-K Ex-99.1; Q3 call.)

**Protected:** impression supply and auction density no rival can assemble
(3.64B DAP; nearly 10M advertisers using gen-AI creative tools), a
first-party model stack now replacing third-party content-understanding
models at >80% lower unit cost (company claim), and — new — the compute
fleet itself: $253B of net PP&E plus $89B of construction in progress. The
mechanism that could fail: an agent layer (Muse or a rival's) that mediates
attention and commerce *between* the user and the feed, which is why the
company is building the agent itself.

**Control:** dual-class; Mr. Zuckerberg holds "a majority of the voting
power" through Class B (10-Q Item 1A) and can decide every stockholder
matter including any merger or sale. The 2026-10-19 recapitalization adds
non-voting Class C (effective 2027-01-19) so the company can issue equity
without diluting that vote. The Founder Alignment Agreement (8-K Ex-10.1)
staples 80% of his Class C dividend shares — selling one requires selling or
converting a vote-bearing share — releasing 2.5%/quarter over 32 qualifying
quarters while he remains a full-time executive, and obliges the board to
declare a further 1:1 Class C dividend at each doubling of the Class C 30-day
VWAP from the $580.60 reference (2026-09-10, split-adjusted) with ≥10%
annualized return, those additional shares unstapled. The structure
forecloses an unsolicited takeover and any activist path; every
equal-treatment clause in the agreement is conditional on his consent to the
transaction. One share, one vote is not the null here, and the 2026-10-26
raise shows the Class C mechanism in use within a week of its approval.

## Kill-thesis record

**Kill-thesis verdict: FLAWED** — conditions=5, refuted=1, unknown=0,
pending=2, not_obtained=0. The bull case is repairable only by the issuer:
a 2027 capex guide that contradicts its own commitments schedule would do
it, and nothing else will.

The list attacks itself before any evidence is read: condition 1 (26%
growth) beside condition 3 (reinvestment falling to depreciation) beside a
moat claim is the high-growth / low-reinvestment / low-risk triad, and the
issuer resolved the strain the only way it can be resolved — by reinvesting
(g = reinvestment rate × return), which is what the commitments schedule
records.

Per-condition adjudication (the §1 bull-case list):

1. Core ad growth ≥15% through 2027 — **SURVIVED** at *probable*. Attack:
   price-per-ad gains (+14%) ride "improvements in macro conditions

   growth is skewing to "lower monetizing surfaces and regions". The Q4
