## 2. Business

**Created:** Licensed on-demand music + karaoke (WeSing) + music-adjacent
audio for Chinese users; the paid tier sells quality, catalog completeness,
and — increasingly — superfan access (SVIP: early concert access, artist
merch, fan clubs). Post-Ximalaya, long-form audio (audiobooks, podcasts).

**Captured:** Four distinct mechanisms, not one: membership fees (RMB
4.57B in Q1'26, 58% of revenue), advertising incl. ad-supported free tier,
offline/IP monetization (concerts, artist merch — inside "other music," +28%
YoY), and declining social-entertainment tipping (RMB 1.38B, −11% YoY, now
~17% of revenue). Music-related services grew +12.2% YoY in Q1'26.

**Protected:** The honest mechanism is distribution plus catalog scale
economics: Tencent ecosystem placement (WeChat Channels integration launched
July 2026), the largest licensed catalog amortized over the largest paying
base, and accumulated user assets (playlists, favorites — "personal music
assets"). It is **eroding at the price-sensitive edge**: management concedes
rivals use AI-generated libraries and aggressive traffic diversion, and Kugou
is bleeding while QQ Music holds (management claim, no data). The moat holds
at the superfan core, not at the margin.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0):** not recorded as a labelled print in the
original run; the figures behind it appear in §1 (revenue RMB 31.2B → 32.9B
2021→2025 while operating income nearly quadrupled, RMB 3.8B → 13.4B).

## 5. Falsifiers

For the pass (flip toward buy): Q2 (Aug 11) and Q3 (Nov 11) prints both
showing membership revenue growing YoY and not declining sequentially — or
restored subscriber disclosure.

For an owner (sell) — all observable:

1. Membership revenue declines YoY in two consecutive quarters.
2. Gross margin turns down for two consecutive quarters (content-cost
   re-inflation — the AI/piracy short case showing up in the P&L).
3. Dividend or buyback suspended.
4. A landmark PRC ruling legalizing AI covers / "song washing" against label
   catalogs (breaks licensed-content pricing).
5. Forced US delisting without a clean HK (1698) conversion path.

**Reopen trigger:** two clean membership prints (Q2 2026-08-11 BMO confirmed;
Q3 2026-11-11 tentative).
