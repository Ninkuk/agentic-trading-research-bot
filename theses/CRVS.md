## 2. Business

**Created:** an oral pill aimed at T-cell-driven disease. ITK is a kinase in
T-cell receptor signalling. Corvus's claim is that blocking it selectively
shifts T-helper differentiation away from Th2/Th17 toward regulatory T cells
(Q2 call; Cantor 2026-09-10). Two patient groups:
(a) relapsed/refractory PTCL, where the standard single agents give modest
responses. Soquelitinib's Phase 1/1b subgroup (1–3 prior lines, n=24) showed
ORR 37.5% (6 CR, 3 PR), median PFS 6.2 months and median OS 28.1 months, with
no dose-limiting toxicity up to 600 mg BID (ASH 2025 final data, CancerNetwork
summary).
(b) moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis. A pill that worked like a biologic
without JAK-class safety baggage would be a real convenience gain for
patients. Phase 1 cohort 4 (200 mg BID, 56 days): EASI-75 75% vs 20%, EASI-90
25% vs 0%, IGA 0/1 33% vs 0%, mean EASI −72% vs −40%, p=0.035. All adverse
events were grade 1–2, at 41.7% vs 50% on placebo (8-K 2026-01-20 exh. 99.1).
The program also covers hidradenitis suppurativa and asthma (starting by
year-end 2026) and ALPS (NIAID-run Phase 2).

**Captured:** nothing today. There has never been product revenue
(stockanalysis financials). Capture would come through three channels:
(a) a US PTCL label, a small orphan market (orphan and fast-track
designations, Q2 release);
(b) an atopic dermatitis franchise, which needs Phase 2, then Phase 3, then
approval. Management names "post the phase II data" as the time for
"substantial strategic discussion", i.e. a partnering or sale event (Cantor
2026-09-10);
(c) its 48.6% equity stake in Angel Pharmaceuticals, which holds greater-China
rights to soquelitinib, ciforadenant and mupadolimab. The stake is carried at
$15.1M, and Corvus put $5.0M into Angel's June 2026 round (10-Q Q2 2026).

**Protected:** composition-of-matter patents and a first-mover position. The
company says it is "the only selective ITK inhibitor in the clinic", with
Aclaris' ITK/JAK3 program not advancing in atopic dermatitis (CBO Arcara,
Cantor 2026-09-10; this is management's claim, not independently verified).
Against that, the oral atopic dermatitis field is crowded by mechanism if not
by target: 10–12 STAT6 programs by management's own count, with Kymera's
KT-621 the most advanced. The protection is clinical data that doesn't exist
yet at scale. Whether the ITK biology is differentiated is UNKNOWN to this
author.

**Control:** one class of common (10-Q cover: 84,120,944 shares at
2026-08-06) plus 8,275,913 pre-funded warrants at $0.0001. 5% holders:
OrbiMed 10.1% (director Peter Thompson sits for it), Sirenia Capital 7.5%,
BlackRock 6.0%. CEO/chair Richard Miller holds 6.6%. Officers and directors
as a group hold 19.7% (DEF 14A 2026-04-24). The board is classified into
three staggered classes, which slows a hostile acquirer or an activist slate
by a year or more. No controlling holder. Miller's 2025 pay was $7.03M, of
which $6.99M was options and $36.6k salary (DEF 14A). Director Ian Clark
resigned 2026-07-22 (8-K Item 5.02; no reason given beyond thanks).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** No revenue in any year. Operating
loss ($M): FY2021 −38.4, FY2022 −32.0, FY2023 −23.3, FY2024 −27.6, FY2025
−43.0, TTM Jun-2026 −57.0 (stockanalysis financials). Quarterly figures are
Q1 2026 −14.9 and Q2 2026 −19.3 (`sec_fundamentals.db` facts). The loss is
widening as the Phase 3 and Phase 2 trials ramp: Q2 R&D $16.0M vs $7.9M a year

because of warrant-liability marks (FY2025 net −15.3 vs operating −43.0; FY2024
net −62.3 vs operating −27.6). Read the operating line.
