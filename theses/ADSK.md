## 2. Business

**Created:** the file format and the workflow. AutoCAD (25% of FY26 revenue
on its own, 10-K), Revit, Civil 3D, Inventor and Fusion are the tools in
which architects, civil engineers, contractors and product designers make
the drawings that other people are contractually obliged to accept; a
customer gets interoperability with every consultant, subcontractor and
regulator who already reads the format, plus a design-to-construction-to-
operations data thread (Forma, Construction Cloud, Tandem, now MaintainX)
that management calls "project intelligence." The surplus is switching cost
avoided: retraining a firm and re-templating its standards costs more than
the subscription.

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms. (i) Named-user annual subscriptions
across four product families — AECO $1,029M, AutoCAD/LT $500M, MFG $385M,
M&E $92M in Q2 FY27 — 97% of revenue recurring. (ii) Price realization: a
multi-year program removing multi-year discounts and winding down
maintenance-to-subscription renewals, which the company says is why RPO grew
only 2% while current RPO grew 12%. (iii) The new transaction model: since
FY25 the customer transacts with Autodesk directly and the reseller's margin
is re-booked as Autodesk revenue plus marketing expense — a ~2pt revenue
tailwind in Q2 and ~1.5pt for FY27 that does not recur in FY28 (CFO). (iv)
Consumption: Flex tokens (minimum purchase cut in June to widen SMB access,
"net accretive") and a stated intent to charge for API/machine-driven usage.
MaintainX adds a fifth, product-led maintenance-management subscription.

**Protected:** the standard-format lock-in above, reinforced by the channel
(TD Synnex alone is 8% of revenue as distributor) and by an enterprise
business agreement (EBA) structure that renews in large cohorts. The 10-K's
own words on the other side: "the software industry has limited barriers to
entry," and the disclosed dependence on AutoCAD-family sales that have "at
times" declined without a matching collections uplift. The moat is switching
cost on the installed base, not technical superiority; whether it survives a
generation of agent-driven design tools is the unassessed part.

**Control:** one class, one vote per share (DEF 14A, 2026-05-06); 211.1M
shares entitled to vote. No controlling holder — Vanguard 10.1%, BlackRock
9.1%. Two directors seated under a 2025 cooperation agreement with Starboard
Value; separate Chair and CEO; annual majority election; proxy access. Nothing
forecloses a takeover or a second activist campaign. Insiders hold 0.15%.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue $4,386M (FY22) → $5,005M
→ $5,497M → $6,131M → $7,206M (FY26) → $7,790M TTM; operating income $748M →
$1,032M → $1,175M → $1,415M → $1,808M → $2,188M TTM. Revenue +78%, operating
income +193%, GAAP operating margin 17.1% → 28.1%. Net income $497M → $1,642M.

January 31.)

**Standing checks.** Base rate: only ~29% of firms earn above their cost of
capital (Damodaran EVA dataset); Autodesk does today at ~58% ROIC, and the
terminal assumption in §4 lets that fade rather than persist — the thesis
does not need the 3-in-10 outcome. Short case (strongest form): per-seat
design software is the category most exposed to agentic tools; Autodesk's
own MCP openness lets a third-party agent do the drafting inside Fusion,
commoditizing the seat; the headline growth was optically inflated ~2pt by a
billing-model change that laps next year; NR3 is slipping; management, paid

revenue with short-term paper; and the last time FCF looked this good (2024)
an Audit Committee investigation found it was being pulled forward by

the sector drawdown were searched for a company-specific negative and none
was found; the disconfirming facts that did surface are the NR3 wording
downgrade and the FY28 comp, both now in condition 1. Moat: switching cost on
an installed standard is a mechanism, not a label — but the 10-K discloses
AutoCAD-family sales declining "at times" without a collections uplift, so
the mechanism protects renewals more than new logos, which is exactly where
an agent-native tool would enter.
