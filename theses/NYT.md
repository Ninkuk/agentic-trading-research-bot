## 2. Business

**Created:** trusted original journalism plus habit-forming lifestyle
products (News, Games, Cooking, Wirecutter, The Athletic) sold as one
bundle. The consumer gets five daily-use products for roughly the price of
one streaming service; >13M subscribers, 310k net digital adds in Q1'26
alone (Q1'26 call). ~2,000-journalist newsroom output "as others do less of
it" is increasingly rare — management's own framing, but the Pulitzer haul
and the five-year Chávez investigation back it.

**Captured:** four distinct streams, unpacked: (1) digital subscriptions —
$389M/q, +16% YoY, ARPU +2.4% via promo→full-price ladder and bundle price
increases; (2) advertising — $127M/q (+17%), digital $93M (+32%) sold on
first-party data across five marketer-safe surfaces; (3)
affiliate/licensing — $68.5M/q (+8%), Wirecutter commissions plus the Amazon
AI licensing deal; (4) legacy print — declining but still cash-generative.
(All Q1'26 call.)

**Protected:** the mechanism is the direct relationship — app habit and the
bundle raise switching costs and make the funnel less dependent on search
referral than any other publisher; brand trust compounds with each news
cycle; the newsroom's fixed cost (~$2.9B revenue base) is unreplicable at
competitor scale. **The unprotected flank is discovery**: the top of the
funnel still passes through platforms NYT does not control, and AI answers
compress it. Management concedes this on the call ("The Times isn't
immune").

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive, and still climbing.** (EDGAR
10-Ks): revenue 2018→2025 $1,749M→$2,825M (+62%); operating income
$190M→$432M (+127%); op margin 10.9%→15.3%, TTM 15.9% (Q1'26 AOP margin
+200bp to 16.6%).

## 5. Falsifiers

What would make me revisit the pass — or sell, if owned:

  bundle price step — the funnel eroding faster than pricing recovers it.
- Digital ad growth below mid-single digits once the supply step-up laps.
- An adverse merits ruling in *NYT v. Microsoft/OpenAI* that legitimizes
  uncompensated training/retrieval use (kills the licensing leg).

  disclosed economics, or a price where the adjusted base implies ≥10% —
  roughly $52 at the margin-lever path — would reopen this at the same
  facts.
