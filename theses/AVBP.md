## 2. Business

**Created:** an oral, once-daily EGFR TKI (firmonertinib, in-licensed from
Shanghai Allist, which holds Greater China rights) for EGFR-mutant NSCLC
subtypes that osimertinib serves poorly — exon 20 insertions and PACC
mutations — plus early ADCs (ARR-217 from Lepu; ARR-002 from Alphamab/
Aarvik work). The patient surplus would be oral convenience and CNS
activity versus IV amivantamab-plus-chemo, which is the first-line exon 20
standard (PAPILLON: mPFS 11.4 months, ORR 73%).

**Captured:** nothing yet — zero revenue in every period. Prospective
capture is US/ex-China product sales of firmonertinib, net of
high-single-digit to low-mid-teen royalties and up to $110M clinical/
regulatory plus $655M commercial milestones owed to Allist ($10M paid);
ARR-217 carries up to $280M development and $890M commercial milestones to
Lepu plus high-single to low-teen royalties. ARR-002 Greater China rights
went to Allist (Aug 2026, terms undisclosed) — the only inbound cash
mechanism, size unknown.

**Protected:** composition-of-matter patents and orphan/BTD exclusivities
on firmonertinib (BTD granted for exon 20), but protection presupposes an
approvable label, which FURVENT no longer delivers on its primary endpoint.
In exon 20 first-line the incumbent (amivantamab + chemo, mPFS 11.4 mo,
ORR 73% per OncLive) matches firmonertinib 240 mg's 11.0 mo BICR mPFS, and
firmonertinib did not separate from chemo alone (HR 0.75); oral rivals sunvozertinib (FDA accelerated approval, post-
platinum, July 2025) and zipalertinib are ahead. No moat on today's record.

**Control:** one share, one vote; no controlling holder disclosed in the
sources read; insiders 1.8%, institutions 70.0% (stocks.db). Nothing
forecloses a takeover or activist path — a cash-shell-plus-pipeline after a
Phase 3 miss is the classic reverse-merger / liquidation-activist target.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): flat (undefined — no revenue).** TTM
operating loss −$175.9M, net loss −$164.2M (stocks.db, through 6/30). H1
2026 vs H1 2025: R&D $80.0M vs $89.0M, G&A $18.8M vs $11.4M, net loss
$93.2M vs $95.8M (10-Q). The expense base is roughly flat; there is no
revenue line against which leverage can run.
