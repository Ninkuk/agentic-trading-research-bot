**Disputed expectation:** the ADS price implies ~5%/yr if peak-cycle FCF
holds flat forever and ~9% on a +25/+10/0 ramp with 2.4% perpetual growth —

showing ASPs still rising *and* a return program sized well beyond ₩40T, or a
Seoul rally that closes the 43% ADS premium from below.

$43B net cash, a ₩40T buyback in flight and a written >50%-of-FCF return
policy. The ADS still stacks two full-priced bets: peak memory pricing held
forever, and a 43% premium over the identical Seoul share that exists only
because the depositary conversion cap is shut. The buyback changed who gets
the cash, not how much there is; the price ran 37% on 3% more FCF. The
respectable counter is unchanged and stronger: forward PE ~8x on the Q3
estimate, Micron's blowout read-across (Sept 30), and now a shareholder-
return program with teeth — the Seoul line is expensive, not absurd. The ADS
buyer pays $1,410B for what Seoul prices at $985B.

with 2.36% terminal growth lands at ~11.3% — 39bp under the hurdle — and a
beta of 1.0 instead of 1.3 would flip it. The pass on the Seoul line is
beta- and path-sensitive; the pass on the ADS (the security under verdict)
survives every parameterization, needing a +40/+25/+10 FCF path just to
reach the hurdle. Second: the return program's "specific scale" due at the
Q3 print could still be a multiple of ₩40T — the policy ceiling is >50% of
FCF, and 2026 FCF could print ₩100T+.
