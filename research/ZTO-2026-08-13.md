## 2. Business

- **Created:** lowest-cost, reliable e-commerce parcel delivery at national
  scale. ZTO owns the scale-sensitive middle (93 sorting hubs — 88
  self-operated, ~3,800 line-haul routes, >10,000 self-owned trucks, 780
  automated sorter sets); ~6,000 direct network partners and >31,000 outlets
  own last-mile economics, aligning local entrepreneurs' capital and
  incentives (Q1'26 6-K).
- **Captured:** per-parcel transit + sorting fees charged to network partners;
  direct key-account revenue (reverse logistics, +92.2% YoY in Q1'26);
  accessories (digital waybill thermal paper); small freight forwarding and
  financing lines. Core express was 94.3% of Q1'26 revenue.
- **Protected:** a cost gap, not a pricing gap. Q1'26 unit sorting+transport
  cost fell 6 RMB-cents on volume-leveraged productivity; volume grew 13.2% vs
  industry ~5.8%. The service is commoditized at the merchant layer and >90%
  of parcels come from e-commerce platforms (concentrated demand). The state
  currently referees pricing (anti-involution), which favors the low-cost
  leader — but a policy is not a market equilibrium, and it caps pricing
  upside too.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0):** revenue/opinc (RMB M): 2021 30,406/5,503 →
2022 35,377/7,737 → 2023 38,419/10,008 → 2024 44,281/11,777 → 2025
49,099/10,475 → TTM 51,490/10,615. Direction: **positive 2021→2024 (margin
18.1%→26.6%), negative in 2025 (revenue +10.9%, opinc −11.1%)**, TTM
stabilizing. Q1'26 opinc +5.8% absolute despite op margin 22.1%→19.2%.
