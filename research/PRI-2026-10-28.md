## 2. Business

Unchanged from `PRI-2026-08-20.md` §2; no filing since the 2026-08-06
10-Q. In one line each:

**Created:** in-person financial education and simple products (term life,
mutual funds, annuities, managed accounts) for $30k–$130k households the
advisory industry does not profitably serve, plus a low-cost income
opportunity for the rep.

**Captured:** (1) term-life underwriting on ~$1T face in force, with most
mortality ceded so it behaves like a fee stream; (2) ISP sales commissions
plus asset-based and advisory fees on $140B of client assets; (3) net
investment income on a ~$4.7B portfolio plus small mortgage brokerage.

**Protected:** the 148,612-rep licensed distribution force and its
50-state/10-province licensing and compliance infrastructure. It is a real
moat, but it needs constant upkeep: the 2027 50th-anniversary convention
countdown runs on discounted licensing fees and incentives (Q2 call).
That upkeep is why condition 3 bears weight.

**Control:** one share, one vote, no controlling holder; institutions
96.3%, insiders 0.44% (`stocks.db`). Nothing forecloses a takeover or an
activist.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue FY2021 $2,772M → TTM
$3,486M (+26%); operating income $834M → $1,105M (+32%); margin 30.1% →
31.7% (stockanalysis income statement, via the 2026-08-20 run; TTM
unchanged — no new quarter since).

## 5. Falsifiers

- **Break —** regulatory action forcing restructure of the
  independent-contractor model. That means a major-state reclassification
  without a direct-sales carve-out, an FTC action against the entity, or a
  finalized earnings-claim rule that reaches licensed insurance
  distribution.
- **Break —** the sales-force decline accelerates. Either the FY2026
  licensed force runs worse than −2% at the Q3/Q4 prints *and* new
  licenses stay down double digits despite the convention incentives, or
  declines exceed 5% YoY in the two prints after the July 2027 convention.
- **Shift —** ADP growth below ~2% or a benefits-and-claims ratio sustained
  above ~60%: the in-force block is repricing; revalue.
- **Shift —** a US equity bear market compressing ISP asset-based revenue —
  expected beta; revalue on the new asset base.
- **Shift —** a negative annual assumption review (a remeasurement loss) in
  Q3. Ordinarily that is one-time noise. If it is driven by higher
  mortality or lapse assumptions, revalue the in-force block's margin.
- **Shift —** capital return cut materially below ~80% of adjusted earnings
  without a named organic use.

**Reopen trigger:** 2026-11-05: pri-q3-sales-force-trend-and-adp-growth —
after the 11-04 AMC print: licensed force vs the flat-to-−2% guide,
new-license YoY (net of convention promotions), ADP growth ≥ ~3%, the
