# SKHY — SK hynix Inc. (NASDAQ ADS) — 2026-08-11

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $141.65/ADS.** Kill-thesis verdict: **SOUND** — 5 load-bearing conditions,
0 refuted, 3 UNKNOWN. Closest attack: the shareholder-return bazooka — a >$57B cash pile
(post-Kioxia, plus $26.2B IPO proceeds) and a program promised within Q3 could re-rate the
ADS regardless of parity math; its scale is unknowable today. Second-closest: at the *Seoul*
price with a sub-1.0 beta the flat-forever run lands near its hurdle — the pass on the KRX
line is beta-sensitive; the pass on the ADS (the security actually under verdict) is not,
surviving every parameterization tried. A data-integrity note from the attack: the displayed
forward PE of 3.45 is an artifact of stockanalysis mixing the ADS price with per-common
estimates (their psForward prints 0.002); the real forward multiple off Robinhood's Q3
estimate is ~6x annualized.
The business is the best version of itself in its history — HBM leader at ~62% share, 76%
operating margin, net cash — but the ADS price stacks two full-priced bets: (i) the most
extreme memory pricing ever printed persisting roughly forever (even peak-TTM FCF held flat
implies only ~9%/yr at the *Seoul* price, below the ~11% hurdle), and (ii) a 25–40% ADS
premium over the identical KRX share that exists only because the depositary facility's 2.5%
cap is exhausted — a scarcity artifact the company can dissolve by consent, worth roughly
−10%/yr of drag if it converges over three years.

The respectable counter: forward PE ~6x on street numbers, ~10 five-year LTAs with deposit
mechanisms, $44.8B net cash, HBM4 ramping with 2027 pricing "progressing smoothly," and a
shareholder-return announcement due within Q3. If the plateau holds and the premium is
ignored, the Seoul line is merely expensive, not absurd. The ADS buyer, however, pays ~$1.03T
for what Seoul prices at ~$735B.

## 2. Business

- **Creates**: DRAM (server, HBM, mobile, PC, graphics), NAND/SSD (incl. Solidigm eSSD).
  The scarce thing it sells in 2026 is HBM — stacked DRAM with TSV packaging that AI
  accelerators cannot ship without; HBM carried a >5x per-GB premium over conventional DRAM
  in 2025 (F-1, citing Gartner). Customers (Nvidia first among them) buy because SK hynix
  qualified HBM3E first, at scale, with yield — a time-to-qualification advantage, not a
  patent wall.
- **Captures**: memory ASPs — historically pure cycle-taking (2023: *negative* gross
  profit). Two capture upgrades in 2026: HBM sold under long negotiated contracts rather
  than spot, and ~10 LTAs of ~5-year terms with deposit mechanisms and varied pricing
  structures "designed to address price volatility" (Q2'26 call). Share of revenue under
  LTA: undisclosed. Q2'26 capture at the extreme: DRAM ASP +30% q/q, NAND +mid-50% q/q;
  operating margin 76.3%.
- **Protects**: DRAM is a 3-player oligopoly (>90% share — F-1/IDC); a competitive fab
  is a multi-$10B, multi-year object (their own Y2 = ₩35.2T). Within the oligopoly,
  though, share moves on every node transition: Samsung passed Nvidia's HBM4 qualification
  first (Feb–Mar 2026 trade press), and IDC-derived projections have SK hynix's HBM4 share
  at ~54% vs ~62% in HBM overall. The honest answer from its own history: the moat protects
  existence, not price — 2021→2023 revenue fell 24% and ₩12.4T of operating profit became
  −₩7.7T. Nothing structural prevented that; the LTA/floor structure that might is one
  quarter old and untested (same posture as SNDK's NBMs).

**Operating-leverage trajectory (KRW, consolidated K-IFRS):**

|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 43.0T | 12.4T | 29% |
| 2022 | 44.6T | 6.8T | 15% |

Quarterly through the boom: Q2'25 22.2T/9.2T → Q4'25 32.8T/19.2T → Q1'26 52.6T/37.6T →
Q2'26 79.3T/60.5T (76.3% OPM). Direction: violently positive — and demonstrably symmetric
(the same leverage ran in reverse 2021→2023). This is a fixed-cost commodity producer at a
price peak, not a structurally 70%-margin business.

## 3. Threads pulled

- **The ADS premium (the defining thread).** 1 ADS = 1/10 common (424B4). IPO Jul 10,
  2026 at $149 — largest-ever US listing by a foreign issuer (~$26.2B net, primary).
  Aug 11 closes: ADS $141.65; KRX common ₩1,425,000 at USDKRW ≈1,412.8 → parity ≈$100.9
  → **premium ≈40%** (press measured 16–51% since listing; ~25–33% late July). Mechanism
  confirmed: the deposit agreement caps common→ADS conversion; the ~2.5%-of-shares
  facility was exhausted at the IPO, so the KRX→ADS arbitrage leg is blocked (424B4 risk
  factor: re-deposit requires company consent and possibly a Korean registration filing;
  Seoul Economic Daily / Bloomberg coverage of the exhausted cap). Two-way conversion
  applications were to be accepted from Jul 29, but no timetable from Citibank (depositary)
  and the cap still binds. Consequence: the premium is a regulated-scarcity artifact.
  The company can widen the cap (and has motive: cheap USD currency); holders cannot
  defend it. ADS-only risk with no Seoul counterpart.
- **The ₩62.2T Q2 non-operating gain is Kioxia, one-off.** Q2 pretax ₩122.7T vs operating
  ₩60.5T; the gap is ₩63.3T of investment sale/valuation gains + ₩1.1T FX (press citing
  the earnings deck; the 6-K discloses only the totals). SK hynix's 2018 ₩3.9T Kioxia bet,
  sold/revalued into NAND-boom prices. TTM net income ($104.7B) and EPS ($20.16 shown by
  aggregators, $8.756 Q2 actual on Robinhood) are therefore not earnings power; core Q2
  net ≈ ₩47T after tax on operating results. PE 7.0 as displayed is a fiction twice over
  (one-off gain in E; KRX-based market cap in P while the ADS trades 40% higher).
- **Forward capex breaks the trailing FCF base.** TTM capex $24.9B. Guided 2026 capex:
  "high ₩40T range" (~$33B). Board-approved Aug 7 alone: Y2 Yongin phases 1–6 ₩35.2T
  (through Oct 2031) + M17 Cheongju ₩19.1T (through Apr 2031) — ₩54.3T (~$38B) of new
  fab shell commitments, 45% of total equity. Capacity races into a price peak are
  exactly how 2023 happened; management says "CapEx discipline," the filings say 29% and
  16% of equity in one board meeting.
- **Competitive erosion at the HBM4 handoff.** Samsung passed Nvidia HBM4 qual first and
  ships first (trade press, Feb–Aug 2026); Micron overtook Samsung in HBM overall.
  Projections: SK hynix ~54% of HBM4 vs ~62% HBM today. Aug 10–11 Korea coverage:
  brokers cutting 000660 target prices, "Samsung poised to increase share." The KRX line
  fell ~10% on the record Q2 print — the home market is already pricing share/cycle risk
  the ADS premium ignores.
- **Shareholder returns are (so far) trivial against the price.** Quarterly dividend
  ₩375/share (≈0.1%/yr yield); 15.3M treasury shares (~2.1%) cancelled earlier in 2026;
  "additional measures" promised within Q3 (Aug 7 6-K; on the call, specifics withheld
  citing ADR regulatory restrictions). At a $735B–$1,032B market cap, returns would need
  to be tens of billions to move the implied-return math.
- **Corporate actions in motion**: Solidigm pre-IPO raise ~₩5T under review (6-K,
  Aug 6); Chongqing packaging stake sale ~₩4T under review (6-K, Aug 10). Both are
  "no matters determined" clarifications with one-month update clocks.
- **Estimate-vs-actual pattern**: unavailable — only one reported quarter as a US
  listing (Q2'26 actual $8.756, no estimate on record; Q3 est $5.97, report 2026-11-13
  PM, tentative). GAAP cross-check vs sec_fundamentals.db impossible: no SEC annual

- **Dead ends**: not in composite.db universe, no candidate_appearances row, no

## 4. Valuation

Reverse DCF, levered TTM FCF paired with market cap; hurdle = rf 4.74% + beta 1.3 ×
ERP 4.90% = **11.1%** (rf and US ERP as of Aug 1, 2026, Damodaran; ERP
production-weighted Korea 4.87%/China 5.14% per the Jan 5, 2026 country table, ~75/25).

is a memory-cyclical comparable level, floored per the anchors rule. Base FCF $65.10B =
NCFO $90.03B − capex $24.93B (TTM, stockanalysis hover figures; SBC negligible at
~$152M). Market caps: ADS-implied $1,032.4B (728,865,500 commons × $141.65 × 10);
KRX-implied $735.2B (× ₩1,425,000 / 1,412.8). Net cash $44.8B ignored (conservative
the other way: netting it out raises implied returns by <40bp). ATM IV 76% → all
figures quoted to whole percents.

Not one scenario clears the hurdle; the ADS-price scenarios cluster around the US
*median* cost of capital (7.79%) and below — Damodaran's distribution clamp calls
that a strong pass regardless of story. The bull path's market-share sentence: +25/+10/0
puts revenue ≈$185B by 2028, roughly SK hynix's current ~1/3 share of a projected
~$500–600B combined DRAM+NAND TAM — it assumes held share, not gains, so the path is
not "bigger than the market"; it is merely priced for perpetuity. Terminal-risk check:
the F-1's dominant structural risk (cyclicality/oversupply, plus China exposure — Wuxi/
Dalian fabs under export-control regimes, CXMT entry) is exactly what 0% (or ≤inflation
2%) terminal growth embeds; any higher rate would not survive it. Reinvestment check:
base FCF ≈ core earnings (≈$65B), so growth-without-reinvestment fires for any positive
path — answered by the 0% terminals; the 2% terminals are repricing-only. Base-year
integrity caveats: (i) TTM NCFO may include LTA customer deposits (SNDK's Q4 alone had
$1.9B; SK hynix's split undisclosed) — the flat-FCF base is if anything overstated;
(ii) effective tax 21.6% ≈ marginal, no NOL flattery.

**The premium drag, separately:** an ADS buyer earns the Seoul return *minus* premium
decay. If today's ~40% premium converges over 3 years, that is ≈ −10%/yr on top of the
table above — the Seoul flat-forever 9% becomes roughly zero. Premium persistence is not
an assumption anyone can underwrite: it requires the company to keep its own conversion
cap shut.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would flip this pass toward interest)

- **ADS premium compresses to single digits** (cap widened, or Seoul rallies through
  it) — SHIFT: rerun the table at parity; the Seoul-price rows become the real rows.
- **Q3 shareholder-return program at scale** (all-in yield >3–4%/yr at this market
  cap, sustained) — SHIFT: changes the levered-return arithmetic directly.
- **LTA floor economics quantified** (share of revenue under floor, floor-price
  margin) showing downside EPS that alone supports the Seoul price — SHIFT; today it
  is undisclosed, exactly SNDK's posture.
- **Memory pricing holds through H1'27 with industry bit-supply growth visibly
  constrained** (capex discipline observable, CXMT/Samsung adds absorbed) — SHIFT
  toward the plateau scenario being real.
- For an owner, the sell-side breaks would be: HBM4 share materially below ~50%
  (Samsung/Micron wins at Nvidia), DRAM ASP declines while the ₩54T fab program
  proceeds unchanged, or the conversion cap widening while premium >20% — the last is
  a BREAK for the ADS specifically, whatever the business does.

  Comes from the H1'26 half-year report (due ~mid-Aug under K-IFRS deadlines).
- **LTA coverage** — share of revenue, floor levels, deposit balances in NCFO. Company

  nothing; it keeps the plateau scenario unprovable — does not kill the pass.

  the Citibank two-way timetable. No disclosure. This is the ADS premium's entire
  support; its opacity is itself a reason the premium deserves no value.
