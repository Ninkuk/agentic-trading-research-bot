## 2. Business

**Created:** converts wasting orebodies into metal at a cost below the metal
price. Harmony runs ~1.4–1.5Moz/yr of gold (FY26 guidance) from nine South African
deep-level mines (the deepest in the world), SA surface retreatment, and Hidden Valley
in PNG, plus first copper from the newly acquired CSA mine in Australia. There is no
customer-preference dimension — gold clears at the world price — so value creation
reduces to three inputs: orebody quality (37Moz reserves, ~136Moz resources — H1 FY26
call), mining skill at extreme depth (the stated basis for buying CSA, a hot,
ventilation-constrained 2,000m underground copper mine), and cost discipline
(>90% of costs rand-denominated; 55% labor under a five-year wage deal, 24% regulated
electricity — H1 FY26 call).

**Captured:** the spread between received price and all-in sustaining cost. H1
FY26: AISC $2,115/oz against a 9M average received price of $3,691/oz (+39% YoY) — a
~40% AISC margin. Management quantifies the torque: every R100,000/kg move in the gold
price is ~R1.5B of adjusted free cash flow (H1 FY26 call). Two deliberate dampers on
that torque: a rolling 36-month hedge program covering up to 30% of production (H1
realized hedge loss: R4.5B as gold ripped), and a dividend policy paying 30–50% of net
free cash *after all capex* — growth is funded before shareholders see cash.

**Protected:** no moat in the price, partial moats in the assets. The orebodies
are irreplaceable and permitted; deep-level mining competence is scarce (few operators
can run 4km-deep mines, and Harmony is explicitly exporting that skill to CSA); the
balance sheet is net cash (R1.3B at Mar 31, 2026 — Q3 update) with first-time public
ratings BB/Ba1/BB. What is *not* protected: the SA underground assets sit high on the
global cost curve (the 20-F's #1 market risk is, in the company's own words, a gold
price falling below cash cost plus sustaining capex for a sustained period), power
comes from Eskom, and the reserve base depletes — which is exactly why management is
spending today's gold windfall on copper (CSA + Eva + Wafi-Golpu, targeting ~40% copper
production by FY35).
