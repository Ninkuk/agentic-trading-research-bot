# WMG — Warner Music Group Corp. — 2026-08-17

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Kill-thesis verdict: UNPROVEN (5 conditions, 0 refuted, 2 load-bearing unknowns).
Ownership: PASS at $26.37.** One of three global majors in a consolidated industry, ~2/3
catalog royalty revenue, operating leverage genuinely turned (FY21→TTM revenue +38%, op
income +106%), and a real structural pricing change — contractual per-subscriber-minimum
(PSM) escalators now cover 88% of subscription streaming revenue, 0% two years ago. But
the market cap implies **9.23% on middle-of-road assumptions vs a 10.26% hurdle (−103
bps)**, and turning the spread positive requires believing management's own 8%-growth
model — i.e. paying today for AI-licensing and Bain-JV upside tagged possible-tier. Two
load-bearing conditions are unverifiable in principle today: whether gen-AI dilutes the
streaming royalty pool over the terminal horizon, and whether WMG's share is actually
holding (the only public metric, Luminate US, read weak in Q3; management's rebuttal
rests on global figures it says are "not publicly available to you"). Base scenario
clears the hurdle near **$22.90**.

**Closest attack (kill-thesis):** condition 5, the share claim. The Q3 subscription
decomposition (+12% adj = 6–7pp subscribers + 3.5pp price + ~1pp share) attributes a
point of growth to share gains measurable only in non-public global data, in the same
quarter the public US Luminate share fell. Second-closest: PSM durability — contract
tenors are undisclosed, and Spotify's 2024 audiobook-bundling maneuver (which cut
publishers' mechanical royalties) is precedent for a DSP re-engineering royalty
structure when its incentives flip. Options timing check: skipped — no dated claim in
the thesis; the Nov chain is anyway illiquid (path-2 stopgap; OI 3/2, spread ~78% of
mark; see §4).

## 2. Business

**Created.** For listeners: access to a million-plus-song catalog (Atlantic, Warner
Records, Elektra, Parlophone; Madonna→Charli XCX) through every DSP. For artists: A&R
capital (advances = $1.79B on balance sheet), global marketing/distribution
infrastructure, and now AI-era protections (deepfake takedowns written into distributor
contracts, per the Q3 call). The artist is the true customer; the DSPs are the channel.

**Captured.** Royalty streams, unpacked: recorded-music subscription streaming ($758M in
Q3, now with contractual PSM floors that escalate on a set cadence across 88% of that
revenue), ad-supported streaming ($243M), physical ($137M), artist services/concert
promotion ($224M), licensing ($111M); plus Warner Chappell publishing ($377M — performance,
digital, mechanical, sync). Catalog (older than 18 months) is ~2/3 of revenue at higher
margin (Q3 call, CFO). Segment margins: Recorded Music adj OIBDA 25.3%, Publishing 28.9%.

**Protected.** Copyright — legal exclusivity over irreplaceable recordings and
compositions for the term of copyright, decades. A competitor cannot re-record Led
Zeppelin. New-release share is contested every quarter (that part is a talent-acquisition
business, not a moat), but the catalog cash flow is structurally defended. The oligopoly
structure (UMG, Sony, WMG) concentrates licensing leverage against DSPs — the PSM
escalators are the observable output of that leverage. What the moat does NOT stop:
gen-AI music diluting the per-stream royalty pool (§5), or DSPs re-engineering royalty
mechanics at renewal.

## 5. Falsifiers

- **Break:** a major-DSP renewal that drops or waives PSM escalators (coverage falling
  from 88%), or public disclosure that escalator cadence was conceded back.
- **Break:** an appellate-level US ruling that training on copyrighted recordings is fair
  use without license — the terminal assumption fails; sell regardless of quarter.
- **Shift:** adjusted subscription-streaming growth below ~8% for two consecutive
  quarters without a called-out comp (the decomposition's subscriber+price floor implies
  ~9–10%; below 8% means share or price is leaking) — revalue.
- **Shift:** FY27 margin guidance below the 150–200bp cadence, or the $300M annualized
  restructuring savings failing to appear in OIBDA — revalue.
- **Shift:** a second abrupt senior-finance departure within 12 months, or the CFO search
  resolving badly — the delivery narrative loses its institutional claim.
- **Shift:** AI-track consumption share on any major DSP moving from ~1–3% toward ~10% —
  the dilution mechanism activating.
