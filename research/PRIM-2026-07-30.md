# PRIM — Primoris Services Corporation — 2026-07-30

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at today's price ($85.66, market cap ~$4.65B).** A sound and genuinely
demand-advantaged infrastructure contractor whose stock halved on six distressed
solar projects and two guidance cuts in eight weeks; at this price the market
already pays ~9–10%/yr *conditional on* a recovery that management's own last two
forecasts failed to underwrite, and the deciding evidence (Q2 print, Aug 4;
2H renewables bookings) does not exist yet. Kill-thesis verdict: **UNPROVEN** —
7 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 4 UNKNOWN. The attack that came closest:
the June guide may be a new-CEO kitchen sink, making "fair" actually "modestly
cheap" — crediting it requires trusting the third forecast in eight weeks from a
process whose first two failed.

Reopen: 2026-08-05 (Q2 print lands AMC Aug 4) — in-guide + no additions to the
distressed set + renewables book-to-bill ≥ 1 reopens the ownership question.

## 2. Business

Two segments (10-K FY2025). **Utilities** (~$2.0B/yr next-12-month backlog):
construction and maintenance of gas and electric distribution, transmission, and
substations, mostly under multi-year MSAs — recurring, relationship-priced,
gross margins 9–12%. **Energy** (~$3.3B next-12-month backlog): EPC for
utility-scale solar (14 GW installed), battery storage, natural-gas generation,
pipelines, industrial. PayneCrest ($422M cash, closed ~April 2026) adds
electrical/data-center interior work with a hyperscaler relationship.

- **Created:** scarce self-perform craft-labor capacity (18.5k employees) in a
  power-demand supercycle (AI data centers, onshoring, electrification). The
  customer buys certainty of crew availability and turnkey delivery.
- **Captured:** two mechanisms, not one. MSA unit-rate services (recurring,
  ~$7.5B of total backlog) and fixed-price/negotiated EPC projects (higher
  margin, higher variance — the blowup channel). Gas-gen work is structured
  open-book reimbursable → GMP only at mature engineering, turbines
  owner-furnished (JPM conference, 6/24) — deliberately lower-risk capture.
- **Protected:** modestly. Capacity + incumbency + track record in a
  supply-short market is a real mechanism but a cyclical one; the solar blowup
  itself proves execution is not a durable edge. Thin margins (10.4% gross,
  5.0% operating TTM) mean protection is operational discipline, which just
  failed publicly.

Operating leverage 2021→2025 ran **positive**: revenue +117% ($3.50B→$7.57B),
operating income +142% ($170M→$412M), margin 4.86%→5.43% (stockanalysis
income-statement history). TTM is rolling over (opinc $366M).

## 3. Threads pulled

**What broke (resolved).** Six solar projects, all negotiated awards entering
backlog in 2H-2024 in first-time geographies; weather, labor productivity,
remob/demob spirals, permitting/ground-disturbance friction (Q1 call, 5/6).
Q4 call disclosed one project; Q1 call expanded to six and cut FY26 EBITDA
~$110M; June 22 8-K cut again — **$480–500M → $275–325M adjusted EBITDA**,
renewables revenue $3.0B → $2.1B, COO terminated same day, third-party expert
(50 yrs) reviewed estimates-to-complete across the whole portfolio. Set went
1 → 6 → 6; cost went up three times. Completion: two done, one early Q3, two
late Q3, one (largest revision, least mature, weather-exposed) December 2026.

**Is it ring-fenced? (UNKNOWN).** The third-party review is real evidence, and
liquidated damages are "conservatively baked in" (JPM conf). But management
claimed containment at Q4 and Q1 and was wrong both times, and the E&C base
rate is against containment — Fluor, Granite, Tutor Perini all took follow-on
charges for 4–8+ quarters after first disclosure.

**Demand (intact ex-renewables; renewables UNKNOWN).** Backlog $11.64B at Q1'26
(10-Q) plus **$2.0B new Energy awards in Q2** (8-K 6/22) — explicitly gas-gen,
industrial, electric/data-center, *not* renewables. Gas-gen: ~$800M verbal
awards, $7.1B funnel (Q1 call). Renewables funnel "$15B" is self-reported CRM;
verbal awards are not contracts; no renewables bookings evidence since Q1. The
48E/OB3 re-engineering pause is why clients delayed — "never seen anything as
disruptive" (CFO, JPM conf). Post-safe-harbor demand is the structural question.

**Management (mixed).** CEO Koti Vadlamudi new since Nov 2025 (30 yrs Jacobs);
the six projects predate him. COO termination "without cause," explicitly not
accounting-related (8-K). **No meaningful insider buying post-crash** — the
only Form 4 since June 23 is a 19.5-share (~$1.7k) director purchase (7/15,
McCallister); May Form 144s were sales. The company bought $50M of stock at avg
**$111.29** — all before the June collapse; $100M authorization remains.

**Legal/accounting tail (UNKNOWN).** Securities class action filed, class
period Aug 5 2025 – Jun 22 2026 (lead-plaintiff deadline Sep 21, 2026). The
10-K's forward-looking bullets already name "the results of the review of prior
period accounting on certain projects" and "governmental investigations and/or
inquiries." POC estimate revisions are normal; a restatement would not be.

**FCF quality (thread landed).** 2024–25 FCF ($382M/$340M) was flattered by the
AR securitization ramp ($150M → $250M max commitment, 10-K) and boom-time
mobilization advances; both unwind as renewables shrinks $900M. Q1'26 OCF was
negative on a ~$100M payables-timing swing (CFO, Q1 call).

**Balance sheet (survives).** Debt $928M, cash $362M, net debt $567M ≈ 1.9x
mid-guide trough EBITDA; revolver undrawn at Q1 (10-Q); trailing interest

## 5. Falsifiers

  six-project set and no new charge on the December project.
- Renewables book-to-bill ≥ 1 in 2H-2026 (management's own claim to beat).

seventh distressed project, CFO departure, an accounting restatement, or a
covenant amendment/waiver.
