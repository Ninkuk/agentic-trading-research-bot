# GDDY — GoDaddy Inc. — 2026-07-30

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Kill-thesis verdict: UNPROVEN** (5 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 2
unverifiable). **Ownership call: PASS at ~$99.80** — not because the price is
wrong, but because the evidence that decides the thesis hasn't printed yet.

The thesis: at ~8× levered FCF ($13.2B market cap / $1.64B TTM FCF), the market
prices GoDaddy as a terminally declining business, while the reported numbers
describe a stable, price-driven compounder — a reverse DCF implies ~12%/yr even
in a decline scenario. What is unproven is precisely the floor: whether the
bookings deceleration (Q1: +2% cc vs revenue +6%) is the self-inflicted,
temporary mix-shift management claims, or the true demand signal. The deciding
evidence lands tonight (Q2 print, AMC 2026-07-30) and over the next two
quarters (the untested $4.99 one-year cohort hits first renewal Q4'26–Q1'27).

**Timing note:** researched hours before a binary event the options market
prices at ±9–11%. The stock is also +40% off its June 12 low ($71.59),
including +20% in the four sessions before the print — driver unidentified.

## 2. Business

**Created** — a one-stop web presence for ~20.4M non-technical micro-business
customers: domain + website + email + commerce + human/AI support ("Care"), at
$246/yr average. The customer surplus is real for the target base: they pay to
not think about infrastructure. 60% of traffic arrives direct on brand
(Evercore fireside, transcribed).

**Captured** — subscriptions in two segments (FY2025 10-K): Core Platform
($3.06B, +5% — domains as acquisition funnel, hosting, aftermarket) and
Applications & Commerce ($1.89B, +14%, 45% segment EBITDA margin — the attach
engine). Growth is 100% ARPU (+9% to $246); customer count is flat (+13k in
Q1 vs Dec-25, press release). Mechanics: renewal-cycle price increases,
bundling, second-product attach (Airo cohorts attach ~30% faster, mgmt claim).
Operating leverage is strongly positive: revenue 2021→2025 $3.82B→$4.95B
(+30%), operating income $382M→$1,127M (~3×), op margin 10%→24%.

**Protected** — a mechanism, not a fortress: brand + integrated support for
non-technical users + auto-renew/switching inertia (email and site tied to the
registrar). Evidence it holds: Cloudflare has sold domains at cost since 2018
and GoDaddy's ARPU still compounds high-single-digits at ~85% retention.
Boundary: technical users leak out; the moat only covers the non-technical
base — which is also the base most exposed if AI agents make "your own
website" optional.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make me sell / stay out)

1. Q2 (tonight) or Q3 constant-currency bookings growth below revenue growth —
   management's own parity-or-better guide broken.
2. Retention slipping below ~85%, or the $4.99 cohort's first renewal
   (Q4'26–Q1'27) showing materially worse renewal than legacy cohorts.
3. Customer count declining >1%/quarter ex-divestiture, or ARPU growth <5%.
4. FY26 FCF guide (~$1.8B) cut for demand (not timing) reasons.
5. Buybacks suspended while the stock sits under ~10× FCF, or a large
   dilutive/levering acquisition — the per-share compounding leg breaks.

Flip-to-SOUND: two consecutive quarters of cc bookings ≥ revenue growth plus a
cohort-renewal disclosure holding retention ≥85%.
