## 2. Business

**Created:** F5 sits in the data path in front of mission-critical
applications — load balancing, traffic management, WAF/API/bot security —
across on-prem, cloud and edge (BIG-IP systems and software, NGINX,
Distributed Cloud SaaS). The customer gets uptime, throughput and one policy
and security layer across an average of 22 environments (Goldman 9/9);
security capabilities serve 80% of the Fortune 500; Distributed Cloud has
1,700+ customers and 33% of the top 1,000 (up from 24%). Q3 FY26 (8-K
Ex-99.1, 2026-07-27): revenue $865M +11%, product +19% (eighth straight
double-digit quarter), systems +32%, software +7%, services +3%; remaining
performance obligations $2.2B with 58.8% recognized inside 12 months
(10-Q).

**Captured:** three mechanisms — (a) high-margin hardware systems
(rSeries/VELOS) at cycle peak, with higher-performance units carrying
above-average gross margin and a "pricing umbrella" from aggressive
competitors enabling takeouts (Goldman 9/9); (b) software subscriptions
(~90% of software revenue; multi-year FCP agreements, roughly two-thirds
recognized upfront); (c) SaaS/managed services (Distributed Cloud, ARR
growing, turning ratable in FY27). Blended TTM: 81.9% gross margin, 25.0%
GAAP operating margin, 28.3% ROIC, 29.3% FCF margin (statistics page).

**Protected:** switching cost on the data path — ripping out the traffic
layer in front of a thousand production apps risks downtime on all of them
at once — plus decades of iRules/policy configuration in the installed base.
The mechanism was tested: a nation-state actor stole BIG-IP source code
(8-K 2025-10-15, CISA emergency directive), management guided FY26 to 0–4%
on expected remediation disruption, and the year is finishing at 9–10%.
Honest limit, unchanged: in FY19–FY24 this moat protected the base without
producing growth (FY24 +0.1%); it defends the estate, it does not by itself
create expansion.

**Control:** one class of common stock, one vote per share, no controlling
holder. Vanguard 13.09%, BlackRock 10.90%, State Street 5.46% (DEF 14A,
2026-01-26, record date 2026-01-06); insiders 0.55% (statistics). CEO
François Locoh-Donou added the Chairman role in March 2026 with a lead
independent director to follow (8-K 2025-10-27). The structure forecloses
nothing — an unsolicited bid or an activist has a clear path.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue FY21 → TTM $2,603M →
$3,309M (+27%); GAAP operating income $444M → $826M (+86%); GAAP operating
margin 17.1% → 25.0%, with a dip in FY22 (15.5%) before the climb
(stockanalysis `/financials/income-statement/`, TTM dated 2026-06-30).
GAAP is the SBC-inclusive view (SBC $250.8M TTM, 7.6% of revenue).
