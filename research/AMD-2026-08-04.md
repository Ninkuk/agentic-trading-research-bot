# AMD — 2026-08-04

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at today's price** ($518.58 official close; ~$473.83 after hours following the
Q2'26 print, ≈$772.6B market cap). The business is superb and executing exactly as
management promised; the price already pays for management's own extraordinary
trajectory — granting FCF rising ~6× by 2031 on an 8-year fade, the implied return is
**~9–10%/yr before, ~9% after, warrant dilution** — thin pay for perfection-track
execution on a name that realizes ~81% vol. Kill-thesis verdict: **UNPROVEN**
(4 load-bearing conditions in the pass, 0 refuted, 1 unverifiable — whether the
management-track path is really the ceiling of defensible optimism). Run timing:
written the evening of the Q2'26 AMC print, after the release and call were public.

## 2. Business

**Created:** compute performance per dollar and per watt across CPUs (EPYC/Ryzen),
GPUs (Instinct/Radeon), and adaptive silicon (Xilinx) — now packaged at rack scale
(Helios: EPYC Venice + MI450 + Pensando networking + ROCm). Customers come for
tokens-per-dollar (claimed up to 30% better than the competition) and for being the
only credible second source against Nvidia.

**Captured:** chip and system ASPs. Q2'26: revenue $11.5B (+50% YoY), data center
$6.7B (58% of revenue, +107%), non-GAAP GM 56%, GAAP GM 54%. Segment op margins:
data center 31%, client+gaming 15%, embedded 40% (all Q2'26 8-K). Distinct engines:
server CPU share gains from Intel (5th straight record quarter, both units AND ASPs
up double digits), Instinct accelerators (more than doubled YoY), client CPUs (+23%),
embedded (+19%).

**Protected:** x86 server incumbency + a decade of execution cadence (chiplets let
new nodes ramp on fewer wafers); ROCm ecosystem finally at an "inflection" (3M+
models run out of box; 10× open-source contribution growth — management's framing);
customer co-design lock-in (OpenAI 6GW + warrant, Meta, Anthropic 2GW, Microsoft).
Against Nvidia the moat is *second-source economics*, not software superiority —
the 10-K names Nvidia's proprietary ecosystem as the structural threat. That
asymmetry is real and is the honest answer.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would flip PASS → look again)

- FCF conversion catching up to non-GAAP net income for two consecutive quarters
  while data-center growth holds ≥ guide (reopen logged: 2026-11-03 print).
- A drawdown that reprices the implied return above ~12–13% on the same mgmt-track
  path (mechanically: market cap ≲ $600B, ≈ $370/share, all else equal).
- Evidence the 2028+ demand path is contracted, not projected — e.g. disclosed
  take-or-pay customer commitments covering MI500-era volumes.

For a holder, the sell-side falsifiers would be: hyperscaler capex guidance cuts;
Helios yield/GM slippage below the 56% guide; anchor-customer deferral (OpenAI
funding); inventory growing faster than revenue for 2+ quarters; server-CPU growth
decelerating below the +70% 2027 guide.
