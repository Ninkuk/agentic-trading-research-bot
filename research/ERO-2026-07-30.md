# ERO — Ero Copper Corp. — 2026-07-30

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $27.05.** Quality operator with real production growth, but the price
already embeds the ramp plus record copper: even if spot copper (~$13,300/t LME,
all-time high) holds AND the ramp delivers, the implied return is only ~11%/yr —
equal to the company's WACC — and at the $4.60/lb long-term planning price Ero's
own Furnas PEA uses, it drops to ~6–8%. Kill-thesis verdict on this PASS:
**SOUND** (4 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 0 unknown); the attack that
came closest was spot-copper forward earnings power (5.6× 2027 consensus EPS if
record copper is the new floor). Not researched into the print blindly: Q2
lands **Aug 5 AMC**, six days out, with guidance H2-weighted and unproven.

## 2. Business

- **Creates:** copper concentrate from three Brazilian assets — Caraíba
  underground complex (Bahia, 36.0kt Cu 2025, 1.09% grade), Tucumã open pit
  (Pará, 28.3kt 2025, 1.79% grade, commercial July 2025), Xavantina gold
  (Mato Grosso, 52.3koz incl. concentrate sales). Grades are genuinely
  above-average; that, not a moat, is the cost position.
- **Captures:** LME-linked concentrate sales; TTM revenue $924M, EBITDA $446M,
  C1 guided $2.15–2.35/lb 2026. Price taker — margin = copper price minus cost
  curve position.
- **Protects:** nothing structural. High grade + operating competence; no
  pricing power. The protection question for a miner is reserve life and cost
  quartile, both adequate but not exceptional.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would flip PASS → buy)

- Pullback or copper normalization such that implied return at $4.60/lb
  copper reaches ≥ ~12%/yr (roughly: today's guided-ramp case at a low-$20s
  price, or better assets at this price).
- Delivered 2026 guidance + Pilar shaft commissioned on schedule (2027) +
  a Furnas funding plan without material equity issuance, while the price
  still implies ≥ ~10% at normalized copper.
- Credible reset of the industry's own planning price above ~$5/lb (majors
  and Ero underwriting reserves there, not just spot).
