## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $71.66.** TechnipFMC is a genuinely improved, differentiated subsea franchise — but the price already pays for the optimistic path: even on management's raised FY26 FCF guide with continued growth, the reverse DCF implies **~7.7%/yr**, and only **~3.6%** on a mid-cycle base. Kill-thesis verdict: **SOUND** (5 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 1 bounded UNKNOWN); the attack that came closest was the permanent-re-rate case — a disclosed forward FCF base ≥$2B would move the arithmetic to ~10% and reopen the name.

## 2. Business

**Creates value:** designs, manufactures, installs, and services the subsea production hardware (trees, manifolds, umbilicals/risers/flowlines) that lets producers develop offshore fields. Its integrated single-contract model (iEPCI) collapses what was historically two-to-three contracts (equipment supplier + installation contractor) into one, cutting time-to-first-oil ("first oil within two years" on the Vår Energi brownfield portfolio) and restoring schedule certainty — which is what lets clients sanction marginal fields at all (Q2'26 call).

**Captures value:** (a) fixed-price integrated EPCI project revenue — subsea adj. EBITDA margin 23.2% in Q2'26, a post-demerger record; (b) life-of-field Subsea Services — an OEM-style 20-30-year aftermarket annuity attached to every direct award, tied to the installed base; (c) Surface Technologies (wellheads; ~$1.1B/yr, 18% margins). 80% of business is **direct-awarded**, not competitively tendered — pricing power is structural for that slice (Q2'26 call).

**Protects value:** the only in-house combination of subsea production systems + umbilicals/flowlines + installation vessels under one contract (competitors need alliances — OneSubsea/Subsea7); Subsea 2.0 configure-to-order platform at 80% of new orders / ~50% of revenue; early engagement up to a year before architecture decisions, which converts to direct awards; a concentrated oligopoly. Weaknesses: a cyclical end market it does not control, and fixed-price execution risk (its own 10-K: "We may lose money on fixed-price contracts").

## 5. Falsifiers (what reopens the name)

- Price near **~$52** (market cap ~$20.4B), where the guided-FCF run implies ≥10%/yr.
- Disclosed evidence of a forward FCF base **≥$2B** (e.g. 2027 guidance with the order step-up booked and margins held ≥23%).

Conversely, the pass is *confirmed cheaper* if: H2'26 inbound misses the $10B target, the 2027 step-up slips, or a fixed-price charge appears.

## 6. UNKNOWNs

- **Saipem7 effect on pricing discipline** — closes ~Q3 2026; both directions arguable.
- **Capex trajectory** if 2027 greenfield orders require vessel/capacity additions (management currently says no: "you don't need to be building or buying assets right now").
