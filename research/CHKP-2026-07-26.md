## 2. Business

**Created:** Check Point sells "prevention-first" enterprise security: block
the attack before it executes, rather than detect-and-respond after. The
original and still-largest piece is network security — firewalls/gateways
(the category CHKP invented in 1994) — sold alongside endpoint, cloud
(CloudGuard), email (Harmony Email, ex-Avanan), exposure management (CTEM,
via the 2024 Cyberint acquisition), SASE (via the 2023 Perimeter 81
acquisition), and now AI security (2025 Lakera acquisition), all fed by one
threat-intelligence backbone (ThreatCloud AI). The customer gets:
independently verified top-tier block rates (management cites NSS
Labs/Miercom 99.9% known-CVE prevention — vendor-selected, so low-confidence
as an unbiased benchmark) and one console across a widening set of security
domains rather than integrating a dozen point products.

**Captured:** Historically a license + maintenance-renewal annuity on a large
embedded appliance base; increasingly a subscription annuity (Security
Subscriptions revenue: $184M/qtr in 2021 → $323M/qtr TTM, source:
`/stocks/CHKP/metrics/revenue-by-segment/`) sold both to the existing base
(cross-sell into email/CTEM/SASE) and as new-logo bookings in the
faster-growing pillars. Appliance/product revenue is the legacy,
lower-growth, now-declining leg.

**Protected:** The classic moat here is switching cost: ripping out a
production firewall/network-security estate is operationally risky, so the
installed base is sticky (management: "renewal business continued to be
stable" even while new business stalled, Q1 2026 call) — a mechanism, not a
label. But the protection is visibly weaker than it once was: CHKP is a
**late entrant via acquisition**, not organic build, into the fastest-growing
adjacent categories (SASE, CTEM, AI security) where Zscaler, Palo Alto
Networks, CrowdStrike and Fortinet are already established or moving faster.
Management's own Q1 2026 framing — a go-to-market reorg, a fired/replaced CRO
(Itai Greenberg → Sherif Seddik) and four leadership hires in one quarter —
is itself evidence the "protect" side needed active repair, not a static
strength. **If there is no crisp answer for why a competitor can't match the
newer pillars faster than CHKP can build them, that silence is the finding**;
the honest answer here is "the legacy base buys time, not certainty."

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue $2,166.8M (2021) →
$2,756.0M (TTM); operating income $907.5M (2021) → $820.7M (TTM); operating
margin 41.9% → 29.8%. SG&A grew 55% ($708.5M→$1,095.4M) and R&D grew 59%
($292.7M→$464.5M) against 27% revenue growth over the same span; stock-based
comp nearly doubled ($120.3M→$224.2M TTM). Source:
`/stocks/CHKP/financials/income-statement/` and `/cash-flow-statement/`, live
2026-07-26. This is the mechanical explanation for why net income still rose
(non-operating interest income on the cash pile, plus a lower effective tax
rate) while the operating business did not improve.
