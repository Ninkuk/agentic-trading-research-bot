## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Pass at $13.12 (pre-market).** kill-thesis: **FLAWED** — 6 load-bearing bull
conditions, **3 refuted, 3 UNKNOWN**. Even down 26%, the price does not compensate on
honest bases: the ~14% as-reported implied return leans on a TTM FCF base ($863M) that
the company's own Q3 guide disavows, and the SBC-expensed (~6.7%), cash-netted (~8.0%),
and forward-run-rate (~7.1%) scenarios all sit **below the 9.18% hurdle** — two of them
below the ~7.8% US median cost of capital. The bull case now requires re-acceleration
evidence that does not exist (new products in alpha/beta, new leaders who "need runway"),
against a guided first-ever revenue decline in a growing digital ad market. This remains
a name to re-run after the Q3 print (2026-11-05, unverified), not to own on a broken base.

Thesis attacked: largest independent buy-side programmatic platform, retention >95% a
decade, $1.05B net cash, now priced at ~7× stale TTM FCF after a −80% one-year drawdown —
cheap **if** the decline is cyclical (CPG/autos macro), the fee level holds, and FCF
stabilizes near the trailing base. All three "ifs" failed the gauntlet (refuted or
unverifiable).

## 3. Threads pulled

- **The reopen thread — cyclical or structural?** Management's macro case: CPG+autos
  ≈25% of the platform, pressured by tariffs/low-income squeeze (P&G, GM quotes);
  political midterm spend *helped* Q2 (so organic ex-political was worse than +3%).
  Against macro-only: intl is thriving (EMEA/APAC +~30% YTD, China +100% YTD, CTV +50%
  in both regions) while the total declines — with US ≈83% of revenue, the Q3 guide
  implies the **US business declining roughly −18% to −19% YoY** (derived: intl ~14% of
  Q3'25 base growing ~25% leaves US $521M vs ~$636M; assumption-stated estimate). A
  −18% US decline in a US digital ad market that every scaled platform is still growing
  in (low-confidence general context, not fetched this run) is not a macro story; it is
  share and/or price. Green conceded execution: "we didn't execute as well as we could
  have" and the cheap-buying migration. Asked directly to decompose macro vs execution
  vs competitive (Helfstein), management declined to quantify. **The macro-only frame is
  refuted; the split remains undisclosed.**
- **The first non-GAAP EPS miss in the 8-quarter window.** $0.34 vs $0.37 est (Q1'26
  met exactly; the six quarters before all beat) [broker tier]. The managed-guidance
  pattern the prior run flagged has now broken twice in 18 months — both times the
  stock repriced −25%+ overnight.
- **The FCF base is flattered by the decline itself.** H1'26 NCFO $545.4M contains a
  net ~+$120M working-capital release (AR −$548.1M vs AP −$427.9M) — the AR/AP

  even before the margin guide-down. Q2 standalone FCF was $136M (CFO, on-call).

  "credible and grounded in the data," "not assuming any meaningful improvement,"
  "visibility is somewhat more limited than it has been." A first guide from a new CFO

  does not exist until the Q3 print.
