## 2. Business

**Created:** keeps mission-critical applications available and secure across
on-prem, cloud, and edge — one control point ("front door") for delivery +
security policy across hybrid environments. Customers get uptime and a
single policy surface instead of stitching point products (10-K; Q3 FY26
call).

**Captured:** three distinct mechanisms — systems (hardware ADCs, $240M in
Q3, +32% YoY), software (BIG-IP subscriptions, NGINX, Distributed Cloud
SaaS — $223M, +7%, 90% subscription), and attached services/support ($402M,
+3%). Recurring sources = 69% of revenue (Q3 FY26 call, CFO).

**Protected:** switching costs on the installed estate — decades of embedded
config/policy (iRules) in front of applications that cannot go down; refresh
happens in place and expands ("refresh plus"). This is a mechanism, not a
label — but it defends the installed base rather than winning cloud-native
greenfield, which caps the terminal question rather than answering it.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** FY21→TTM revenue $2,603M→$3,309M
(+27%) while GAAP operating income $394M→$812M (+106%); GAAP operating
margin 15.1%→24.5% (stockanalysis `/financials/`, TTM row dated 2026-06-30,
post-print). GAAP is the SBC-inclusive view; non-GAAP op margin 35%.
