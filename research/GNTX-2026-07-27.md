## 2. Business

- **Creates:** auto-dimming interior/exterior mirrors (~90% share; competitor
  Magna Mirrors) plus electronics content per vehicle — FDM, HomeLink, driver/
  cabin monitoring; OEMs get automotive-PPM-quality electronics from one
  vertically integrated Michigan supplier. Non-auto (aerospace dimmable windows,
  fire protection, Klipsch/Onkyo audio via VOXX) now ~14% of revenue (Q2'26 call).
- **Captures:** per-unit hardware pricing with content-per-vehicle mix
  (advanced features >75% of Q2 launches). No recurring revenue.
- **Protects:** electrochromic chemistry + 30 years of OEM qualification +
  vertical integration. Real mechanism for mirrors — Magna with more resources
  holds minority share. **Unproven for DMS/ICMS**, where Bosch/Continental/
  Smart Eye and Chinese suppliers compete; CEO concedes those margins run
  "slightly below corporate average" (Q2'26 call).
