# SEZL — Sezzle Inc. — 2026-07-30

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $155.12** ($5.22B market cap). Sezzle is a genuinely and unusually
profitable subprime-BNPL platform executing very well, but the price already
assumes years of 30%+ compounding: reverse DCF on guided growth implies only
**~6–8%/yr**, and the load-bearing fee and credit legs are unverifiable
forward. **kill-thesis verdict: FLAWED** — the valuation condition is refuted
by arithmetic; 5 of 7 load-bearing conditions are UNKNOWN-class
(`research/verdicts.log` 2026-07-30). Load-bearing condition count: **7**.
Repairable: the same business at roughly ≤$105 (~10%+ implied) is a different
conversation; reopen on the Q2 print (2026-08-06).

## 2. Business

**Created.** Instant, mostly interest-free installment credit (pay-in-four /
-five, 33-day weighted-average life) for US/Canadian "value" consumers who
are subprime or credit-invisible — plus, via the Sezzle Anywhere subscription
and virtual card, the ability to use that credit at any merchant. The
customer surplus is real: access to credit the banking system won't price,
without revolving-debt mechanics. Purchase frequency 7.1x/quarter (from 6.1x
a year ago) says customers come back (Q1'26 call).

**Captured** — five distinct mechanisms (FY2025 10-K, Note 2):
- Merchant processing + partner/interchange income: $102.2M (22.7% of revenue)
- Subscriptions (Premium + Anywhere): $99.4M (22.1%)
- Consumer fees (card-payment "late saver", failed payment, reschedule,
  On-Demand finance charges): $131.9M (29.3%)
- Late payment fees: $74.0M (16.4%) — **tripled** from $25.2M in 2024
- Pagaya take-rate on long-term lending referrals (no credit risk; new 2026)

Net: ~46% of revenue is consumer-paid fees, not merchant- or
subscription-paid. The Munger-quoting "aligned with the consumer" narrative
(Q1'26 call closing) sits uneasily on that mix.

**Protected** — thin, and the silence is the answer. Underwriting data on
repeat subprime borrowers (proprietary Prophet Score; book turns ~11x/yr so
credit learning is fast) and subscription attach are real but replicable.
Multi-tenanting is the BNPL norm; nothing structural stops Cash App Afterpay
or Klarna from pricing the same utility at zero. Sezzle's own antitrust suit
against Shopify (filed 2025-06-09, MTD ruling pending — Q1'26 10-Q Item 1)
is first-party evidence that bigger players can foreclose distribution. What
protects Sezzle today is niche neglect and execution speed, not a mechanism.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make an owner sell)

- Provision >3% of GMV for two consecutive quarters, or delinquency buckets
  rising while GMV decelerates (vintage deterioration under mix shift)
- Any binding late-fee/consumer-fee cap (CFPB posture reversal, state action,
  or a US echo of the Canadian provincial prohibitions)
- Subscriber count declining Q/Q (the growth engine is subscription attach)
- Revenue yield below ~10% of GMV without offsetting provision improvement
- ILC charter denial **coincident with** a true-lender enforcement action
- April 2027 refinance at materially worse terms (>200bp)
- Youakim pledge foreclosure/forced sales (Form 4 cluster without 10b5-1)

## 6. UNKNOWNs
