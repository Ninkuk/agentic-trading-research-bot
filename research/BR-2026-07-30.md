## 2. Business

- **Creates:** solves the many-to-many problem between ~1,000 brokers and virtually every public issuer/fund for 200M+ retail accounts — proxy distribution, voting, regulatory disclosure — at mutualized cost (RBC conf, 2026-06-09). GTO adds back/middle-office processing that lets banks retire legacy stacks.
- **Captures:** per-position regulatory processing fees set by the NYSE fee schedule (issuer pays, broker chooses the vendor — payer/chooser split); recurring per-account and per-communication fees; GTO SaaS/license; low-to-no-margin distribution pass-through; event-driven (proxy contests, fund communications). 98% revenue retention, $430M recurring backlog (Q3 FY26 call).
- **Protects:** SEC/NYSE rules *compel* broker distribution at scheduled fees — a competitor can't underprice a regulated fee, and winning share means rebuilding ~1,000 broker integrations for a customer (the broker) who doesn't pay the bill. Weakness is the source: the rulebook that grants the toll can amend it.
