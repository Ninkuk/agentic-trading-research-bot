## 2. Business

**Created:** advertisers (mostly Fortune 500, via agencies) get one objective buying
platform across the open internet — CTV, audio, display, retail media — evaluating
~20M ad opportunities/second with the buyer's own data kept private, versus walled
gardens that grade their own homework. Retention >95% every quarter for a decade
(Q1'26 release).

**Captured:** a platform fee on gross ad spend (historically ~20%; no longer
disclosed — see UNKNOWNs), plus data-marketplace fees (claimed >80% of top-US-retailer
sales represented in its retail data marketplace vs Amazon <15%, Q1'26 call). Float
economics from the AR/AP pass-through ($3.3B AR / $2.6B AP at Q1'26).

**Protected:** the mechanism is objectivity-plus-scale — TTD owns no media, so brands
and data owners (Disney, Netflix, Spotify, LinkedIn, retailers) share data and
inventory access they will not give a conflicted buyer; switching costs live in agency
workflow/JBP integration (45 JBPs signed in March alone, count +55% YoY). The moat
protects the *niche* (neutral buyer for premium open internet), not the *fee level* —
nothing structurally stops fee compression from Amazon pricing DSP access near cost.
