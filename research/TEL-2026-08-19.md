## 2. Business

**Created:** TE sells connectivity and sensors that must not fail in harsh
environments — vehicles, aircraft, factories, the grid, and now AI racks. The
customer gets engineered reliability at trivial BOM cost relative to the
system it protects (a connector failure strands a vehicle platform or a data
center rack), plus co-design engineering presence at ~130 countries' worth of
OEM sites. FY25 mix: Transportation 54% (auto 75% of segment), Industrial 46%
(DDN 28%, automation/connected living 27%, aerospace/defense/marine 19%,
energy 17%, medical 9%). Geography: APAC 38%, EMEA 33%, Americas 29%.

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms, not one. (a) Content per platform —
sockets won at design-in, then shipped for the platform's life; content per
vehicle grows 4–6pts faster than production. (b) Price — explicit increases
passed through tariff/inflation years (10-K, FQ3 call). (c) Operating
leverage — 30%+ incremental fall-through on volume (CFO, FQ3 call; margins
+90bp YoY to 21.9% adjusted). (d) Bolt-on M&A into adjacent power/filter
niches (Schaffner, Richards Manufacturing $2.3B-class FY25, RAM Photonics,
Astrodyne TDI $1.4B pending) funded from FCF while returning ~$2B/yr.

**Protected:** qualification lock-in is the mechanism — parts are specified
into platforms years ahead on 5–7-year development cycles, and re-qualifying
a supplier risks the whole program for pennies saved. Scale and localization
harden it: 76% of production localized in-region, 90% of supply in-region
(Investor Day), which tariff regimes reward. The oligopoly (Amphenol, Molex,
Aptiv, Yazaki, Sumitomo) has been stable for decades. The honest limit: none
of this stops Amphenol, which runs higher margins — the moat holds returns,
it does not guarantee share.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue FY21→TTM $14,923M →
$19,322M (+29%); operating income $2,710M → $3,949M (+46%); op margin 18.2%
→ 20.4%. Caveat inside the trend: FY22→FY24 revenue went backwards
($16,281M → $15,845M) — the cyclicality the 10-K warns about is in the
recent record, not hypothetical. (FY23 op margin dipped to 16.6% on charges;
FY24/25 net income swings — $3,193M then $1,842M — are tax one-offs, not
operations.)
