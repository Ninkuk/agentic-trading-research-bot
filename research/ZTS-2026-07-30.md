## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $75.81**, reopen after the Aug 6 Q2 print. kill-thesis:
**UNPROVEN** — conditions=6, refuted=0, unknown=3.

The floor math is genuinely attractive — at a $31.77B market cap the price
implies ~8%/yr with zero growth forever and ~9%/yr on 3% growth — but every
piece of evidence that revenue has stopped declining is management-sourced,
from the same executives a securities class action alleges concealed the
deterioration, and the first independent-ish data point (audited Q2 franchise
revenue) arrives in five trading days with the options market pricing an ±11%
expected move around it.

Thesis (plain): Zoetis is the animal-health leader whose 6–8% growth algorithm
broke publicly in 2025–26 under price-fatigued pet owners, two branded JAK
rivals to Apoquel, triple-combo rivals to Simparica Trio, generics on two
non-core blockbusters, and the Librela safety overhang. The 50% drawdown has
likely overshot: ownership at this price requires only that revenue holds
roughly flat, not that the old algorithm returns. Unverified ≠ wrong — this is
a timing-of-evidence pass, not a business rejection.

**Closest attack:** the class action impairs the independence of every
stabilization claim the flat-revenue condition rests on.

## 2. Business

**Created:** Vets get the broadest, deepest portfolio in animal health (18
blockbusters, 8 species, ~300 product lines) plus a direct field force that
supports clinic economics; pet owners get chronic-care drugs (itch, pain,
parasites) that mostly have no substitute but steroids or non-treatment.
Livestock producers get vaccines/parasiticides tied to protein economics.
~70% companion animal / 30% livestock (CFO, William Blair 2026-06-02).

**Captured:** Premium pricing on patent- or practice-protected franchises sold
through vet clinics (and increasingly retail/home delivery), with price
contributing 2–3pts of growth historically — cut to 1–2pts in the May guide.
Concentration is the flip side: top-5 franchises ≈42% of revenue, top-10 ≈57%,
largest distributor 16% and rising (FY2025 10-K).

**Protected:** Not a checkbox — mechanisms: (a) field force + portfolio breadth
bundled into clinic economics; (b) biologics/mAb manufacturing scale (animal
mAbs have no easy generic path); (c) a 12-year category-creation record
(Apoquel 2013, Cytopoint 2016, Trio 2020, Librela 2023). Eroding at the margin:
Apoquel's compound patent runs only to ~Sept 2029 (third-party patent trackers,
medium confidence), and branded rivals are already in-market.

## 5. Falsifiers

For an owner (sell / stay away):

- Q2 (Aug 6) shows US companion-animal organic decline steeper than Q1's −8%,
  derm decline accelerating past −13%, or a third guidance cut.
- Any US/EU regulatory escalation on Librela beyond label updates (restriction
  or withdrawal) — kills the mAb-franchise credibility that the pipeline needs.
- Livestock growth reversal (it is the diversification offset; producer
  economics are cyclical).
- Leverage pushed past ~2.5× EBITDA to fund buybacks.
- A transformational acquisition (management has explicitly disclaimed one).
