- **Variable-price conversion with a deep floor.** Conversion at the
  greater of 80% of the lowest daily VWAP over the five preceding trading
  days and a floor equal to 20% of the NYSE American §713 "Minimum Price".
  At today's $0.18 the conversion price is ~$0.144 and $3.36M converts into
  ~23.4M shares, 77% of the current count; at a floor near $0.057 (20% of
  the $0.2831 close preceding signing — the §713 definition was not
  re-fetched, see §6) it is ~59M shares. The 4.99%/9.99% beneficial-
  ownership cap limits holdings at any instant, not cumulative conversions
  and resales, and the Rule 144 holding period tacks to 2026-03-31, so
  conversion shares are already resalable without registration.
- **Reverse split pre-agreed.** The company has agreed to obtain and
  maintain authorisations for reverse share splits and authorised-share
  increases sufficient to convert the notes in full at the floor. The July
  amendments' separate covenant — call a shareholder meeting within 30 days

  2026-10-14 if the tape holds.
