## 4. Valuation

**Inputs and pairing:** same FCFE frame as the prior file. Owner cash =
common tangible book × (ROE − book growth), because a growing bank retains
equity in step with its book. Common tangible equity is $1,208.3M (Q3 8-K
non-GAAP reconciliation), against market cap $1,690.0M ($36.44 ×
46,377,874). No net-debt adjustment (deposits are operating liabilities).
No SBC deduction (expensed in GAAP income). Preferred ($96.3M) and NCI
($4.0M) are excluded from book. Year 1 FCFE = BV0 × (ROE − b), growing at b
for years 1–4, then stepping by (1+b)(ROE−gT)/(ROE−b) as book growth fades
to the terminal rate.

**Hurdle:** rf 5.15% (DGS10, 2026-10-19) + beta 1.2 × ERP 4.20% (Damodaran
implied, 2026-10-01, against his 5.29% T-bond) = **10.19%**. Beta is held at
1.2 as in the prior file. The 5y regression is 1.94 (`stocks.db`) and spans
the 2021 fintech bubble. At 1.94 the hurdle is 13.30% and every row
below fails.

**Terminal-growth ceiling:** 10-year breakeven 2.35% (T10YIE, 2026-10-20),
used as `--terminal-growth`, which means no real growth in perpetuity.
