# NKE — Nike, Inc. (2026-08-11)

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $41.29 / $61.3B.** The price already pays for a fully successful
turnaround: even modeling full margin restoration (13% EBIT, 3% revenue growth)
the implied return is 8.0%/yr, and a maximal 2021-redux path reaches only
9.2% — both below the 10.2% operations-weighted cost of equity. Good business,
wrong price. **kill-thesis: SOUND** (5 conditions, 0 refuted, 0 unknown);
the closest attack (bull-max path + US-only ERP + sub-1.0 beta) needs three
simultaneous generosities, each individually contradicted.

Entry path: user-directed. Composite covers NKE with one signal, score 0,
never flagged.

## 2. Business

**Creates:** performance athletic footwear/apparel with authenticity built on
athletes and sport moments (World Cup: 1.5B story views, 2.5× kit sales vs
2022; call, 2026-06-30). Consumers pay for status + performance credibility.

**Captures:** wholesale (FY26 $27.5B, +6%) and NIKE Direct ($17.7B, −6%)
across four geographies; NA $20.5B, EMEA $12.6B, China $5.8B, APLA $6.2B;
Jordan Brand $7.0B inside those; Converse $1.2B (−31%) (press release).

**Protects:** brand + a $15.5B contracted endorsement portfolio ($1.7B/yr
current — 10-K) + supply-chain/distribution scale. The mechanism is real but
priced: the 10-K's own dominant risk (Item 1A) is brand relevance amid intense
preference-driven competition — exactly the thing the last three years dented.

## 4. Valuation

Reverse DCF, levered FCF ↔ market cap ($61,298,166,408 hover, 2026-08-11).
FY26 reported levered FCF $2,184M (NCFO $2,868M + capex −$684M). Clean base
strips $300M refund cash → $1,884M. No minority interests. Effective tax
20.3% ≈ marginal — base not tax-flattered. Cash $9.0B vs debt $11.0B (incl.
leases): no excess-cash netting. Capex abnormally low ($684M vs D&A $747M,
FY22–24 avg ~$846M) — Run A normalizes to $850M and deducts SBC ($715M).

Hurdle: rf 4.74% + beta 1.118 × ERP 4.87% = **10.18%**. ERP is
operations-weighted from the Jan 5, 2026 country table (US 4.46×44%, EMEA
~4.9×27%, China 5.14×13%, APLA ~6.0×13%, coarse regional mapping); rf/ERP
headline as of Aug 1, 2026. US median cost of capital 7.79%; 80% band
5.26–9.88% (Damodaran 2026).

Runs B–D model margin expansion via front-loaded FCF growth (skill's
mid-expansion rule): B maps EBIT 8.3%→11% (Damodaran shoe-industry anchor
10.4%; management's "clear path to double-digit margins"); D maps to Nike's
own FY22 14.3%. Run E rebuilds the base from EBIT ex-severance (~$4.25B,
tariff-neutral), tax 21%, capex=D&A, no WC drag — the bull's best base; its
terminal ROE prints 1.40 (near-zero reinvestment), i.e. its terminal is
flattering, and it still misses. Market-share check: +2%/yr puts FY31 revenue
at ~$51B, ~12% of a faster-growing global sportswear market — continued share
loss; top line is conservative. Terminal risk (Item 1A: brand relevance):
terminal growth 2–2.5% ≤ inflation survives so long as the brand persists at
scale; the contested terminal input is the 11–13% margin level, defended by
the endorsement lock-up and the 10.4% industry anchor. Implied terminal ROE:
Run B 10.3% ≈ hurdle (excess returns fade — the ~29% base-rate default).

$23/share**; at $40B (~$27) it earns 9.0%.
