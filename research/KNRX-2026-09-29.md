## 2. Business

**Created:** Knorex XPO is a self-service cloud platform for planning,
buying and optimising programmatic ad campaigns across channels. Customers,
mostly agencies and resellers, get automation and optimisation over 8,759+
advertiser accounts as of 2026-06-30 (F-1). The KAIROS AI engine and
"Agentic AI" are the current framing. AscendX is a separate publisher-side
monetisation arm (sell side): 10 live publishers, 70+ app/game bundles, 2.0B
impressions YTD 2026 (6-K 2026-09-28).

**Captured:** three disclosed streams (20-F FY2025):
- platform subscription fees;
- usage-based platform services, where the client controls when and how much
  it uses XPO;
- managed activations and professional services.

The company must pay inventory and data suppliers within negotiated terms
whether or not its customers pay. That working-capital squeeze explains why
current liabilities sit at $13.3M on a $6M revenue base. The mix across the
streams and the gross-vs-net revenue policy were NOT OBTAINED; see §6.

**Protected:** no mechanism found. The 20-F concedes that "the digital
advertising industry is highly competitive" and that competitors have
"significantly more financial, technical, marketing… resources." A single
customer's tariff-driven pullback erased 44% of revenue. That is what
unprotected looks like. The silence is the answer.

**Control:** dual class. 25,642,538 Class A and 4,780,575 Class B shares; the
Class B carries enhanced voting rights. The 20-F risk factor says the
structure "may limit your ability to influence corporate matters." Votes per
Class B share and the insider voting percentage were NOT OBTAINED. The CEO is
Phu Le (6-K 2026-09-28). The structure forecloses an unsolicited takeover and
an activist path while Class B holds its enhanced vote. The ELOC's 156.8M new
Class A shares dilute economic ownership, not necessarily voting control.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Op income for FY2023/24 was NOT
OBTAINED, so net loss stands in for it; this is labelled, not substituted
silently.
