## 2. Business

**Created:** For users, the fastest route from a question to an answer or
an action at zero price — Search (AI Overviews, AI Mode and classic results
as one product), YouTube, Maps, Android, Chrome, Gmail, and the Gemini app
(one billion users, "the fastest-growing product in Google's history").
For advertisers, intent: a typed or spoken commercial query is worth more
than any audience segment, and AI Max now captures long-form and
multilingual queries that were previously unmonetizable (Telstra: +52%
conversions, −36% cost per order — Q3 call). For enterprises, a vertically
integrated AI stack — TPU silicon, Gemini models, Vertex, Wiz security,
Workspace — sold as consumption, seats, and since 2026 as whole TPU systems
delivered into customer or third-party data centers.

**Captured:** six distinct mechanisms, not "advertising":
(a) Search & other auction ads, $63,350M/qtr, paid per click, with $16,366M
of TAC paid to distribution partners (+10%);
(b) YouTube ads, $11,207M/qtr (+9%), brand and direct response, living room
over one-third of the total, revenue-shared with creators;
(c) Network ads, $7,455M/qtr (+1%), the piece exposed to the ad-tech remedy;
(d) subscriptions, platforms and devices, $14,290M/qtr (+11%) — 150M YouTube
Music/Premium subscribers incl. trials, Google One AI plans, Play's take,
Pixel;
(e) Google Cloud consumption and seats, "the vast majority" of $30,709M;
(f) TPU-system sales — hardware revenue recognized on delivery, with
inventory built ahead of revenue (inventory $13,308M at Sept 30 from
$2,439M at Dec 31), only against signed contracts, now also distributed via
Oracle and the Blackstone-backed Crux AI joint venture (Q3 call; 8-K Ex.
99.1 balance sheet).

**Protected:** In Search, a three-sided flywheel — the largest query stream
trains the ranking and ad models, the largest advertiser base makes every
query more monetizable, and distribution keeps the stream — now under a
behavioral court order to share data and syndicate to qualified rivals. In
Cloud/AI, cost: owning TPUs and the model stack gives a lower cost per
token than a GPU-renting rival (TPU 8t/8i "2.7× training performance, 80%
better inference price-performance" vs the prior generation — Kurian), and
$561B of backlog is contracted demand. Management's own moat answer is
scale economics in silicon ("in the semiconductor industry, economies of
scale matter" — Pichai) and "the full stack"; neither forecloses a
frontier-model rival being simply better, and the TPU business now
deliberately arms competitors (Anthropic, Oracle) in exchange for scale.

**Control:** Three classes. Class B (10 votes) 828M shares, ~89% held by
Larry Page and Sergey Brin for a majority of the vote (10-K FY2025 Item 1A;
share counts 8-K Ex. 99.1 at Sept 30: A 5,888M, B 828M, C 5,578M). Class C
(this ticker) has no vote. Plus 19M shares of 6.25% mandatory convertible
preferred ($19.0B, converting May 2029 into 2.25–2.84 shares per preferred
share — 10-Q Note). The founders foreclose any unsolicited takeover or
activist path.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue 2021→TTM $257.6B →
$471.1B (+83%); operating income $78.7B → $157.0B (+99%); margin 30.6% →
33.3% (stockanalysis income statement, annual). FY2025 $402.8B / $129.0B;
FY2024 $350.0B / $112.4B; FY2023 $307.4B / $84.3B; FY2022 $282.8B / $74.8B.
Q3 2026 margin 31.8% vs 30.5% a year ago but down from 34.0% in Q2 —
other cost of revenues +32% on TPU hardware costs, depreciation and content;
R&D +36% on AI talent (Q3 call). Positive year over year, flat to negative
sequentially for the first time in the cycle: the hardware line and the
depreciation wave are now visible in the P&L. **Net income is still not
the number to read:** TTM $235.3B carries $127.3B of nine-month equity-
security gains; Q3 swung to a −$8,664M loss (marketable equity −$12,436M,
the SpaceX mark), which is the whole of the EPS "miss" ($2.12 vs $2.96
estimate — Robinhood estimate side; actual matches SEC diluted EPS).
Operating-basis net income ≈ $128.7B TTM (OI × (1 − 18.1% tax)).
