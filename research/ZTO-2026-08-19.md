## 1. Verdict and thesis

conditions=5 (4 probable, 1 plausible), refuted=0, unknown=1

net cash, a ≥50%-of-earnings shareholder-return commitment being honored —

pricing discipline held, unit costs fell again, margins expanded. The

combination; the label grades the thesis's provability, not its direction.
