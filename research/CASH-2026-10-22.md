Pathward is a high-return sponsor bank. Card and tax partners hand it about

income +12%, expense −10%, H&R Block renewed through mid-2030, and no

still rising, the reserve against them is shrinking as losses are
recognized, and the stated plan is to fund, finish, and sell the stressed
solar projects. That is a workout whose outcome nobody has quantified yet.
At 7.9× FY27 guidance the stock is cheap if the workout recovers most of
the $289M, and fairly priced if it does not. This run waits for the first
recoveries rather than buying the guide.

**Closest attack:** coverage fell while NPLs rose. Commercial ACL is now

$287M of nonperforming commercial loans. FY27 guidance still assumes the
ratio drifts *down* to 120–130bp. The whole position rests on construction
collateral being worth close to par once finished, and those projects face a
tax-credit placed-in-service deadline and a rising-rate cost of capital,
which management conceded "changes the economics."
