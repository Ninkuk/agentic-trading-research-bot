## 2. Business

**Created.** Treprostinil delivered four ways (infused Remodulin, nebulized
Tyvaso, dry-powder Tyvaso DPI, oral Orenitram) for pulmonary arterial
hypertension and PH-ILD — rare, progressive, fatal diseases where the
alternative to therapy is death. Patients get years of function. Management's
own framing on the Q1 call: "patients are like snowflakes" — the portfolio wins
by covering every delivery route, not by having the best molecule.

**Captured.** Specialty-pharmacy distribution of high-priced orphan drugs, near
90% gross margin (FY2025 gross margin 86.6%). FY2025 revenue splits: Tyvaso DPI
$1,292.5M, nebulized Tyvaso $585.7M (**the Tyvaso franchise is 59% of total**),
Remodulin $526.8M, Orenitram $496.9M, Unituxin $226.8M. Growth in Q1 2026 came
mostly from price, not volume — Tyvaso DPI's +9% was $16.0M of quantity on a
$27.8M increase.

**Protected — and this is where it thins.** Not composition-of-matter: the '793
patent was invalidated by PTAB, affirmed by the Federal Circuit, and the Supreme
Court denied cert. What is left is (a) the MannKind Crystal Carrier device/
formulation IP behind Tyvaso DPI, (b) 30 years of PAH prescriber relationships
and specialty distribution, and (c) orphan exclusivity on *new* indications
(7 years if IPF is granted). That is a real mechanism, but it is a device-and-
channel moat, not a molecule moat — which is exactly why a competitor is
currently inside the franchise.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0):** not stated as a direction label in the
original run; the original records TTM revenue ($3,169.8M) and TTM operating
income ($1,435.5M) both sitting *below* FY2025 ($3,182.7M / $1,492.5M) — see

## 3. Threads pulled

- **Yutrepia is not a scare, it is already in the numbers.** Liquidia's competing
  inhaled treprostinil launched June 2025 and did **~$130M in Q1 2026** with 980+
  prescribers. UTHR's nebulized Tyvaso fell **-22% YoY to $127.2M**, with US
  quantities down $33.3M — a near-exact handoff. Total Tyvaso -2% hides this: DPI
  +9% masking neb -22%.
- **The commitment trap — the strongest single find.** In **January 2026** UTHR
  amended its MannKind supply agreement to include "committed annual purchase
  amounts through December 31, 2031" (10-K). Within one quarter, Q1 2026 cost of
  sales rose 44% including a **$26.8M estimated loss from that commercial supply
  agreement**. They locked in six years of fixed DPI volume just as a substitute
  took share, and the onerous-contract accrual arrived immediately. Total purchase
  obligations are **$1,022.8M, of which $869.5M falls within one year**.
- **Capex is not winding down — it is deferred.** TTM capex $546.4M against D&A of
  only $88.4M (6.2x). The purchase-obligations footnote attributes those
  commitments partly to "open purchase orders for capital expenditures primarily
  related to our continued investment in construction of additional facilities,"
  and the 10-K states the intent "to defer the most significant capital
  expenditures until we achieve certain clinical trial milestones" for
  xenotransplantation. The largest organ-manufacturing spend is **ahead**, not
  behind.
- **Dilution is bigger than the share count suggests.** Q1 2026 diluted shares
  47.2M vs basic 43.5M — an 8.5% gap. Market cap on shares outstanding (42.45M) is
  $22.41B; on the diluted claim it is ~$24.9B. SBC ran $33.9M in Q1 (~$136M/yr).
  At the June AGM the 2026 Stock Incentive Plan passed with **10.07M shares against
  vs 25.87M for — 28% dissent**.
- **Insider selling is systematic, not opportunistic.** CEO Martine Rothblatt files
  a Form 4 nearly every trading day: 9,500 options exercised at $135.42 and sold
  same-day at $521–536, ending each day at exactly 324,443 direct shares. A 10b5-1
  plan (box checked), so it carries no information about her view — but it is a
  standing ~$5M/day seller, and net share count still falls 1.4%/yr only because
  $1.5B of accelerated buyback (March 2026, 2,164,459 shares) outruns it.
- **Options read (mandatory):** path 2 (Robinhood stopgap) only — UTHR is not
  in the 24-symbol CBOE catalog, so path 1 does not exist for this name. See
  §4; liquidity gate FAILED → UNRELIABLE.
- **Dead ends:** (1) The July 23 8-K is a routine board appointment (Victor
  Dzau), not a thesis event. (2) The June 18 volume spike (2.0M shares, ~3-5x
  normal, -2.7%) has no matching filing — unexplained, and I could not source
  it. (3) The Q1 effective tax rate fell 24%→14% on excess SBC benefits, which
  flatters net income but not FCF; not a thesis item, just a reason not to read

  filings (Q1 2026 $5.82 diluted both ways) — no discrepancy to report here.

## 5. Falsifiers

For the pass (flip toward buy):

- **Nebulized Tyvaso stabilizes sequentially for two consecutive quarters** (Q2
  prints Aug 5 BMO, 8 days out) *and* total Tyvaso returns to YoY growth.
- **Capex guided down below ~$300M/yr** with the xeno build scoped and dated,
  which would make the add-back rows legitimate and move the implied return to
  ~11%.
- **IPF sNDA accepted with a broad label**, plus the 7-year orphan exclusivity
  granted — that resets the terminal question the 2027 patent expiry raises.
- **Price alone would need roughly $260–300/share** for flat-FCF-on-diluted-
  shares to imply 10%/yr. That is a ~45% drawdown; the realistic flip is
  evidence, not price.

For an owner (sell):

- A second quarter of Tyvaso DPI quantity decline (not just price-carried
  growth), or another onerous-contract accrual on the MannKind agreement —
  that would mean the six-year commitment is structurally above achievable
  demand.

## 6. UNKNOWNs

1. **Peak sales for Tyvaso-in-IPF and ralinepag.** Management says the two
   products each exceed today's total revenue and "lap our 2027 $4 billion
   revenue run rate twice over" (Q1 call) — but declined to break it out
   ("we'll provide some more granularity as we get later in the year"). No
   disclosure supports or refutes the magnitude. **Does its absence kill the
   thesis?** No, but it means conditions 2 and 3 cannot be sized, only believed.
2. **Which Tyvaso patent expires in 2027, and whether it blocks.** The 10-K
   gives only the range (2027–2042) across six Orange Book patents. Resolving it
   needs an Orange Book pull per patent number, which I did not do. This is the
   input to terminal growth, so it is load-bearing.
3. **The June 18 volume spike** (2.0M shares) — no filing, no dated news found.
4. **Efficacy science I cannot evaluate.** TETON-1 (+130.1 mL FVC vs placebo at
   52 weeks) and TETON-2 (+95.6 mL) both hit with company-reported p<0.0001, and
   ADVANCE OUTCOMES hit all primary endpoints. I can read that these are
   statistically strong and clinically framed as best-in-class; I cannot
   independently assess whether the FVC effect translates to the commercial
   uptake management projects, nor judge the xenotransplantation program's
   probability at all. Marked UNKNOWN, not credited.
