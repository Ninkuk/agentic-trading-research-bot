## 2. Business

**Created:** the verified identity network + daily workflow layer for US
medicine — 85%+ of US physicians are members (FY26 press release); free
telehealth (Dialer), on-call scheduling (Amion), fax, secure messaging, and
now HIPAA-compliant AI (DoximityGPT, Scribe, AI Search via the $63M Pathway
acquisition, summer 2025). Physicians get real workflow utility and pay
nothing.

**Captured:** pharma HCP marketing budgets — subscription-based sponsored
content/native ads reaching verified prescribers, plus hiring solutions and
health-system workflow subscriptions. 125 customers ≥$500k = 83% of revenue.
New: AI Search keyword/condition sponsorships (launched late April 2026,
first top-20 pharma deals closed per Q4 call).

**Protected:** the claimed mechanism is hospital-sanctioned distribution —
140 health systems bought the Clinical AI Suite in two quarters; 250k
prescribers have HIPAA-compliant enterprise access; EHR integration; the
verified-identity graph. **This mechanism is real but demonstrably not
exclusive:** Cedars-Sinai deployed rival OpenEvidence enterprise-wide in May
2026 with EHR-integrated patient-context queries. The moat protects the
workflow franchise; the *ad* franchise rests on feed/search attention, which
is exactly what is under attack.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative in FY26** — revenue +13.1% while
GAAP operating income fell 5.7% ($227.8M → $214.9M); R&D +40%, SG&A +20%.
The self-declared "AI investment year."
