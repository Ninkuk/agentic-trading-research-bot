# TPTS — Terra Property Trust, Inc. 7.00% Senior Secured Notes due 2029 (NYSE) — 2026-07-27

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Kill-thesis verdict: FLAWED (7 load-bearing conditions, 6 refuted, 1 unknown).
Ownership call: PASS at $16.80.**

TPTS is not a stock. It is a $25-par exchange-traded senior secured note — $25,578,000
of 7.00% paper maturing 2029-03-31, created on 2026-04-02 in a distressed exchange —
issued by a non-traded, externally managed commercial-real-estate lender whose own
Q1 2026 10-Q states that "substantial doubt about the Company's ability to continue
as a going concern exists," which had $5.0M of cash at 2026-03-31, and 4 of its 7
remaining loans ($121.5M) non-performing against a $66.0M allowance. The ~25% yield
to maturity is not compensation for a mispricing; it is the market pricing a second
restructuring, and this run is a Phase 0 instrument-type kill: the skill's engine
(business → moat → reverse DCF on FCF vs market cap) has no purchase on a fixed-income
claim, and the correct method — collateral-level recovery analysis — is apparatus this
repo does not have.

Direction is sharply negative, and it is a level *and* a direction — the book is
shrinking and impairing at the same time.

## 3. Threads pulled

- **What actually happened to the capital structure in 2026.** Two distressed
  exchanges in four months. (i) 2026-04-02: only 29.89% of the 6.00% notes
  ($24,027,025) and 4.04% of subsidiary TIF6's 7.00% notes ($1,550,975) tendered →
  **$25,578,000 of TPTS**. (ii) 2026-06-29: $36,208,750 (66.4% of $54,549,125
  outstanding) of the 6.00% notes swapped for **$18.75 of new 11.00% Senior Secured
  Notes due 2027-07-01 plus $6.25 cash per $25**. TIF6 repaid its untendered notes
  in cash on 2026-03-31.
- **The thread that kills it: TPTS is the patient half of an impatient pool.**
  The 11% 2027 notes (~$27.16M) are **pari passu with TPTS on the same Shared
  Collateral under an intercreditor agreement** (S-4/A), and mature **21 months
  earlier** at a coupon 400bp higher. ~$52.73M of secured notes now share one
  collateral pool, and the other half gets to demand cash first.
- **The collateral is subsidiary *equity*, which is junior to subsidiary debt.**
  The liens are on "Capital Interests … in certain of our direct subsidiaries"
  (18 entities). At 2026-03-31 the company carried **$71.5M of secured financing**,
  and the proposed **$25.0M Supplemental Liquidity Financing is a term loan at
  Subsidiary Holdings II, LLC** — new money *at* the subsidiary, structurally ahead
  of a pledge of that subsidiary's equity. TPTS's collateral is the residual.
- **The arithmetic does not close.** Annualized Q1 revenue $12.8M. Against it:
  TPTS interest $1.79M + 11% notes $2.99M = $4.78M, plus manager fees ~$4.59M/yr
  (Q1: $935,071 asset management + $211,211 servicing) = **$9.37M** — before any
  interest on the $71.5M secured financing, before G&A, before further credit
  losses. Cash was $5.0M ($8.8M with restricted).
- **The manager is paid to hold assets, not to repay noteholders.** $1.15M of Q1
  fees = **36% of quarterly revenue**, taken in a quarter with a $15.0M loss and a
  cash balance falling from $33.2M to $5.0M. Asset-based, not performance-based.
- **The June 30 2026 wall — genuinely unresolved.** $18,340,375 of 6.00% notes went
  untendered and came due 2026-06-30. Required cash: $9.05M consideration +
  $18.34M untendered = **$27.39M**, against a proposed $25.0M facility — ~$2.4M
  short on its face. No filing I could reach confirms the untendered notes were
  paid or the facility closed. **Dead end, and it is the live question.**
- **The bounce is not information.** +17.42% in a week, +20.77% in a month sounds
  like a recovery, but stockanalysis's tiingo series holds only **22 bars starting
  ~2026-06-25** (`firstYear: 2026`), so "one month" is nearly the entire series,
  and the vendor's own `l52` of $16.36 is arithmetically inconsistent with the
  ~$13.91 level a +20.77% move implies. The window coincides exactly with the
  second exchange settling — relief that a maturity was pushed, not evidence that
  credit improved. **Dead end; the price move is not usable as a fact.**
- **Options (mandatory thread): no listed options.** $25-par baby bonds do not have
  chains; TPTS is absent from the CBOE catalog behind `data/options.db` (path 1)
  and Robinhood 404s the instrument outright, so path 2 is unavailable too. No
  options-implied move exists for this security.
- **Point-in-time DB cross-check: not performed** — `sqlite3` was unavailable in
  this run's shell (see UNKNOWNs). Expected result is empty on all three:

## 4. Valuation

No reverse DCF was run, and running one would have been a category error —
`tools/valuation/reverse_dcf.py` solves for the return implied by *equity* cash
flows against a market cap, and TPTS has neither an FCF claim nor a market cap.
The instrument's analogue is yield, so:

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Price (NYSE close 2026-07-27) | **$16.80** (67.2% of $25 par) |
| Coupon | 7.00% of par = $1.75/yr, paid monthly |
| Current yield | **10.4%** |
| Maturity | 2029-03-31 (2.68 years) |
| Approximate YTM | **~25%/yr** |
| Issue size / market value | $25,578,000 principal ≈ $17.2M at market |
| Call | 101 before 2026-12-31, par thereafter |
| Covenant | Collateral Coverage Ratio ≥ 1.35:1.00 to incur debt or pay dividends >90% of taxable income |

YTM is my own arithmetic (annual compounding, bisected to ~25.3%; monthly coupons
would raise the effective figure modestly), and it is quoted to the nearest whole
percent deliberately — a distressed note's yield is a wide range, not a point.
Critically, **YTM assumes par at maturity**; it is not a return, it is what the
return would be if nothing further goes wrong, and the issuer has had two things
go wrong in four months.

**The issuer's own credit was repriced in June, and TPTS is on the wrong side of
it.** In an arm's-length exchange three weeks ago, Terra had to pay **11.00% plus
25% of principal in cash** to move $36.2M of debt out 15 months. TPTS holds 7.00%
for 33 months on the same collateral. That spread is the market's mark, and it
argues for a price well below par irrespective of any recovery estimate.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make this interesting rather than avoidable)

1. A disclosed **actual** Collateral Coverage Ratio comfortably above 1.35x,
   computed on cash-realizable subsidiary equity *net of* the $71.5M secured
   financing and the Supplemental Liquidity Financing.
2. Confirmation the $18.34M untendered 6.00% notes were repaid in cash on
   2026-06-30 without new structurally senior debt.
3. Non-performing loans falling below ~25% of the book, with revenue recovering
   toward the $12.5M/quarter of Q1 2025.
4. Removal of the going-concern qualification in the Q2 2026 10-Q (due ~mid-August).
5. Dismissal of Portage Point Partners / Alston & Bird.
6. An asset sale large enough to retire the 11% 2027 notes, which would leave TPTS
   alone on its collateral rather than sharing it with a nearer maturity.

Conversely, the sell/stay-away case needs none of these to fail — it needs only
the status quo to persist.

## 6. UNKNOWNs

- **Actual Collateral Coverage Ratio.** Never disclosed in any source reached.
  Would come from the indenture compliance certificate or a 10-Q covenant
  footnote. **This is the one condition I could not attack** — if the pledged
  equity happens to sit above the $47.4M of industrial buildings and $70.0M of JV
  equity unencumbered, $52.73M of secured notes could in principle be covered.
  Uncertainty withholds credit; it does not earn it.
- **Whether the $18.34M untendered 6.00% notes were paid at the 2026-06-30
  maturity, and whether the $25.0M Supplemental Liquidity Financing closed.**
  Would come from an 8-K dated 2026-07-02 or later, which I could not retrieve
  (SEC returned 403 to every direct fetch — see below). Its absence does not
  change the verdict but it is the single most decision-relevant open fact.

  asset-level marks; no public source supports one. Its absence is why this is a
  PASS rather than a distressed-value opinion in either direction.
- **Primary filings not read directly.** `data.sec.gov` and `www.sec.gov/Archives`

  fingerprint sensitivity), so the 10-K, 10-Q, S-4/A and 8-Ks are cited here
  through filing-summary intermediaries rather than read first-hand. Every figure
  in this note is therefore one tier below primary and should be re-verified

- **Point-in-time DB baseline.** `sqlite3` was not runnable in this session's

- **Q1 2026 6.00%-notes balance discrepancy.** The 10-Q shows $56.4M outstanding
