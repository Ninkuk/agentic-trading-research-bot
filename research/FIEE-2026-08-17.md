# FIEE — FiEE, Inc. — 2026-08-17

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS.** FiEE is a Nasdaq shell (ex-Minim, ex-Zoom Telephonics, CIK 1467761) that
carried a going-concern opinion for FY2024, changed control to a Hong Kong buyer group
in February 2025, and has since booked TTM revenue of $13.10M against roughly $45k a
year earlier — with 74% of first-half revenue still sitting in receivables, no earnings
call in three years, and no analyst covering it. I could not verify a single figure
against a primary filing this run, and at $5.63 the implied return spans 6.19%–15.65%
depending entirely on whether that receivable converts, so the price offers no margin of
safety in either direction.

kill-thesis verdict: **UNPROVEN**. Load-bearing conditions: **4**, refuted 0, UNKNOWN 2.

This is a pass on unverifiability, not an accusation. Nothing here establishes that the
revenue is not real; what it establishes is that I have no reachable evidence that it is.

## 2. Business

**Created.** FiEE sells digital-content operations to creators and brands: account
management, content production (graphics, short video, editorial calendars), growth
analytics, and creator partnerships — an MCN/KOL agency wrapped in SaaS language. Since
March 2026 it has also bought into AI music (a music-copyright library and performer
roster in China) and AI/blockchain authentication of art collections in Japan. The
customer gets outsourced social-media operations; the surplus, if any, is that a creator
or brand does not have to staff this in-house.

**Captured.** Four unrelated lines, all acquired inside 15 months (H1 2026, company
press release 2026-08-14):

| Line | H1'26 revenue | Customers |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS — MCN digital services | $2.6M | 900+ (138 new) |
| Software services | $1.7M | **31** (18 new) |
| Digital authentication (art, Japan) | $2.6M | **5 corporate** + 459 individual |
| Other | $11.3K | — |

$2.6M from five corporate customers is ~$520k each. Two of the three real lines are
concentration bets, not subscription books.

**Protected.** *No mechanism found.* An MCN/KOL agency has no switching cost, no scale
economy, and no network effect — the creator owns the audience and leaves with it. The
art-authentication line is 12 months old with five customers; the AI-music VIE is three
months old. I looked for something that stops a competitor tomorrow and did not find it.
Per the skill's rule, that silence is the answer, and the write-up carries it as one.

**Operating-leverage trajectory.** Not computable in the ordinary sense — there is no
comparable prior period. Revenue went $45k → $13.10M TTM (+28,926%); operating income
went from a loss to +$5.63M TTM (43.0% margin), Q2'26 alone $2.64M on $4.83M (54.8%).
Direction is positive and extreme, but it is the arithmetic of a business appearing from
nothing via acquisition, not of leverage on an existing base. Stating it as "operating
leverage" would be a category error, so I am not stating it as one.

**Revenue is lumpy, not recurring.** TTM $13.10M less H1'26 $6.9M leaves ~$6.2M for
H2'25; H1'25 was $0.05M in total, so essentially all of it landed in Q4'25. The implied
shape is Q4'25 ~$6.1M → Q1'26 ~$2.07M → Q2'26 $4.83M. A 3x quarter-over-quarter swing is
not a SaaS revenue book, whatever the label on the line item.

## 3. Threads pulled

**The shell lineage — this is the loudest one.** Minim, Inc. (formerly Zoom Telephonics,
founded 1977, SIC 3661 telephone apparatus, 2021 revenue $55.4M / loss $2.2M) collapsed;
David Lazar — a career shell-custodianship operator — served as CEO/CFO until an Amended
and Restated Securities Purchase Agreement dated 2025-02-18 handed control to Cao Yu, Hu
Bin, and Youxin Consulting Limited (Hong Kong). Lazar resigned 2025-02-26; Li Wai Chung
became CEO. Stock reinstated on Nasdaq Capital Market 2025-06-02; ticker changed to FIEE
2025-07-10. This is the standard custodianship-shell → reverse-merger pipeline, and it is
the correct prior to hold before reading any of the numbers below.

**The receivable.** AR $5.13M against H1'26 revenue $6.9M — 74%. TTM change in AR is
−$5.62M against TTM net income of $4.55M: **more than the entire year's earnings sits in
receivables that did not exist twelve months ago.** DSO on annualized H1 revenue is ~135
days. Every one of the four business lines is new, so there is no collection history to
appeal to. This is the single number the thesis would turn on, and it is unresolved until
the Q3 print.

**Auditor churn — attacked, and it came back weaker than expected.** Beckles & Co. issued
the FY2024 report with substantial doubt about going concern; the audit committee
dismissed Beckles 2025-07-11 and appointed **UHY LLP** for FY2025. UHY is a large
PCAOB-registered US firm. Appointing UHY is an *upgrade* from Beckles, not a downgrade,
and I am recording that against my own bear case. What remains unknown is who audits the
PRC and Japanese components.

**The 3-employee figure — a dead end, and probably wrong.** stockanalysis's company
profile reports 3 employees. A business serving 900+ MCN customers across four
subsidiaries plainly employs more than three people; this is almost certainly a stale
field carried over from the Minim shell period. I am not using it as evidence.

**Acquisition spend versus reported free cash flow.** TTM `ncfo` $3.555M, capex −$0.696M,
FCF $2.859M — but cash paid for acquisitions was **$3.811M**, more than the FCF, and those
acquisitions produced 100% of the revenue. Growth here is bought, not generated, and the
`fcf = ncfo + capex` definition excludes the cost of buying it.

**Minority interests.** `netinc` $4.549M vs `netinccmn` $3.462M — 23.9% belongs to
noncontrolling holders (the 49% of YinLian FiEE does not own). Consolidated FCF paired
against market cap overstates the owner's yield by that much; §4 haircuts for it.

**Dilution — real but not a kill.** Weighted H1 shares: 8.2M basic vs 10.5M diluted, a 28%
overhang. A private placement of 394,476 shares at $5.07 closed 2026-01-30 ($2.0M gross),
and an S-3 shelf is open (common, preferred, warrants, units). A reverse split took effect
2026-04-17. The placement priced ~10% below today's $5.63, which is not a distressed
print — I tried to make this a thesis-breaker and it did not hold up as one.

**Disclosure cadence.** The transcript corpus holds **8 calls, the newest dated
2023-03-29** — the Minim era. New management has held no earnings call, taken no analyst
question, and has zero sell-side estimates in all 8 trailing quarters (all `estimate:
null`). The company communicates by press release only. Read the change, not the count:
a company that stopped talking is a different disclosure regime from one that never had
coverage.

**Options check.** `get_option_chains` returned an empty chain list. **No listed options** —
path 1 does not apply (FIEE is not in the 24-symbol CBOE catalog), and path 2 has nothing
to read. Recorded as unchecked, not as clean.

**Dead end — primary filings.** SEC EDGAR was unreachable from this slot: WebFetch returns
403 on `browse-edgar`, `data.sec.gov/submissions`, and direct `Archives/` documents (no
declared UA), and `curl` is not permitted in a scheduled bash sandbox. The Q2 10-Q
(filed 2026-08-14, accession 0001829126-26-008898) exists and was not read. Neither could
I query `data/*.db` for the point-in-time record (`sec_fundamentals`, `stocks`,
`composite`) — no `sqlite3` in this sandbox. Every figure below is live
stockanalysis.com or the company's own press release.

## 4. Valuation

Inputs live from `/stocks/FIEE/statistics/` and `/financials/*` (stockanalysis, TTM
through 2026-06-30). Market cap $48.02M (8.53M shares × $5.63). No debt. Cash $5.44M.
Levered FCF paired with market cap, `--net-debt` zero, per the `fcf = ncfo + capex`
definition. Hurdle uses risk-free 4.74% and implied ERP 4.28%, **as of 2026-08-01**

0.8–1.2 stable band is floored into it, so the headline hurdle is **9.88%** — the
mechanical beta-4.90 hurdle of 25.71% is shown only to mark how far outside the
distribution this name's price behaviour sits.

Haircuts in the $1.847M base: consolidated FCF $2.859M × 76.1% owner share (NCI took
23.9% of net income) = $2.176M, less the owner share of $432k SBC = $1.847M. The $3.130M
base instead assumes the $5.13M receivable converts in full, so owner earnings
($3.462M less SBC) is the right proxy for cash.

**The valuation does not decide this name — and that is the finding.** A ~950bp swing in
the implied return (6.19% → 15.65%) turns entirely on one unverifiable input: whether the
receivable is cash. Both ends sit inside the 5.26%–9.88% band that holds 80% of US firms
or just above it, so neither end is a screaming buy or an obvious short. I had drafted the
pass on the valuation leg; that leg does not hold, and the pass now rests on §3's
verifiability argument alone.

**Growth path and market share.** The 15/12/10/8/6% path compounds to 1.62x, putting
revenue at ~$21.2M in year five. That is a rounding error against the global
influencer-marketing market, so this forecast has no "bigger than the market" problem.
The aggressive 30→10% path reaches ~$32M — still immaterial as a share. Growth is not
what is wrong with this name.

**Terminal integrity.** On the $1.847M base, `implied_terminal_roe` is **8.37%, below the
9.88% hurdle** — the terminal value already assumes perpetual value destruction, and
nothing in the write-up defends that as intentional. On the $3.130M base it is 26.08%, more
than 5 points above the hurdle and therefore "tough to do" in perpetuity for an agency
business with no identified moat. Neither reading is comfortable. Damodaran's EVA dataset
puts the base rate for earning above cost of capital at ~29%, and the default for the
terminal period is excess returns fading, not persisting.

**Terminal-risk sweep: not performed.** Item 1A requires the 10-K, which was unreachable.
The 2.5% terminal rate was chosen by convention, not defended against the company's own
disclosed endgame risk — a documented gap, not a judgment.

**Precision.** No options chain exists, so there is no ATM IV to check the implied return
against. Beta 4.90 and a +98.94% move are the only vol evidence available, and both say
the range is wide. Quote the implied return as roughly 6%–16%, not to two decimals.

## 5. Falsifiers

What would make me revisit — each tagged break (story over) or shift (revalue):

1. **AR conversion (shift).** Q3'26 (due ~2026-11-11) shows receivables falling in absolute
   dollars while revenue holds. That is the single number that moves the DCF from 8% to
   12%. If instead AR grows again alongside revenue, the pass hardens to a **break**.
2. **A clean FY2026 audit from UHY on the consolidated group (shift).** With named
   component auditors for the PRC and Japanese subsidiaries and no going-concern or
   internal-control adverse opinion.
3. **Management holds an earnings call and takes questions (shift).** Three years of
   silence is the cheapest thing on this list to fix, and not fixing it is information.
4. **Customer concentration disclosed and bounded (shift).** The 10-K's concentration note
   showing no customer above ~10% of revenue would defuse the 5-customer line.
5. **Any of: SEC comment letter, auditor resignation, Nasdaq deficiency notice, restatement,
   or a large discounted shelf takedown (break).**

## 8. kill-thesis

**UNPROVEN.** Conditions: 4 — (1) the reported earnings cannot be verified from any source
reachable this run; (2) the implied return is indeterminate, spanning 6.19%–15.65% on one
unverifiable input, so price gives no margin of safety; (3) no moat mechanism was found and
every revenue line was bought inside 15 months, the highest-margin one holding 5 corporate
customers; (4) ownership means accepting a VIE claim over PRC entities with an open S-3
shelf. Refuted: 0. UNKNOWN: 2 (related-party revenue; PRC/Japan component auditors).

**Attacks that landed against my own draft.** Three, and they changed the shape of the pass:
the auditor-churn point runs backwards (UHY is an upgrade over Beckles); the 3-employee
datum is almost certainly a stale shell-era field; and the Jan-2026 placement at $5.07, ~10%
under the current price, is not a distressed print. Most importantly, **the valuation leg of
the original draft was refuted** — a defensible bull parameterization returns 11.73%–15.65%,
+185 to +577bp over the hurdle. The pass as shipped therefore rests only on condition (1),
and condition (1) is unverifiable rather than refuted. That is what UNPROVEN means here.

**What would flip the verdict:** reading the Q2 10-Q's customer-concentration and
related-party notes, and seeing Q3'26 receivables fall in dollars while revenue holds.
