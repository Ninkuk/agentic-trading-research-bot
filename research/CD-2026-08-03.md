## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS.** The market is paying $198.8M of enterprise value for a business with
$2.35M of trailing revenue, a −$3.9M operating loss, 13 employees, and eight
mutually unrelated business lines announced in nine months — the most recent
being a board-approved initiative to explore *space-based AI infrastructure*.
To earn ~10%/yr from here the operating business needs ~$13.9M of sustainable
free cash flow; it currently produces **−$2.42M**.

kill-thesis verdict: **SOUND** (5 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 1 UNKNOWN).
A PASS is a lower bar than a BUY — the unread disclosures could only make the
picture worse, so their absence does not threaten this direction. That
asymmetry is why SOUND was reachable here and is stated plainly, not claimed as
unusual rigour.

## 2. Business

**Created.** Genuinely unclear, and that is the finding. The registrant is the
former **Mercurity Fintech Holding (MFH)**, rebranded to Chaince Digital on
2025-11-13. Cayman-incorporated (ISIN KYG594672027), NYC HQ, 13 employees, CEO
Shi Qiu, founded 2011, CIK 0001527762. The one durable, checkable thing it does
is **Chaince Securities LLC**, a FINRA-licensed broker-dealer (licence received
Feb 2025), which on 2026-07-14 was named exclusive sales agent for a $50M ATM
program for another microcap (WETO). That is a real service with a real fee.

**Captured.** Advisory and consulting fees, IPO-preparation work, and
broker-dealer agency commissions — deal-contingent and lumpy. TTM revenue
$2,349,549 at a 62.19% gross margin. Nothing recurring is disclosed.

**Protected.** Nothing. A FINRA broker-dealer licence is obtainable; hundreds
exist. On the tokenization side the actual infrastructure is supplied by third
parties (Apex Group, Tokeny) — CD is the distributor. Its own Chief Strategy
Officer said at LD Micro that *"distribution, not tokenization, is the main
challenge"* — and CD is a 13-person firm with no distribution. **The silence
here is the answer.**

## 3. Threads pulled

**The eight business lines.** In nine months CD announced: tokenization/
blockchain, the broker-dealer, AI/HPC data-centre infrastructure, liquid
cooling, a **$200M** "precision components gigafactory" JV with ZJK Industrial
(2025-11-24), a USDC yield vault on Morpho (2026-06-15), "tokenized mineral
assets" out of Argentina (2026-03-18), and **space-based AI infrastructure**
(2026-06-11). Thirteen employees. A $200M JV commitment from a company holding
$39.9M of cash is not a plan; the announcement cadence *is* the business model.

**The SOLAI deal — the funding gap.** CD proposed to take SOLAI (NYSE: SLAI)
private for **$57.3M cash** on 2026-03-12, revised 2026-04-23 to $3.162/ADS =
**170% of NAV**. CD holds $39.9M. The deal exceeds its entire cash balance, and
paying 1.7× net asset value in cash destroys value on its face. It remains
"preliminary non-binding" with no completion news in the 3½ months since. Two
readings, both bad for an owner: it is funded by issuing stock, or it never
closes.

**Dilution.** Shares outstanding 79,443,800, **+12.79% YoY**. A 2025-12-15
private placement sold 1,000,000 shares at $6.14 ($6.14M gross); those buyers
are −51% today. This is the financing mechanism, and it is the incentive that
explains the press-release cadence.

**The circular news flow.** Separate releases announce Russell 2000 inclusion
(2026-07-07), that BlackRock/State Street/UBS hold the stock (2025-11-18), and
"over 100 institutional holders based on 13F filings" (2026-02-18). Those
holders own it *because* it is in the Russell 2000 — the index is mechanical
and the ownership is its consequence, so both are presented as achievements
while being the same fact. A "Wellball VIP League" sponsorship is also a press
release.

**The price.** $36.77 intraday high (2025-10-06) → $2.99 = **−91.9% in ten
months**; −27.5% over 52 weeks; beta 8.44. Two full round-trips since: +41% on
2026-02-27 and +70% over 2026-05-14→18 into the LD Micro appearance, both fully
retraced.

**Dead end — the unexplained −52%.** The fall from $10.49 (2026-05-18) to $4.99

find a cause. The honest reading is that this stock moves on flow, not

**Dead end — a net-cash floor.** Net cash is $38,718,937 = **$0.49/share**

## 4. Valuation

**`reverse_dcf` REFUSED — exit 2:** `base_fcf must be positive, got -2424133.0`.
That is the category error the tool exists to catch, and it is the answer:
there is no cash flow to discount.

Inverting instead — what the price demands. Net cash means enterprise value
($198,818,025) is the right claim on the operating business here:

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Enterprise value | $198,818,025 |
| Required steady-state FCF @ ~10%, g=3% | **$13,917,262** (solver returns 10.21%) |
| TTM revenue | $2,349,549 |
| Implied revenue @ 25% FCF margin | $55.7M = **23.7× current** |
| Implied revenue @ 15% FCF margin | $92.8M = **39.5× current** |

Assumptions stated: 3-year explicit horizon, 3% growth, 3% terminal, ~10%
required return; enterprise value used because the company holds net cash, so
EV is *below* market cap and is the cleaner claim on operations. The
25%/15% FCF margins are illustrative ceilings for an advisory/brokerage
business — CD's current operating margin is **−166.81%**, so reaching either
requires a ~190-point margin swing *and* 24–40× revenue growth simultaneously.

Put in per-share terms: $0.49 of the $2.99 is net cash. **$2.50 — 84% of the
price — is payment for a business losing $3.9M a year on $2.35M of revenue.**

No forward multiple is quoted: there are no analyst estimates to build one from
(see §6).
