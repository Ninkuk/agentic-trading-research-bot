# PTC Inc. (PTC) — 2026-07-27 — $125.21, mkt cap $14.46B

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $125.** Kill-thesis: **UNPROVEN** (5 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 2
unverifiable). A sticky PLM/CAD franchise at a ~10% implied return on guided numbers is
interesting, but the growth is running on banked deferred ARR while the new-bookings engine is
admitted "approximately" flat — and earnings land in 2 days (Jul 29 PM). The price pays
roughly fairly for the AI-transition and bookings uncertainty; it does not overpay you for it.

Thesis attacked: system-of-record industrial software (Creo/Windchill/Codebeamer/ServiceMax)
with decades-long switching costs; post-IoT-divestiture pure-play compounding HSD ARR into
low-teens per-share via a buyback ~9%/yr of market cap; AI as modernization tailwind, not
seat-killer.

## 2. Business

- **Creates**: engineering systems of record — CAD geometry (Creo, Onshape), product
  lifecycle/config data (Windchill, Arena), requirements/ALM (Codebeamer), service (ServiceMax).
  Customers are regulated, complex manufacturers; the data is the company's product memory.
- **Captures**: per-seat/per-tier subscriptions; cc ARR $2.388B +8.5% YoY (Q2 FY26). GM 84.7%,
  GAAP op margin 40.6% TTM, capex $10M — extreme operating leverage: FY21→TTM revenue
  $1.81B→$3.00B while opinc tripled $381M→$1.16B (direction: strongly positive).
- **Protects**: switching cost is a mechanism for PLM/ALM (decades of validated product
  records; regulated-process lock-in), weaker for CAD (migrations happen; Onshape is PTC's own
  proof lighter-weight works). Growth moat undemonstrated — see threads.
