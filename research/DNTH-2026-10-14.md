## 2. Business

**Created:** CIDP and MMN are immune-mediated nerve diseases. Today most
patients are treated with IVIg: hours-long infusions every few weeks, with
supply constraints and infusion-centre burden. In gMG the field is crowded
(FcRn blockers, C5 inhibitors). Claseprubart's proposition is efficacy in the
classical-complement mechanism, given as a 2 mL self-injection every two or
four weeks, with no signal so far of serious infections or drug-induced lupus
(8-K ex-99.1, 2026-08-04). The surplus to the patient is time and autonomy,
on top of a convenience-led share of a market already served by IVIg and
efgartigimod.

**Captured:** Nothing yet. Revenue is license income only: $2.8M (FY2023),
$6.2M (FY2024), $2.0M (FY2025), $1.2M (6M 2026). The XBRL tags reference
Zenas, Tenacia, Alloy, Iontas and a "Leads License Agreement". The payer and
the licensor milestone and royalty obligations were not in the 10-Q excerpt
read (§6). Future capture would be specialty-biologic pricing, either
self-commercialized or partnered. Neither path is disclosed.

**Protected:** For now, by composition-of-matter IP on the antibody and the
active-C1s-only selectivity. That is a mechanism claim this run cannot
evaluate scientifically (domain science: UNKNOWN, bounded below). The
competitive evidence cuts against a durable edge. The approved incumbent in
CIDP (Vyvgart Hytrulo, weekly SC) has randomized data. The other C1s antibody
(riliprubart) failed futility in refractory CIDP. argenx's anti-C2
empasiprubart is reported in late-stage MMN/CIDP development (low-confidence,
unverified this run). Any moat here is regulatory exclusivity plus dosing
convenience, not a mechanism that stops a rival.

**Control:** single class of common stock (10-Q cover, 55,934,378 shares);
