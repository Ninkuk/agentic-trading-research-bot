## 2. Business

**Created:** de facto standard modeling/simulation applications
(MicroStation, STAAD, OpenRoads, SYNCHRO, Seequent, Power Line Systems) plus
the system-of-record data layer (ProjectWise / Bentley Infrastructure Cloud)
for infrastructure engineering — roads, bridges, rail, grid, water, mining.
Customers are ~5,800-employee-scale engineering firms and owner-operators with
near-record backlogs and a demographically shrinking engineer pool; the
software is a factor of production, ~1% of a design project's cost (management:
~$2,000 BSY ARR per $1M design billings; labor ≈ 89%).

**Captured:** not "subscriptions" — three distinct mechanisms. (a) E365
consumption subscriptions billed on attended usage days, with embedded success
teams (45% of revenue from 220 accounts >$1M/yr; ~2/3 from 824 accounts
>$250K/yr — Q1'26 call). (b) Virtuoso annual subscriptions for SMB (600+ new
logos/qtr, now upsell-driven). (c) Asset Analytics — per-asset consumption
subscriptions (>$50M run-rate) — plus Cohesive (Maximo services). 93% of
revenue is subscription; 99% cc account retention; NRR 109%.

**Protected:** switching costs by mechanism — DOT/agency design standards
and deliverable formats specify these tools; decades of project archives in
ProjectWise; engineers trained on them; and the simulation engines are
regulatory-validated deterministic physics codes where liability attaches to
output correctness. An AI-native entrant can generate geometry; it cannot
quickly earn code-compliance trust or migrate 40 years of archives. Autodesk
is the real competitor (verticals overlap in civil), but the horizontal-infra
incumbency has held for decades. Data-stewardship stance (never train on
customer data unasked — Q1 call) is itself a selling point to
IP-sensitive engineering firms.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive** and steady. FY21→FY25: revenue
$965M→$1,502M, op income $94.6M→$362.6M, margin 9.8%→24.1%; flat ~330M
diluted shares. Management guides ~100bp/yr AOI improvement.
