# SFM — Sprouts Farmers Market — 2026-07-27

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Pass at $76.83.** Kill-thesis: **UNPROVEN** — 5 load-bearing conditions, 0
refuted, 3 UNKNOWN. A genuinely good, self-funded specialty grocer with an
~8%/yr unit-growth runway and a clean balance sheet — but at today's price the
implied return is only ~8%/yr *and* that arithmetic requires the record 7.6%
operating margin (vs 5.1–5.6% pre-2024) to be durable, while comps are
negative, management itself guides margin pressure, and Q2 prints in two days
(Jul 29 after close). The price pays for margin durability that cannot be
verified from disclosures.

## 2. Business

- **Created:** a curated health-and-wellness grocery (483 small-box stores,
  25 states) — attribute-driven assortment (organic, functional, "foraged"
  innovation) that conventional grocers stock late or not at all; the customer
  is buying trust and discovery, not price. E-commerce is 16% of sales, +10%
  YoY (Q1'26 call).
- **Captured:** ~39% gross margin (39.4% Q1'26) — far above conventional
  grocery — via differentiated/private-label mix and a small (~30k sqft)
  box; TTM operating margin 7.59%, net margin 5.70% (stockanalysis
  statistics).
- **Protected:** narrow mechanisms, honestly: vendor "trusted partner for
  innovative product launches" status (first-shelf access for viral/new
  brands), foraging/merchandising cadence, loyalty data (program launched
  2025). No structural barrier stops Kroger/Whole Foods/Trader Joe's from
  encroaching; the moat is speed and identity, not lock-in.

**Operating leverage trajectory — positive.** Revenue FY21→FY25:
$6,100M → $6,404M → $6,837M → $7,719M → $8,806M; operating income
$334M → $358M → $350M → $505M → $686M; margin 5.5% → 5.6% → 5.1% → 6.5% →
7.8% (TTM 7.6%). Income compounded ~2× revenue's rate 2023→2025
(stockanalysis financials).

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make an owner sell)

- Two consecutive quarters of comps below -2% (lap excuse expires ~Q3'26).
- Operating margin below 6.5% on a trailing-year basis (reversion underway).
- New-store openings guided below ~35/yr or pipeline shrinking (growth engine
  stalls).
- Gross margin down >100bp YoY (price investment war with conventionals).

## 6. UNKNOWNs

1. **Durable vs transient share of the 230bp margin expansion since 2023** —
   not decomposable from disclosures; would require a gross-margin bridge
   (shrink, mix, self-distribution) the company doesn't publish. Doesn't kill
   the thesis; it *is* the thesis risk — this is why the verdict is UNPROVEN.
2. **10-K commitments footnote unchecked** — self-distribution DC / purchase
   obligations could pin capex above the trailing base. Mitigated: the base
   FCF already carries elevated growth capex; absence widens the range, does
   not kill.
3. **Official financials cross-check** — `sec_fundamentals.db` carries no SFM
   row; all statement figures here are stockanalysis-sourced (vetted
   exception), uncorroborated against EDGAR this run.
4. **Wednesday's print** — unknowable by construction; researching into it is
   itself a reason the pass costs little.
