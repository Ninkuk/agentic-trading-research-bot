Management guided 2027 demand "similar to current year" and called Americas

price is the problem. At $174.32 the owner-FCF yield is 4.65%, rates have
risen 40bp since September, and no honestly paid-for growth path clears the
hurdle. On the organic path it clears at roughly $147 (US ERP) to $140
(operations-weighted ERP).

~3.4× sales (the 2025 cohort), an acquired business needs roughly a 35%
pre-tax margin to earn the 8.6% hurdle in year one. Q3 shows acquisitions
*adding* 20bp to International's 13.9% margin and *costing* 40bp of the
Americas' 30.1%, so the acquired businesses sit somewhere between those
margins. That is well below the bar, but it is not a disclosed return.
