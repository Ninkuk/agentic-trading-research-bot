# PIPR — Piper Sandler Companies — 2026-07-30

Spot $73.97 (post 4:1 split of 2026-03-24), market cap $5.2395B, written the morning of
the Q2 2026 print (BMO today; the print is folded in, not researched around).

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Good business, fair-to-full price: PASS at $74.** A sector-specialist middle-market
investment bank earning cycle-high margins (24.2% TTM op margin vs 21.7% at the 2021 boom
peak and 9.1% at the 2023 trough); at today's price the market already pays for the
upcycle, and the implied return on owner earnings (~9.5%) compresses to ~6–8% on any
mid-cycle base. Kill-thesis verdict: **SOUND** (4 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted,
0 unknown) — the attack that came closest was that the mid-cycle anchor understates a
structurally bigger platform (193 MDs, +6% YoY), which lifts mid-cycle implied return
from 6.4% to 8.0% but not over the bar.

## 2. Business

**Creates value:** middle-market corporations, PE sponsors, depository banks, and
municipalities get senior-banker sector depth plus execution/distribution. The density is
real and third-party-verified: #1 US bank M&A by announced count AND deal value in 1H'26,
#1 medtech M&A by deal count (Q2'26 release). Clients pay for the banker who has done the
last twenty deals in their niche.

**Captures value:** success fees per completed deal — advisory is 55% of net revenues
($274M record Q2), plus underwriting spreads (corporate financing $38M, muni $50M record),
commissions/spreads (equity brokerage $63M record, fixed income $49M declining). Employees
capture first: comp ratio 62.3% GAAP / 61.5% adjusted; shareholders get the operating
leverage on the residual (1H op income +42% on +22% revenue).

**Protects value:** deal-flow density per niche, which takes years to replicate — the
CEO's own mechanism on today's call ("getting hired is about deal flow… takes several
years"), offered while dismissing money-center re-entry as "the sixth or seventh cycle"
he's seen. The asset walks out the door nightly; the 10-K names human capital its #2 risk
and concedes capping comp hinders retention. The moat is real but rented, and ~62% of
every fee dollar is the rent.

## 3. Threads pulled

- **Operating leverage is symmetric.** Revenue 2021→2023→TTM: $2.03B → $1.35B → $2.12B;
  op income $442M → $123M → $512M. Off the trough revenue +57%, op income +318% — and it
  ran in reverse 2021→2023 (op income −72% on revenue −34%). This is the thesis.
- **Guidance conservatism.** 8/8 trailing quarters beat estimates (several by 30–45%);
  the May 1 Q1 print fell 9% on a "flat or down Q2" guide that Q2 then beat by 21 points.
  Today's beat was met with +0.2%. The market prices the cycle, not the quarter.
- **Owner flow vs FCF.** FCF $476M adds back non-cash comp; share count is flat YoY
  (+0.12%) only because ~$200M/yr of buybacks (H1: $101M at avg $79.12) absorbs grant
  dilution. Adjusted net income (~$336M TTM, sum of adjusted EPS × diluted shares)
  already expenses stock comp and is the honest owner flow; subtracting the buyback from
  FCF as well would double-count.
- **Minority interest is noise, not a stable claim.** TTM NCI $56.5M is inflated by
  2H'25 fund marks; Q2'26 NCI was $2.3M and Q2'25 was −$10.4M (8-K). Adjusted figures
  exclude it — another reason to prefer adjusted NI over consolidated FCF.
- **Insider filings: dead end.** Forms 4 (5/21, 6/15, 7/1) are code-A director grants;
  two small May Form 144s. No signal either way.
- **Asset-light claim survives the commitments note.** $30.9M of fund commitments only;
  no take-or-pay/capacity obligations. Capex $25.7M TTM (1.2% of revenue). Offsetting
  soft item: New York office double-expense runs through 2026 into 2027 (call).
- **Fixed income (-14% YoY) is the one declining line** — depository clients hostage to
  the fed-funds-to-5yr spread (President, today's call); ~10% of revenue.
- **Dead end: sec_fundamentals cross-check.** PIPR is not in the DB's 294-company
  universe; EPS actuals were cross-checked against today's 8-K instead (GAAP $0.95;
  Robinhood's "$1.04 actual" is the adjusted figure).

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make this a buy / reopen)

- Price at or below ~$60, where the $300M mid-cycle owner flow implies ≥9.3%.
- Evidence the middle-market fee pool is durable through a rate/recession shock with
  PIPR's operating margin holding above ~18% — that would prove the "new baseline" case
  and retire the mid-cycle discount.
- (Against the pass, watch for) sponsor-activity release: CEO says close rates are low
  and sponsor M&A still depressed — a broad sponsor upcycle could extend earnings growth
  well past the guided flat Q3.
