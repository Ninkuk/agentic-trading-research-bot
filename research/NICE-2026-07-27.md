## 1. Verdict and thesis

conditions, 0 refuted, 2 unverifiable. The price already implies a mild-decline

## 2. Business

**Created:** CXone runs enterprise contact centers in the cloud (routing, WFM,
recording, digital channels, now AI agents via Cognigy); Actimize watches
banks' transactions for fraud/AML/market-abuse in real time. Customers get
regulatory-grade reliability and one throat to choke across voice + digital +
AI at enterprise scale.

**Captured:** subscription cloud fees (FY25 cloud $2,238M, 76% of revenue,
per-seat plus usage/consumption), declining on-prem maintenance ($560M
services) and licenses ($147M). Segment economics (20-F Note 16, FY2025):
Customer Engagement $2,460M rev / $665M segment op income (27%); Financial
Crime & Compliance $485M / $167M (34%). Actimize is largely orthogonal to the
AI-seat fear — compliance software priced per assets/coverage, not per agent.

**Protected:** enterprise switching costs (telephony + compliance recording +
integrations), regulatory certification depth in Actimize, and now a tier-1
agentic-AI asset (Cognigy, $887.3M, closed 2025-09-08; $390M assigned to
technology). The honest answer on the AI moat: unproven. NICE's claim — only
vendor unifying voice, digital, and agentic AI at enterprise scale — is
plausible and is exactly what Salesforce/Sierra/Genesys are attacking.

Operating-leverage trajectory (income statement, stockanalysis, cross-checked
20-F): revenue $1,921M→$3,014M (2021→TTM) while operating income $264M→$624M;
margin 13.7%→20.7%. **Direction: positive**, every year. SBC fell in absolute
dollars ($183M→$138M); diluted shares 66.9M→62.4M.
