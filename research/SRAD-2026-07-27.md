# SRAD — Sportradar Group AG — 2026-07-27

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Kill-thesis verdict: UNPROVEN (conditions=5, refuted=0, unknown=2). Ownership call: PASS at $14.68.**

Sportradar is a genuinely good business — a two-sided rights-and-data toll road between
sports leagues and bookmakers, growing mid-teens cc with expanding cash conversion, net
cash, an accelerated buyback, and a founder who just bought $10M of stock — but at
today's price the conservative-path implied return is only ~8%/yr, so ownership requires
believing management's own quantification of the exact thing two short sellers and (per
the shorts) three regulators are investigating. That number is unverifiable from any
disclosure, and the Q2 print lands in five trading days with the options market pricing
a ±15.5% mean move — the honest position is to wait, not to own.

## 2. Business

**Creates:** collects and produces official live data, odds, streaming, and trading
services across ~1M matches/yr (NBA, MLB, NHL, ATP, UEFA, FIFA; tennis/golf/UFC via the
IMG Arena portfolio acquired 2026). A sportsbook gets its entire content/odds/risk supply
chain from one vendor instead of negotiating dozens of league deals and building scouting
+ low-latency infrastructure. NRR 108% (Q1'26 call).

**Captures:** fixed fee + revenue share with minimum guarantees; ~2/3 of revenue fixed-fee,
1/3 variable (CFO, Q1'26 call). Product ladder (data → odds → managed trading (MTS) →
AV streaming → marketing/fan engagement → integrity services) cross-sold into one global
client base; >75% of core betting clients now take IMG content.

**Protects:** exclusive official league rights (a competitor cannot legally sell official
NBA/MLB data), scale economics in scouting/production, and switching costs in the
integrated odds/trading stack. Effective duopoly with Genius Sports. The caveat: the
exclusivity is *rented* — leagues re-auction at renewal, so part of the moat's rent
escalates structurally. Off-balance rights commitments are only €233.5M (20-F Note 26);
contracted rights sit on-balance and amortize straight-line, and the CFO states all major
deals are locked long-term.

## 4. Valuation

€300M net cash is ignored, a small conservative buffer). Base: $215M (€187M TTM ×

That is ~21× owner FCF (~4.7% yield) with FY26 guidance implying owner FCF +~26%
(conversion >56% of €390–400M adj EBITDA).

|---|---|---|---|
| management-track | 25/18/14/10/8% | 2.5% | **~11%/yr** |

legal/regulatory status of betting and dependence on league partners; 2.5% terminal is
defensible only on the management path where rights renewals keep their current
economics — the bear path's 1.5% is the honest terminal under rights-cost ratchet.

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make me revisit a pass → buy, or flee if owned)

- A named regulator closes its review without action, or audited disclosure isolates
  unregulated-market revenue near management's figure → thesis strengthens materially.
- FY26 guidance cut (cc revenue or EBITDA) at Q2 (Aug 3) → margin-expansion story breaks.
- License suspension or formal action in any major jurisdiction → the cascade risk in
  the 20-F's own risk factors activates; exit territory.
- Loss of a tier-1 league renewal to Genius Sports → moat-rent repricing confirmed.
- Buyback suspension → signal of legal-reserve need.

## 6. UNKNOWNs

- **True gray-market revenue share** — the load-bearing number. Exists only as
  management's self-estimate (3–12%) vs the shorts' claim (40%+). No disclosure
  isolates it; only a regulator finding or new audited segment data would. Its absence
  is what holds the verdict at UNPROVEN — it does not kill the business case, but at
  ~8% conservative-path implied return it kills the *ownership* case at this price.
- **Status of the claimed three regulator reviews** — unconfirmed in either direction.
- **Kalshi deal economics** — structure disclosed (fixed fee + rev share + minimum
  guarantee), magnitude not; management's "tens of millions annually" at maturity is
  ~2% of revenue.
- **Rights renewal economics beyond ~2030** (NBA et al.) — outside the modeled horizon,
  loaded into the terminal-growth choice.
- **Q2 actuals** — five trading days away; this run deliberately precedes the print.
