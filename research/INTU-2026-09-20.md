## 1. Verdict and thesis

**BUY at $303.19.** kill-thesis: **SOUND (on this pass)** — conditions=8
(6 probable, 2 plausible), refuted=0, unknown=0, pending=2, not_obtained=0.

**p(beat SPY, 63 td): 0.57** · kill-thesis: 0.53

**Disputed expectation:** at 9.4× reported and 11× tax-normalized free cash
flow the price earns the 8.98% hurdle only if FCF shrinks ~3%/yr for two
years and never grows again (§4's structural-decline row) — i.e. that the FY27
"reset" is the first year of a decline, not a trough; the Q1 FY27 print
(~Nov 19) showing operating margin on the guided path with business
platform ≥13% is what forces the revision, and a miss on either is what
confirms the market.

The business half of Intuit — QuickBooks, payments, payroll, mid-market,
~56% of FY27 guided revenue — is guided +13–14% with 83% retention and
customers still growing, and it carries the accountant distribution nobody
else has. The consumer half is decelerating on purpose: DIY tax is being
repriced downward to stop share loss, and assisted tax, the engine meant to
replace it, is guided to the teens. At $303 a tax-normalized $7.35B of free
cash flow clears a 9.0% cost of equity by ~330bp even if growth is 5%, and
by ~150bp with every dollar of stock compensation charged as cash. You are
paying a no-growth price for a business whose larger half is growing in the
teens; the TurboTax bet is not in the price and is not in the base case.

**Closest attack:** assisted tax. The thesis's replacement engine grew 37%
by moving existing TurboTax customers up a tier (75% of the growth), is now
guided to the teens, and the new-to-franchise Live customer it needs next is
the one Intuit has the least evidence of winning. If Live lands at ~12% and
DIY keeps shrinking double digits, TurboTax is flat-to-down in FY28 and
consumer's 4–8% algorithm rests on Credit Karma alone. Second: management
itself declined to let the three-year CAGRs be underwritten before FY27
execution is visible — the algorithm is a statement of intent. Neither
refutes a condition; §4's 5%-growth row is roughly what both failing
together looks like and it clears the hurdle.
