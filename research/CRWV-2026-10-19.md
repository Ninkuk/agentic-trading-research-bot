## 2. Business

**Created:** Customers get dense, NVIDIA-first GPU clusters faster than the
three hyperscalers deliver them, with software tuned for training and
inference. That includes being first to new GPU generations, and NVIDIA named
its software stack a reference architecture (Goldman conference,
2026-09-08). The surplus is time-to-compute for labs where months of delay
cost market position.

**Captured:** Three mechanisms.
- Multi-year take-or-pay capacity contracts (the $103.7B RPO), often
  prepaid in part, priced per GPU-hour. A July 2026 change raised prices
  ~25% across SKUs, and component-cost increases pass through (Q2 call).
- Add-ons: storage, CPU, networking and software at >$400M ARR.
- Managed inference: >$100M ARR, target ≥$250M by YE-26.
The core is a financing spread. Contracts are posted as collateral for SPV
delayed-draw term loans, so CoreWeave captures the gap between the
contracted GPU-hour yield and the cost of debt plus depreciation.

**Protected:** Weakly. The defenses are mechanisms with expiry dates:
contracted backlog (switching costs run only for the contract term), power
contracted ahead of rivals (4.2 GW contracted, 1.5 GW active), and allocation
priority with NVIDIA. None stops a hyperscaler or another neocloud with
cheaper capital from bidding the next contract. Management calls the
incumbents "an oligarchy of three massive companies," and those incumbents
have a far lower cost of capital. The silence on a cost-of-capital moat is
the answer: CoreWeave's funding is more expensive than its competitors', not
less.

**Control:** dual-class. As of 2026-07-31 (Q2 10-Q cover) there are
458,871,690 Class A and 92,664,912 Class B shares; Class B carries 10 votes
per share per the March 2025 S-1 (not re-read this run). That puts roughly
two-thirds of the vote with Class B holders (founders), which forecloses an
unsolicited takeover or an activist path. Insiders hold 19.2% of shares.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue has multiplied ~4× while
operating income went from positive to negative:

|---|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | $0.229B | −$0.014B | −$0.594B |
