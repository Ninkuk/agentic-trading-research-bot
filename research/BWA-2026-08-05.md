## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Ownership call: PASS at $66.77.** Kill-thesis verdict: **UNPROVEN** (6 load-bearing
conditions, 0 refuted, 3 load-bearing UNKNOWNs — all under condition 6, the
data-center business, the only path above a ~9.5% implied return, and every economic
fact about it is currently withheld). The attack that came closest: the base-rate-
plus-own-history attack — BWA entered its last hot adjacency (EV charging) and
exited it in 2025 at $0.13/share of exit costs; the turbine business differs in kind
(radial turbomachinery is the actual core competence, with an exclusive committed
counterparty), but the precedent is real and cited. Second-closest: three straight
years of "non-recurring" charges — held off by H1'26 evidence (GAAP operating income
now ~90% of adjusted, vs ~35% for FY25).

The thesis in plain language: BorgWarner is a scaled, cost-disciplined powertrain
incumbent earning ~11% adjusted operating margins and ~$1B/yr of free cash flow in a
flat-to-declining light-vehicle market, returning most of that cash via buybacks
(share count −4.1% YoY, authorization raised today by $1B to ~$1.35B through 2029).
At $66.77 the market already prices roughly a **7.5–9.5%** annual return on the auto
business alone; getting paid more than that requires the six-month-old data-center
power business (turbine generators, BESS, grid-tie inverters) to become real revenue
in 2027 — a business with committed capacity, shipping B-samples, and **zero booked
revenue**. Fair price for the core, unpriceable option on top, after a +74% year and
a +6% print day. There are cheaper ways to wait for proof; the reopen trigger below
is the proof date.

## 2. Business

**Creates value.** BWA sells the hardware that makes powertrains efficient:
turbochargers (largest product line), EGR, variable cam timing, transfer cases and
dual-clutch modules, plus e-products (motors, inverters, iDM, commercial-vehicle
battery packs). OEMs buy decades of turbomachinery/power-electronics engineering,
a localized global footprint (84% of sales outside the US; strong China position),
and launch reliability they cannot cheaply replicate in-house — 82% light-vehicle,
10% commercial-vehicle, 5% off-highway, 3% aftermarket (FY25 10-K).

**Captures value.** Multi-year platform awards under negotiated annual contracts and
LTAs; content per vehicle across combustion, hybrid, and BEV architectures. Capture
is structurally constrained by OEM bargaining power (VW 13%, Ford 12% of FY25 sales;
top ten customers 71%) and annual price-downs; BWA's counter is cost control — Q2'26
adjusted operating margin 11.3% (+100bp YoY), decremental conversion held to
mid-teens, seven consecutive quarterly EPS beats ($0.05–$0.17 over estimate,
Robinhood estimate vs release actuals — the "actual" is adjusted EPS, consistent
with the print's own definition).

**Protects value.** Program lock-in (design-in for 5–7-year platforms; switching
mid-program is a re-engineering event), scale and localization, and relationship
depth (a 7-year off-highway controller extension, turbo conquest wins at European
OEMs, Q1'26 call). This is incumbency-and-capability protection, not pricing power —
and it erodes at the rate the combustion/hybrid pie erodes. Written down honestly:
**the moat is real but rented from the ICE/hybrid production cycle.**

## 5. Falsifiers (what would make an owner sell / keeps this a pass)

1. **FY26 FCF guide cut below ~$800M** or adjusted operating margin below 10.5%
   without a weighted-production excuse — the cost-control engine is the thesis.
2. **Turbine generator slips out of 2027**, the Endeavour/TurboCell agreement is
   terminated or renegotiated, or no capacity-expansion decision is announced by the
   Q4'26 call — the option expires worthless and scenario C dies.
3. **A new impairment/restructuring cycle** after 2024–25's "non-recurring" charges
   — would flip the closing GAAP/adjusted gap back into a pattern.
4. **Weighted LV production down >5% YoY** — mid-teens decrementals on a $14B base
   overwhelm the buyback.
5. **Capital-discipline break**: a large acquisition outside management's three
   stated criteria (competence leverage, accretive, fair price).

## 6. UNKNOWNs

1. **Turbine-generator unit economics.** Pricing, margin, and backlog are all
   withheld ("multiples of $300M" for 2 GW is the only bound). Resolves late
   2026/early 2027 per management. Absence doesn't kill the pass-verdict thesis —
   it *is* the reason for it: scenario C cannot be underwritten today.
2. **Endeavour/TurboCell counterparty quality.** Private company; exclusivity
   terms, credit, and captive-vs-merchant demand mix undisclosed. Would come from
   the MSA terms if ever filed, or from customer announcements. Material to C only.
3. **BESS competitive position.** Entering against Tesla Megapack, Fluence, CATL,
   BYD with an automotive-derived pack. Domain call I cannot make from filings —
   marked UNKNOWN, counted against C, not bluffed.
4. **True NCI cash claim.** Distributions ranged $49–116M over three years; I used
   the range, not a point. A $70M error moves the implied return ~0.3pp — not
   decision-bearing.
5. **iv30 percentile** (path 1) — structurally unavailable; the options read above
   is labelled stopgap accordingly.
