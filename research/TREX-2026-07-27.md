## 2. Business

**Created:** low-maintenance wood-alternative decking/railing — the customer
trades ~2x wood's upfront cost for no staining/sealing over a 25–50-yr
warranted life; sold through ~6,700 retail outlets (10-K). Outdoor living is
one of the faster-growing R&R categories (10-K).

**Captured:** premium-priced manufactured product at ~40% gross margin
(Q1 2026: 40.5%), sold sell-in to a concentrated two-step distribution layer —
top three customers 65–75% of net sales (CFO, Jul 13 call) — plus home-center
retail. TTM: revenue $1,178M, operating income $259.8M (22.1%), FCF $226.5M
(stockanalysis statistics; EDGAR-consistent).

**Protected:** three concrete mechanisms. (1) Brand pull-through — consumers
and pro contractors ask for Trex by name; management's stated (and analysts'
unchallenged) framing is that distributors need Trex more than the reverse.
(2) Feedstock: recycled LDPE, domestically sourced, priced with a lag to and
less volatility than virgin PE — a structural COGS advantage vs competitors on
virgin inputs (CFO, Q1 2026 call). (3) Scale in a consolidating category the
CEO describes as top-3-and-everyone-else (Jul 13 call).

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative-to-flat** (EDGAR, annual). Revenue
2019 $745M → 2025 $1,174M (+58%); operating income $188M → $258M (+37%);
margin oscillates 22–28% with no durable expansion (28.0% in 2024, 22.0% in
2025). Revenue has not exceeded 2021's $1,197M in four years.
