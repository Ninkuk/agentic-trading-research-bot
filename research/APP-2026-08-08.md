# APP — AppLovin Corporation — 2026-08-08

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at today's price ($346.75, $116.06B market cap).** An extraordinary business — 898 employees producing $6.83B TTM revenue at a 77% GAAP operating margin — but the price still demands ≥25%/yr revenue growth for five years to clear even a generously band-capped hurdle, and the two things that would justify that path (the e-commerce second act, and uninterrupted model-improvement cadence) are respectively undisclosed in dollars and freshly demonstrated to be lumpy.

**Kill-thesis verdict: SOUND** (on this pass call). Five load-bearing bull conditions enumerated, none refuted, two unknown. The attack that came closest: CFO-guided FY26 FCF normalization (~75% of adj EBITDA ≈ $5.0B vs the $4.5B TTM base) plus capping beta into the stable band narrows the conservative-path shortfall to roughly −200bp — the pass is a judgment about the underwritability of growth, not arithmetic.

Context: APP entered this repo's candidates screen 2026-07-29 (F-score 7, FCF yield 4.0%); composite flags it bullish only on RSI oversold (28.9 on 2026-08-08). The stock fell −19.7% on 2026-08-06 after the Q2 print (revenue $1.924B just under the guided midpoint; adj EBITDA $1.614B just below range) and a wave of PT cuts followed (Benchmark $775→$500, Scotiabank $775→$515, Citi $710→$650).

## 2. Business

**Creates value:** Axon, a deep-learning ad-recommendation engine, matches advertisers to users inside mobile-game inventory (and now e-commerce placements) at return-on-ad-spend targets the advertiser sets. For game developers it is, per management, the largest user-acquisition channel in mobile gaming; for publishers, MAX runs the real-time auction that monetizes their inventory. The customer surplus is measurable ROAS: advertisers spend more only when the model's matching improves.

**Captures value:** take-rate on the ad transaction between advertiser and publisher — captured as revenue with ~88% gross margin because the "factory" is a model plus rented compute (~$0.10 of compute per incremental revenue dollar, CFO on Q2'26 call). Two demand pools, one auction: gaming advertisers (majority of revenue) and consumer/e-commerce advertisers (growing fastest; Q2'26 spend 28% above the Q4'25 seasonal peak).

**Protects value:** in gaming, a genuine data/scale flywheel — 14 years of install-and-monetization data, the largest UA footprint in the category, so its models see more conversions than any competitor's and better models attract more spend, which generates more data. In e-commerce the same mechanism is *asserted* but early: less data density, model admittedly less sophisticated, and the 30–60s video creative unit is a hurdle management concedes is unsolved for the long tail. The moat is real in gaming, unproven in the vertical the growth narrative depends on.

Operating-leverage trajectory (continuing ops, stockanalysis income-statement route; 2021–22 include the since-divested Apps business): revenue 2023 $1.84B → 2024 $3.22B → 2025 $5.48B → TTM $6.83B while operating income went $0.77B → $1.91B → $4.15B → $5.29B. Direction: strongly positive — operating margin 42% → 77% in three years.
