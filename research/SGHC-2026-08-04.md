# SGHC — Super Group (SGHC) Limited — 2026-08-04

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $13.67.** A genuinely good, casino-first online gambling business with
real operating leverage, net cash, and heavy cash returns — but the price
already embeds guidance-level growth: the reverse DCF prints ~8%/yr on
guidance-flavored assumptions and only ~10% even granting five more years of
operating leverage, with a World-Cup comp cliff and a global gaming-tax ratchet
sitting between here and those cash flows. Kill-thesis verdict: **UNPROVEN**
(7 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 2 unknown). Timing note: this run was
written **hours before the Q2 2026 print (AMC today, 2026-08-04)** — every
number here is pre-print by construction.

## 2. Business

**Created.** Two brands: Betway (online sportsbook + casino) and Spin
(multi-brand online casino), licensed across Europe, North America (Canada;
US sportsbook exited 2024), and Africa. The customer gets localized betting and
casino product — local payments, local marketing, local sports content — in
markets global majors underserve (Betway South Africa is the anchor; Nigeria,
Botswana ramping). Q1-26 monthly active customers 6.4M, +18% y/y (Q1-26 press
release).

**Captured.** Net gaming revenue (accounted as IFRS 9 derivatives, not IFRS 15
— the "revenue" line is stakes minus payouts minus incentives). Q1-26 split:
iGaming $489M of $606M segment revenue = **81% casino**; sportsbook is the
acquisition funnel (World Cup cross-sell to casino "normally 60–70%" — CEO,
Q1-26 call). Segments since Q1-26: Africa ($267M, 44%, +33% y/y) and
International ($339M, 56%, +9% y/y). Gross margin 30.4% (after gaming duties,
payment processing, content fees); operating margin 19.3% TTM.

**Protected.** The mechanism, where it exists, is local: payment rails,
brand, and licenses in African markets, plus a 2.7k-employee proprietary
marketing/data engine. Casino content itself is licensed and replicable; the
sportsbook software was third-party until the group bought it in-house
(€24M, owns it from 2026-02-28 — Q1-26 press release). In the UK/Ontario it
competes head-on with Flutter/Entain/bet365 with no structural edge — the
call's own framing is share gains from "product improvement," not moat. The
protection is real in Africa and thin in International; write that down.

## 3. Threads pulled

- **Operating-leverage trajectory (Phase 0, printed):** revenue / IFRS
  operating income: 2021 $1.50B/$225M → 2022 $1.39B/$110M → 2023 $1.56B/$37M →
  2024 $1.84B/$162M → 2025 $2.23B/$348M → TTM $2.33B/$449M
  (stockanalysis.com income-statement route). Direction: **strongly positive**
  since the 2023 trough (US sportsbook exit year). The 12× opinc move on +50%
  revenue is the bull case's engine and it is real on IFRS numbers, not only
  adjusted ones.
- **FCF conversion vs the "75%" claim:** CFO stated "free cash flow conversion
  of 75%" (Q1-26 call). Measured: TTM FCF $319M / TTM adj EBITDA ~$565M ≈
  **56%**. The gap is mostly cash taxes — effective tax rate 39.7% (2024),
  38.8% (2025), 29.5% (Q1-26). The 75% figure does not reconcile with the
  GAAP-derived FCF on any route I checked; treated as unverified management
  framing, not an input. (Whether the tax drag is structural — gaming-duty
  geography — or transient is an UNKNOWN below.)
- **UK tax ratchet:** disclosed hit ~$30M ≈ 6% of FY25 EBITDA pre-mitigation,
  effective 2026-04-01; management says April data shows no massive impact and
  mitigation levers are being pulled (Q1-26 call). One jurisdiction's ratchet
  absorbed is not evidence the class of risk is absorbed — the FLS list in the
  press release names regulation, license renewal, payment-processor access,
  and gray-market blocking as standing risks.
- **World Cup 2026:** 88% of 2025 revenue comes from World-Cup-participating
  markets; 73% of GGR from football; 104 matches Jun–Jul 2026 (63% more than
  2022) (Q1-26 call + deck). Q2/Q3 get the bump; 2027 laps it. Guidance
  (revenue >$2.55B, adj EBITDA >$680M) was reaffirmed, not raised, at Q1.
- **Momentum entry, not dislocation:** the candidates screen admitted SGHC on
  the off-52-week-high branch, but the stock is +29% y/y and +46% over 6
  months; the "high" it sits below is the 2021 SPAC-era print. This is a
  momentum name at 22× P/FCF, not a drawdown.
- **ZAR "Super coin" crypto wallet:** beta-launched mid-April for Betway SA
  customers, exchange listings on OVEX and VALR planned (Q1-26 call). A
  regulated-gambling operator issuing a crypto token into its own betting
  ecosystem is a governance/regulatory surface the market may not be pricing;
  currently immaterial to revenue. Flagged, not weighted.
- **Dead end — vendor net-income conflict:** TTM net income reads $93M
  (income-statement route `netIncome`), $245M (statistics route), $384M
  (`netincCompany`). The statistics figure is consistent with summed quarterly
  profits (FY25 $217M − Q1-25 $59M + Q1-26 $86M ≈ $244M); the others look like
  route artifacts around the segment-reporting change. No thesis weight placed
  on any of them; profit direction is taken from the operating line.
- **Dead end — insider split:** stats show insiders 2.92% yet float is only
  266M of 508M shares (52%). The two can't both describe the founder block.
  Resolution would need the 20-F ownership section; not load-bearing for a
  pass verdict, left unresolved.
- **Composite/DB cross-check:** composite has never flagged SGHC (latest
  2026-08-04 snapshot: 0 bullish, 0 bearish, 1 informational, score 0);
  sec_fundamentals.db has no CIK row for it (foreign private issuer, 20-F
  filed 2026-04-17 per press release).

## 5. Falsifiers (what would flip PASS → look again)

- Q2/Q3 prints plus a **raised** FY26 guide that pushes forward FCF toward the
  $425M+ zone at a price ≤ today's — the aggressive scenario's ~10% becomes
  the base and the pass weakens.
- The Q3-26 print (first post-World-Cup read, ~2026-11-03) showing underlying
  ex-WC growth still ≥ 10% — kills the comp-cliff condition.
- Cash tax rate durably below ~30% (20-F tax note or two consecutive
  quarters), repairing FCF conversion toward management's 75% claim.
- Price: a return to the ~$10–11 zone (Mar–Apr 2026 levels) mechanically puts
  the base-case implied return near 10–11% with no assumption changes.
