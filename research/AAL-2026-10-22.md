## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $12.585.** kill-thesis: **FLAWED** — conditions=4 (1 probable,
3 plausible), refuted=2, unknown=0, pending=1, not_obtained=0.

**p(beat SPY, 63 td): 0.40** · kill-thesis: 0.38
**Disputed expectation:** the market prices an $8.3B option on a firm whose
trailing levered FCF is −$91M and needs ~$0.7B of sustained levered FCF to
clear a 10.93% hurdle; what revises it is the FY27 guide on Jan 26 — FCF
and net debt at the then-forward fuel curve — or a fuel break the company
has chosen not to insure.

American is selling more, at higher prices, to better customers than at any
time in its history, and it still cannot turn that into profit or cash
because its non-fuel costs and its capex grow nearly as fast as the
revenue, and fuel takes the rest. The Q3 print shows the mechanism cleanly:
revenue more than recaptured the fuel increase, and operating income fell
anyway. The balance sheet is not in danger before 2028 ($11.8B liquidity,
revolvers out to 2031), but the deleveraging story — the thing that would
turn the equity's option value into a claim on cash — has been pushed out
again by management's own guide. At $12.585 the equity is cheap only if
fuel breaks, and that is a bet on a price, not on the business.

**Closest attack:** the revenue print plus the oversold tape. Q3 revenue
growth beat the top of guide, Q4 is guided higher still, AAL fell from
$18.15 (July 1) to an $11.68 close on Oct 19, composite flagged it
(`stocks_rsi` +1, RSI 27.4 on 2026-10-14), and management-normalisation
scenario C still clears the hurdle by 362 bp. A fuel break would hit a
company with record unit revenue and no hedges — the best-levered equity to
lower jet prices among the network carriers. The attack fails because the
lever it needs is exogenous, and because the evidence it cites cuts the
other way: Q3 shows that even full fuel recapture leaves operating income
falling, so a lower fuel price restores 2025's margin, not a better one —
and 2025's margin cleared nothing (operating income $1.69B, levered FCF
−$0.68B).

Load-bearing conditions of the bull case (tested, not endorsed). Fuel is a
§4 scenario input, not a condition — the prior run's condition 1 is
