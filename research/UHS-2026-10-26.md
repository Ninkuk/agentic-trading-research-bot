**Disputed expectation:** the market prices roughly 9–10%/yr on reported
FCF with the 2028–2032 statutory cuts absorbed by core growth; owner FCF
after stock compensation and the August refinancing step-up implies ~8.5%
on the enacted-law path. Tomorrow's print (Q3 EPS vs $5.19 consensus, the
Florida FY26 decision, the self-pay run-rate) and the February 2027 guide
are what revise it.

UHS is the same business the July run described: 27 acute hospitals and
~346 behavioral facilities earning a 11.4% operating margin, 7.7× trailing
earnings, a 7.7% FCF yield, shares shrinking ~6.7% a year, and more than
half of EBITDA flowing through state Medicaid supplemental programs that

$161.91 the deep-stress path implied 6.6% and the base ~11%; at $188.36
the base path on honest owner FCF — reported FCF less $95M of stock

and the Talkspace borrowing — implies 8.9% on flat 3% growth and 8.5% once
the enacted 2028–2032 cuts are netted against core growth, against a 9.28%
cost of equity. The business did not get worse; it got 16% more expensive
while its single largest earnings driver still has no approved FY2026
Florida preprint and the Q3 print is tomorrow.

**Closest attack:** the hurdle condition itself. The bull case needs the
market to be paying for *reported* FCF ($845M, +141bp on 4% growth) or for
normalized capex (+313bp), and both are generous — the $955M of trailing
capex is building hospitals that are still EBITDA drags, and the SBC and
interest step-up are real cash the owner does not keep. Strip them and the
price sits 35–78bp below the hurdle before any policy stress (§4).
