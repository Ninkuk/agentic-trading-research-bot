## 2. Business

**Created:** a survival benefit where almost none existed. Metastatic
uveal melanoma barely responds to checkpoint inhibitors. KIMMTRAK is a
bispecific (an ImmTAC: a soluble T-cell receptor that binds gp100 peptide
presented on HLA-A\*02:01, fused to an anti-CD3 that recruits the
patient's T cells). It roughly doubled 5-year survival in its Phase 3
follow-up (16% vs 8%, Q2 2026 call). Oncologists treat ~1,000 patients
a year with it, and real-world duration (~14 months) exceeds the trial's
10–11 months because physicians treat beyond radiographic progression
(Morgan Stanley conference, 2026-09-14).

**Captured:** one mechanism: list-price biologic sales, net of rebates and
government pricing agreements, by region. Q2 2026: US $74.9M, Europe
$34.1M, international $6.9M (8-K 2026-08-06). Europe carries
country-level price negotiations, and France's retroactive settlement
drives the ~$120M H2 2026 rebate payment. There is no royalty or
partnership income of note. Interest on $880M of cash is material
against a near-zero operating line.

**Protected:** three layers, in descending strength. (a) Regulatory:
US BLA approval January 2022 gives 12 years of biologic exclusivity, to
~January 2034. Patent expiry beyond that is NOT OBTAINED. (b) Clinical
incumbency: it is the only agent with randomized OS proof in this
setting, so a challenger in HLA-A2+ patients needs its own OS trial
against KIMMTRAK. (c) The ImmTAC platform know-how (soluble high-affinity
TCR engineering) is genuinely hard to copy. I cannot evaluate whether it
transfers to PRAME, HIV, or autoimmunity, so it is UNKNOWN and not
counted as a moat. HLA restriction caps the moat's reach: ~half of
patients (HLA-A2-negative) are open ground, and IDEAYA/Servier's
darovasertib+crizotinib (OptimUM-02, PFS 6.9 vs 3.1 months, NDA planned
H2 2026) is taking it.

**Control:** UK plc listed via ADS. Institutions hold ~92%, insiders
~0.1% (stocks.db). There is no controlling holder or dual-class structure
visible in this run (proxy NOT OBTAINED). The UK Takeover Code applies,
so an unsolicited bid is possible; nothing forecloses activism.
