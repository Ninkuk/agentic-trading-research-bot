## 2. Business

**Created:** eye-care physicians get branded products for surgery
(IHEEZO chloroprocaine gel, TRIESENCE triamcinolone), for chronic ocular
surface disease (VEVYE cyclosporine 0.1%, TYRVAYA varenicline nasal spray)
and, through a Samsung Bioepis commission arrangement, for retina (BYOOVIZ,
OPUVIZ biosimilars). ImprimisRx compounding supplies lower-cost formulations.
Physicians and patients get breadth from one ophthalmology-only sales force.
That is the value proposition: one rep visit covers several products (Q2
call: "Breadth matters").

**Captured:** five distinct mechanisms. (a) Branded Rx net revenue from
wholesalers and the PhilRx specialty pharmacy (VEVYE, TYRVAYA). This is a
gross-to-net business where list price minus rebates, co-pay cards and PBM
fees is the captured value, and it moved by $8M in one quarter. (b)
Buy-and-bill/facility products (IHEEZO, TRIESENCE), sold to ASCs and
offices. IHEEZO lost cataract-surgery pass-through reimbursement on
2026-04-01. (c) A sales commission on BYOOVIZ/OPUVIZ, which management calls
high-margin and which is not product revenue at full price. (d) Compounding
cash-pay and pharmacy revenue ($14.6M Q2), shrinking after the California
Board settlement. (e) Royalties, notably from Viatris on TYRVAYA in Japan.
Royalty expense paid out was $5.9M in H1 2026.

**Protected:** patents (VEVYE 2030–2039, IHEEZO 2038–2039, TRIESENCE
2029; FY2025 10-K) and the switching friction of an installed account base
(IHEEZO TTM reorder rate ~85.5%, 224 ordering accounts; TRIESENCE 805
accounts). The protection is real but narrow. Each product is a licensed or
acquired molecule in a market crowded with alternatives, and in dry eye
VEVYE holds 14.6% branded share (IQVIA, per the Q2 call) against
Restasis/Xiidra/Miebo/generic cyclosporine. The moat is in the commercial
channel, not the chemistry. A competitor with a better dry-eye drug and a
PBM deal takes share without any barrier Harrow controls.

**Control:** one class of common stock (FY2025 10-K cover); insiders hold
16.43% and institutions 52.18% (stockanalysis). No controlling holder is
disclosed. CEO Mark Baum's exact stake was not obtained (proxy not read).
Nothing structural forecloses a takeover or activist path.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative** over the latest twelve months,
after a positive FY2023–FY2025 stretch. Revenue is flat vs FY2025 (+1.2%)
while operating income fell $33.3M.
