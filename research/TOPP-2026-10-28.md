Toppoint is a drayage broker. It moves export waste paper, scrap and logs
to the Newark and Philadelphia ports and pulls import containers back,
using trucks it does not own. The stock has fallen 97% from its $4.50 IPO
high to $0.116. At that price it trades at 0.24× book and below net current
assets. The bull case is that the market wrote off the assets because the
stock is tiny and illiquid. The evidence says the assets are impaired
instead. $6.0M of the $8.28M net IPO proceeds went as a loan to an unrated
counterparty four days after listing. That loan has accrued every dollar of
its interest without paying any in cash. Founders are selling. The board
has armed reverse-split authority up to 1-for-900, a 1B-share
authorization and an effective S-3 shelf. This is persistently loss-making
(Phase 0 kill criterion 1) and governance-impaired. Unattended run: the
skill's "ask whether to go on" for a loss-maker is resolved as stop at
Phase 0, with the asset-value bull case attacked rather than skipped,
because it is the only bull case that exists.

## 2. Business

**Created:** short-haul container trucking ("drayage") between Mid-Atlantic
recyclers and importers and the Newark and Philadelphia ports. It has
satellite lanes in Tampa, Jacksonville, Miami, Baltimore, Ensenada and
Houston. The customer gets capacity and dispatch on a commoditised lane.
Nothing in the filings shows surplus the customer could not get from the
many other regional drayage firms (10-Q 2026-08-12, business note).

**Captured:** per-load freight revenue. In H1-26 that was paper $4.29M,
import $3.05M, metal $1.17M, logs $0.20M and plastic $0.04M. The company
does not own trucks: "All the trucks in our fleet are owned by our truck
owner-operators" (10-K FY2025, Item 1A). It pays them out of the haul, so
the margin is a broker spread: 3.0% gross in FY2025 and 8.1% in Q2-26.
Interest on the Golden loan (7%, accrued) is the only other income line.

**Protected:** no moat. The company runs an asset-light brokerage on a
commodity lane. The top ten customers are ~59% of revenue (FY2025 10-K). It
depends on owner-operators "more than some of our competitors" (10-K Item
1A). An internally built "AI-based" dispatch tool ($806,614 gross
capitalised) is not a barrier anyone has to cross. The silence is the
answer.

**Control:** single common class, NYSE American; Delaware since the
2026-09-26 reincorporation. CEO and Chairman Hok C Chan "still effectively
controls the Company" (10-Q note) after selling 3.6M shares in Dec 2025–Jan
2026 to three investors. Each of those investors can designate one of six
board seats. Insiders hold 34.0% (stocks.db). At the 2026-09-08 meeting,
99.6% of votes cast approved board-discretion reverse splits from 1-for-2
to 1-for-900 through 2029, and authorized common rose from 300M to 1B. The
structure forecloses activist pressure; minority holders cannot block
dilution.
