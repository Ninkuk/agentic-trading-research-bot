## 2. Business

**Created:** Structured, curated investment data and independent research that
lets investors compare instruments across public funds/equities (Direct,
Data, ratings), private markets (PitchBook), credit (DBRS), and now indexes
(CRSP, acquired Feb 2026). Customers get workflow trust: the data is clean,
comparable, and compliance-safe.

**Captured:** Not "subscriptions" — five distinct mechanisms: (1) per-seat and
enterprise licenses (Direct ~104% revenue renewal, PitchBook ~103%); (2)
compulsory licensing of ratings for fund marketing (Essentials — funds that cite
a Morningstar rating license it); (3) issuance-linked ratings fees (DBRS,
+23.3% organic in Q2 on private-credit and structured issuance); (4) AUM-linked
basis points (Retirement $311B AUMA, Wealth, Indexes); (5) index licensing
(CRSP: $3.3T linked, Vanguard flagship ETFs now co-branded "Vanguard Morningstar
Total Stock Market Index ETF").

**Protected (mechanisms, not labels):** Ratings move fund flows (documented in
their own research; June 8-K Q&A), so asset managers must engage regardless of
sentiment; DBRS holds NRSRO regulatory licensure; PitchBook's private-market
dataset is analyst-built from non-public submissions — not scrapeable; index
switching costs for a $3.3T tracking complex are prohibitive (Vanguard's 2026
co-branding is a costly commitment signal). Weakest wall: Direct/Advisor
Workstation software, where FactSet/YCharts/in-house tools compete and AWS
renewal disclosure went dark in 2025.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive** ("strongly positive since
2022"). Revenue 2021→TTM: $1,699M→$2,567M (+51%); operating income
$257M→$604M (+135%); op margin 15.1%→9.0% (2022 trough)→23.5%. Q2 opex
+5.9% vs revenue +9.6%. (stockanalysis income-statement route; Q2 8-K.)
