# PCTY — Paylocity Holding Corp — 2026-08-03

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Pass at $137.90; the name goes on the re-entry watchlist.** Paylocity is a
genuinely good business — mid-market payroll/HCM SaaS with >92% revenue
retention, real GAAP operating leverage, net cash, and a shrinking share count
— but today's price implies ~10%/yr on honest FCF only if growth holds ~8%,
and the bear floor (float compression + slowdown) is ~8%. That margin is thin
with the Q4 FY26 print **tomorrow** (Aug 4 after close, confirmed), FY27
initial guidance landing with it, and the stock up 12% in the final week into
the event. Kill-thesis verdict: **UNPROVEN — 5 load-bearing conditions,
0 refuted, 1 unknown** (the Fed-rate path behind float income; growth years
3-5 also rest on undisclosed cross-sell attach rates). The attack that came
closest: conditions 1 and 4 fail *together* in a recession — per-employee
pricing makes revenue track client headcounts down at the same time float
yield leaves.

Re-entry logic: at ~$120 the same base assumptions imply ~11%/yr. A FY27
initial guide of ≥9% recurring growth with flat-or-up ex-float margin would
supply the missing year of evidence on condition 1; a sub-8% guide or margin
give-back flips the thesis toward FLAWED at any price.

## 2. Business

**Created:** payroll that cannot be wrong, for companies too small to build a
compliance function (~150-employee average client, >40,000 clients). Paylocity
runs pay, tax remittance across 5,700+ jurisdictions, benefits, time, and now
finance/IT workflows on one employee-record platform, moving >$100B per
quarter for clients (Q3 FY26 call). The customer's alternative is ADP/Paychex
(older platforms), point tools, or an in-house team they cannot staff.

**Captured:** per-employee-per-month recurring subscription (recurring + other
= 93% of revenue), plus ~$117M FY26E interest earned on $3.25B of client
payroll funds held in trust (~7% of revenue, nearly pure margin) — the float.
ARPU expands by attaching modules: the chargeable suite is >3× its IPO size
(call); Airbase brought office-of-the-CFO spend management; Elevate (2026)
adds managed services; Grayscale/Aidora add AI SKUs management intends to
price as premium (call).

**Protected:** switching a payroll provider risks mispaying employees and
misfiling taxes — error intolerance plus data gravity of the employee record
makes churn painful (revenue retention >92% each of FY23/24/25 — FY25 10-K).
Distribution moat: benefit brokers refer >25% of new business, and Paylocity
does not sell insurance against them (call). These are mechanisms, not labels
— but note the moat is strongest at 150 employees and weaker above, where
Rippling/Workday press.

## 5. Falsifiers

- Recurring revenue growth <7% for two consecutive quarters, ex-acquisitions.
- Annual revenue retention disclosed below 92%.
- Ex-float adjusted-EBITDA margin contraction two consecutive quarters.
- Share count flat or rising (buyback stops outrunning SBC).
- A large, dilutive acquisition (order of magnitude beyond Airbase's $277M).
- Near-term signpost: FY27 initial guide (tomorrow) — recurring <8% or a
  margin give-back to fund AI/services is the early warning.
