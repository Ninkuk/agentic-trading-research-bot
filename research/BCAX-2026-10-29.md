## 0. The reopen question, answered first

The class is validated, and the bar for a second entrant went up sharply.
Genmab's LiGeR-HN1 (petosemtamab + pembrolizumab vs pembrolizumab, 1L PD-L1+
R/M HNSCC, 1:1, open-label) met both primary endpoints at the **first interim**
analysis: OS HR **0.50** (95% CI 0.37–0.67, p<0.0001), ORR odds ratio 3.17
(2.06–4.89). Benefit was seen **regardless of HPV status**, and there were no
new safety signals (Genmab release 2026-10-28, via CancerNetwork; the
genmab.com page returned 403). Median OS, ORR %, and n are not yet disclosed.
BCAX closed at $9.76 on 15.4M shares, about 20× normal volume ($17.48 → $9.76,
−44%), and $9.73 the next day. Bicara filed no 8-K or press release in
response (EDGAR filing list through 2026-10-29).

The answer is a split verdict. The randomized evidence that EGFR blockade on
a pembrolizumab backbone works in 1L HNSCC is now strong, and FORTIFI-HN01 has
the same control arm (placebo + pembro). That probably *raises* the odds
Bicara's own trial is positive. But an OS HR of 0.50 in an all-comer HPV
population is about as strong as phase 3 results in this disease get. It
takes away the "HPV-negative differentiation" story the bull case leaned on,
and it sets the commercial bar any second entrant has to clear. The market
cut what the program is worth beyond cash from about $0.78B to about $0.2B.
