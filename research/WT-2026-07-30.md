# WT — WisdomTree, Inc. — 2026-07-30

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**Pass at today's price and timing; revisit after tomorrow's Q2 print.** WisdomTree is a
genuinely improving, now-diversified ETF issuer whose price implies ~9–10%/yr on
consensus-ish growth — a fair return, not a mispricing, and it is on offer the day before
an earnings print the options market prices (noisily) at a ±16% expected move.
**kill-thesis verdict: UNPROVEN** (5 load-bearing conditions, 0 refuted, 2 unknown) — the
two conditions that carry the return (market/gold path, flow persistence) rest on future
market data that no disclosure can supply today. The attack that came closest: operating
leverage here is symmetric market-beta — 2022 ran the same machine in reverse (margin
29.3% → 19.9% in one year), and ~26% of AUM is commodity-priced.

## 2. Business

**Created:** ~$163B AUM across 416 products [Q1'26 release]. Differentiated exposures —
USFR floating-rate Treasuries, currency-hedged Japan, European physical gold/defense/
rare-earth UCITS, leveraged/inverse ETPs, model portfolios, farmland private funds
(Ceres), defined-outcome strategies (Atlantic House), tokenized money-market fund
(WTGXX). Customers get exposures the giants don't offer, in liquid wrappers.

**Captured:** advisory fee on AUM — avg 0.36%, firm revenue yield 0.42% (Q1'26). Three
mechanisms, per the CFO's own modeling guidance on the Q1 call: (a) ETP advisory fees
(AUM × capture, mix-driven); (b) Ceres management fees ~1% of AUM plus 15%-participation
performance fees (seasonal); (c) "other revenues" — 40% transaction-based (European ETP
trading activity), 60% AUM-based, plus Atlantic House structuring fees (~$16M/yr
pre-acquisition).

**Protected:** the mechanism is installed-asset stickiness (selling an appreciated ETF
triggers capital gains for taxable holders), niche first-mover positions where scale
players don't bother competing on price, and self-built indexes (no licensing cost, no
index-provider squeeze). This is a real but **narrow** moat: nothing stops BlackRock
launching a competing defense UCITS tomorrow — only the installed base is defended.
Management's answer is product velocity (20–30 launches/yr) and now bought capabilities
(95bp Atlantic House, 200bp+ Ceres). Fee *capture* rising while the industry compresses
is the load-bearing evidence — but see Threads: most of that rise was bought, not organic.
