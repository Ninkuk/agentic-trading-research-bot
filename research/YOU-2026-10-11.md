## 2. Business

Unchanged from `research/YOU-2026-09-08.md` §2 except for the deltas below.

**Created:** queue position and certainty on the day of travel. A biometric
identity lane, increasingly eGates, puts a member at the front of security
at ~60 US airports. At $219/yr and last-published ~7 uses/yr, that is about
$31 per trip. CLEAR1 sells enterprises verified-human onboarding, and the
VA.gov pilot (2026-10-07) adds a federal-agency reference customer at six VA
facilities.

**Captured:** four mechanisms:

- (a) Prepaid annual consumer subscriptions ($219 standard, $125 family,
  airline and Amex tiers). These are collected upfront, which is why
  deferred revenue is $573.0M.
- (b) Partner-funded distribution: Amex and card statement credits plus
  airline tiers, accrued as a $315M liability that pays out each Q3.
- (c) TSA PreCheck enrollment fees.
- (d) CLEAR1 B2B contracts.

Against all four, airports take a revenue share of ~14% of revenue.

**Protected:** three mechanisms:

- (i) Finite lane real estate under multi-year airport contracts, won one
  airport at a time.
- (ii) A certification stack: TSA Registered Traveler, SAFETY Act, FISMA
  High.
- (iii) A ~44M-identity enrolled network.

The named hole is unchanged: the government needs neither an airport
contract nor a certification.

share; Class B and Class D carry **twenty** (proxy/S-1 language, located this
