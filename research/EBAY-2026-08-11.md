# EBAY — eBay Inc. (2026-08-11)

## 1. Verdict and thesis

**PASS at $106.03 / $47.2B.** The business has genuinely re-accelerated (Q2'26
GMV +14% organic FXN, ROIC 28.5%, asset-light, 5.5% shareholder yield), but the
price already pays for more than the durable part of it: on an SBC-cut levered
FCF base the implied return is 6.9%/yr vs a ~9.9–10.5% cost-of-equity hurdle
(−366bp), and even 13%/yr FCF growth for five years reaches only 8.5%. The
price also still carries residual takeover optionality — GameStop holds 9.8%
and its rejected $56B (~$126/sh) offer began visibly deflating on 2026-08-10
when Bloomberg reported Cohen is weighing pulling it. Good business at a price
that needs either the deal or high-teens growth. **kill-thesis: SOUND**
(5 conditions, 0 refuted, 2 unknown); the closest attack is the perpetuity
framing — a secular-recommerce bull who rejects the 2.5% terminal cap gets
SBC-cut yield 3.8% + 4–5% perpetual growth ≈ 7.8–8.8%, still under the hurdle
but within ~100bp of the clamped variant.

Entry path: candidates screen (quality-first: fcf_yield 5.1%, F-score 6, RSI
43, −9.7% off the 52w high). Composite covers EBAY with only the informational

## 2. Business

**Creates:** liquidity and trust for long-tail, non-commodity goods — used,
refurbished, collectible, enthusiast — where Amazon's catalog model is weakest.
Buyers get selection depth (2.6B listings) and authentication (Authenticity
Guarantee, Guaranteed Fit); sellers get 136M active buyers with near-zero
inventory risk to eBay.

**Captures:** ~14% blended take rate on $22.4B/qtr GMV, in layers: final-value
fees; first-party advertising ($596M/qtr, 2.7% of GMV, growing 24%); shipping
programs (double-digit growth, partly contra-revenue); payments/financial
services (Pay by Bank, eBay Balance). Plus adjacent marketplaces: TCGplayer,
Goldin, Caramel (vehicles), and now Depop ($1.4B cash, closed 2026-07-30).
(Q2'26 call, 2026-08-05.)

**Protects:** in the enthusiast niches that now drive growth (focused
categories = 40%+ of GMV, +26%), the mechanism is real: two-sided liquidity
depth in each vertical plus physical authentication infrastructure and 30
years of pricing data (80M card scans, Card Ladder integration) that a new
entrant must rebuild per category. In generalist e-commerce there is no
mechanism — eBay is the canonical name that passes the network-effect checkbox
and fails the "what stops a competitor tomorrow" question, and Vinted forcing
C2C fee elimination in the UK/Germany proved the fee umbrella is contestable
(Q2 call Q&A; management: "no current plans" to change US fees).

## 5. Falsifiers

For the PASS (each flips toward buy — all shifts, not breaks):
- **Price ≤ ~$88** — the base case then at least beats the median-firm return;
  materially below that it becomes interesting. The likely route is a formal
  GameStop withdrawal unwinding the residual premium.
- **Two consecutive post-lap quarters (Q4'26, Q1'27) of ≥10% organic FXN GMV
  growth with non-GAAP OI margin held ≥25%** — durability proven, revalue with
  a higher growth path.
- **Take-rate inflection** — ads pushing past the 3% "midterm goal" with the
  blended take rate rising rather than mix-eroding.

Against ever owning it (breaks, if later bought): US C2C fee concession under
competitive pressure (the Vinted-in-Europe pattern repeating at home);
collectibles GMV contracting outright while overall growth reverts to the
2021–24 ~0% base.

## 6. UNKNOWNs

1. **The deal premium embedded in $106.** No disclosure decomposes it; the
   pre-stake February price (~$85–95) and GameStop's $101.30 average cost
   bracket it loosely. Absence does not kill the thesis — the reverse DCF
   stands on fundamentals regardless of why the price is here.
2. **Post-lap durability of collectibles demand.** Undecidable until the
   Q4'26/Q1'27 prints; management's two-year-stack framing is argument, not
   evidence. This is the load-bearing uncertainty of the growth path — it is
   why the base case fades to 4%.
3. **Depop standalone economics** (GMV, losses) — not separately disclosed
   beyond guided dilution points; matters to 2027–28 margin, not to the verdict.
