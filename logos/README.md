# Source seals

Agency seals shown on the home page's source cards. Each is a work of the
United States federal government and in the public domain (17 U.S.C. § 105),
rasterised at 160px from the SVG on Wikimedia Commons:

- `fed.png` — Seal of the United States Federal Reserve System
- `treasury.png` — Seal of the United States Department of the Treasury
- `sec.png` — Seal of the United States Securities and Exchange Commission
- `cftc.png` — Seal of the Commodity Futures Trading Commission
- `eia.png` — Seal of the United States Department of Energy (the EIA's parent)
- `usda.png` — Seal of the United States Department of Agriculture

FINRA, Cboe and the New York Fed are shown as wordmarks: the first two are
private trademarks and no public-domain seal exists for the third.
